# Claim Ledger View

Source: controlling specification, Section 3.1.

The binding ledger keeps continuation stiffness, temporal relaxation,
read-back gain, transition multipliers, and spatial Hessians distinct. It also
keeps retention, read effect, write effect, and closed-loop evidence distinct.

GRV0 extracts every `T-*` row without changing its statement, provenance,
source anchor, assumptions, or limits. No claim receives a positive GRC9V3
status at this gate.
