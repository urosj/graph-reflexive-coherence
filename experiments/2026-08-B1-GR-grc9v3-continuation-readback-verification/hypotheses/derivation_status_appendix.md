# Derivation Status View

Source: controlling specification, Section 3.3 and Appendix A.

GRV0 records all `PN-*` proof notes and preserves the permitted status vocabulary:
`inherited_explicit`, `derived_with_sketch`, `conditional_lemma`,
`candidate_witness_class`, `open_problem`, and `realization_specific`.
Serialization does not promote a sketch or candidate into a theorem.
