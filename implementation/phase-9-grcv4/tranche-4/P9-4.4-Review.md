## Implemented behavior and ownership

[CandidateCOSPass](../../../src/pygrc/models/grc_v4_realizations.py) performs one
reference predictor, one structural geometry update, and one fresh fixed-geometry
corrector. The source is `zeta_C * iota_C(StarAssembly(causal_flat))`: the C adapter
uses the declared common structural coordinates, chi is already in `causal_flat`,
and zeta is applied once outside the adapter. Hodge, physical flux and mobility
remain separate types and owners.

The corrector rebuilds selector, selected content, retained Hodge, potential,
baseline, identification, resolvent and flat/sharp maps from its own inputs.
A second geometry evaluation computes the explicit residual operand only;
that geometry never feeds another current solve. A bounded nonzero residual is
accepted. Exceeding the declared tolerance fails without another OS iteration.

[ProvisionalCandidateCOSStep](../../../src/pygrc/models/grc_v4_step.py) admits current
and reset charges and the reset's reference current domain, selects the actual
corrector, calls the existing resource boundary once, and rebuilds the complete
Candidate C current/derived chain from provisional final C at the consumed
geometry. That final current is a readmission surface, not a second continuity
operand. The next inputs use the profile reference geometry, new C and advanced
clock/index; corrected geometry, residual work and final caches are never
retained history. Reset coordinates and the supplied receipt-ID sequence stay
unchanged by this provisional calculation.

This leaf completes the numerical pass and resource/final-reconstruction pipeline.
The existing ownership review assigns live model mutation, source/ledger
authentication and receipt construction to later operation/lifecycle work.
These immutable components return no successful result on a failed substage;
they do not implement or claim rollback of a live facade. P9-4.5 owns concrete
positive/atomic-negative operation vectors and their disposition composition;
P9-4.6/P9-4.7 own the corresponding lifecycle and receipt product. Generic runtime
and specialization support remain empty.

## Numerical policy and limits

For the OS geometry residual, `edge_l2_v1` is applied in the source-declared
reference-whitened edge coordinates:

```text
R = H1^(1) - H1_hat^(1)
|| Href^(-1/2) R Href^(-1/2) ||_2 <= tolerance
iff tolerance*Href + R >= 0 and tolerance*Href - R >= 0.
```

Both PSD conditions use exact dyadic subtraction/products and symmetric inertia.
This is the reference-relative Hodge norm, not the unscaled physical-current
solver norm or a norm of K4. Equality passes; no norm-squaring overflow, underflow
or eigensolver tolerance decides the boundary. The result retains both rounded
residual entries and exact rational entries. An unrepresentable output fails.
The three operator roles and the two residual operand geometries remain explicit.

The geometry update must stay on the admitted fixed-rank strict-gap selector
stratum. Exact symmetric order certifies monotone affine stiffness paths when
endpoint ranks agree. For mixed directions, dyadic subsegments use exact inertia
and an inverse-norm Neumann bound. An interior cutoff or changed rank rejects;
a 256-bisection limit rejects an unresolved certificate. This is a bounded
numerical admission policy, not a second OS iteration, a global current-domain
certificate, or a quadratic/time-order theorem. Every executed stage separately
passes the inherited selector, SPD and physical-current conditioning checks.

Duration never scales the same-beat structural map. Zero duration admits declared
reference current/reset domains and charge, returns identity, and performs no
OS pass, continuity write or clock advancement. Every strictly positive duration,
including the minimum subnormal, takes the complete positive path. Represented
resource or clock increments may round to zero; the positive step still advances
its index. Clock overflow, nonfinite/negative resource or charge failure rejects.

The accepted binary64 star, affine geometry and fixed-stage current arithmetic
are unchanged. In particular, star entries round at the existing StarAssembly
boundary before the external zeta multiplication; this leaf does not claim
arbitrary-precision nonlinear products or arbitrary-range certification. The
P9-4.3 oracle's saturation and spectral limitations remain unchanged.
