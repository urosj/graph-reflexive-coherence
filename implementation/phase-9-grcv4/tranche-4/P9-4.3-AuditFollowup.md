## Correction and numerical boundary

The [test oracle](../../../tests/models/test_grc_v4_candidate_c.py) keeps its
NumPy spectral proposal and carries the subsequent selected-content, nonlinear
and complete derivative product chain in an explicit 90-digit Decimal context.
Only returned coordinates are rounded to binary64. Merely repairing `sech²`
and then rounding `delta_D` or `delta_Hm` before mobility would still lose
representable final derivatives; the widened arithmetic continues through J0.
The context explicitly fixes precision, rounding, exponent bounds and traps,
independently of the caller's Decimal settings.

Subnormal and nonzero Decimal coordinates that round to zero carry an output
index, Decimal sign, logarithmic magnitude, binary64 encoding and disposition.
The ordinary finite-difference comparator rejects these cases instead of allowing
its absolute tolerance to certify a zero quotient against a tiny derivative.
The metadata describes the working-precision result; it is not an exact
nonzeroness certificate for arbitrary cancellation-sensitive derivatives.

This is a bounded numerical oracle. Decimal exponent bounds are -999999 to
999999, with underflow/overflow and invalid operations trapped. An exceeded
Decimal range or a nonfinite final binary64 coordinate raises an explicit
`ValueError`; it does not return an unexplained zero or NaN. A final coordinate
that rounds to zero within that context is labeled separately. These are oracle
limits, not restrictions on production admission. The NumPy spectral proposal
and 90-digit arithmetic do not certify arbitrary near-gap or catastrophic-
cancellation cases; successful return alone is not such a certificate.

At `C_ref=5e-324`, the audit's `T=delta_T=1` case explicitly rejects the
general oracle's exponent range.
A separate literal scalar witness proves the mathematical derivative is positive
and bounds its logarithm without evaluating the unrepresentable exponential.
It uses `C=(0.75,1.25)`, `Hpre=W=2`, `eta=0.5`, `kappa_M=0.75`,
`kappa_Phi=1` and uniform `delta_C=(1,1)`. For `cf=C_ref`, its exact equation gives

```text
1.5 exp(-0.75-2/cf)/cf <= dJ0 <= 6 exp(0.75-2/cf)/cf,   dJ0 > 0.
```

Directed 110-digit arithmetic bounds the log; its upper bound is below the
logarithm of half the minimum binary64 subnormal. Thus the final rounded value
is zero while the mathematical value remains positive. The current finite
quotient is also zero and supplies no derivative-resolution evidence here.
A separate `T=0` witness exercises final binary64 overflow rather than exponential
underflow; production still admits its current while the oracle rejects.
