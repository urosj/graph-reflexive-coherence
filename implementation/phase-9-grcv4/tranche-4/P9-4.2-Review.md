Indeterminate parent edges are not promoted to accepted scientific support.

At the supplied stage geometry, with `Hpre` the supplied structural one-form
Hodge and `H0` the vertex Hodge, the equations are:

- `T = P_Lambda(Hpre) C`, using the H0-weighted strict-gap spectral projector;
  `rho = tanh(T/C_ref)`, endpoint-averaged on each edge;
  `D_ee = exp(kappa_M*rho_e/2)` and `Hm = D Hpre D`.
- `Phi0 = kappa_Phi B Hm B^T C - V'(C,U)`, and
  `J0 = -eta Diag(W_C_tr) B^T Phi0`.
- `G = Hpre^-1`, `I4 = Hm Hpre^-1`, `Q = I4 G`,
  `Delta = B^T H0^-1 B Hm`, `Rhat = (I + tau Delta)^-1`.
- Physical current is lowered by G, identified by I4, acted on by ungated Rhat,
  returned by I4 inverse, gated by chi exactly once, and raised by G inverse.
  `Rflux = Q^-1 Rhat Q`; `Lphysical = I - zeta chi Rflux`;
  `Lphysical J = J0`.

The declared potential evaluator is
`quadratic_site_potential_zero_derivative_v1`: V' is zero, as in the frozen
finite algebra witness. The stage uses the existing unit vertex measure,
constant-zero context and nondimensional reference declarations, the component
zero-mean potential gauge and unnormalized stiffness. Unknown evaluators,
domains, gauges, normalizations, solver kinds or residual norms reject.
There is no inferred implementation of arbitrary declaration strings.

The general frozen equation and schema allow finite chi/zeta; this leaf checks
actual regularity and the serialized conditioning policy, including signed or
nonbinary algebra controls. The paper's binary-gate/nonnegative-gain sufficient
inverse-margin result is a narrower profile result. No such theorem or full
runtime profile admission is claimed for the wider local algebra. In particular,
we do not invent an unserialized zeta-bar or infer that an invertible block
satisfies all future profile/beat admission obligations. Tau-zero is the separately
authorized identity-response control, not an alternative runtime solver.

The immutable reference transport remains the mobility authority. Retained Hodge
and supplied nonreference geometry may alter Phi0 and Read-Back, but never supply
`W_C_tr` or mobility. Trial J belongs to the stage identity and is a Read-Back
operand; at fixed h it cannot condition the selector or J0. Reconstruction accepts
only complete stage inputs and the versioned numerical-policy identifier, then
recomputes all results. Derived current, selector or response arrays cannot be
loaded as authority.

## Numerical policy and its limits

`grcv4-c-stage-exact-linear-binary64-v1` uses exact rational arithmetic on the
represented binary64 linear coefficients, with explicitly rounded finite outputs.
Linear solves use exact elimination and then check the returned binary64 solution
against the declared absolute/relative edge-L2 tolerance. Residual comparisons
square exact rational norms, so underflow cannot turn a nonzero residual into zero.
A zero-tolerance policy can reject a regular equation whose exact solution is not
representable. Intermediate required solves are checked as well; this is a
conservative executable domain, not a promise to solve every real regular input.

Selector rank and exact cutoff ties are decided by rational generalized shifted
inertia, including 2x2 congruence pivots. Empty/full spectral sectors and the
component-kernel projector use exact algebra. Interior nonkernel sectors use
NumPy symmetric eigendecomposition as a proposal, with exact weighted Gram and
generalized-eigenvector residual checks. If delta bounds the Gram defect and R
is the whitened eigen residual, polar orthonormalization gives the conservative
spectral error bound `(||R|| + 4*max|lambda|*delta)/(1-delta)`. The implementation
bounds the residual by its entrywise L1 norm and the vertex measure's certified
lower square root. The proposed spectral distance must exceed this bound, and
the selected count must match exact inertia. Unresolved gaps fail closed.
The weighted projector is constructed onto the proposed selected span through
its exact Gram inverse, making it independent of signs, eigenpair order and
basis rotations within a selected cluster. Its coefficients remain numerical
approximations to the true spectral projector; the certificate proves rank/gap
separation, not exact projector coefficients or platform-independent bits.
No previous-stage rank or projector is reused, and separate local strata do not
authorize a beat to cross a rank boundary.

Current singularity is checked before rounding the resolvent: by exact similarity,
Lphysical is singular iff `(1-zeta*chi) I + tau Delta` is singular. This prevents
a rounded reciprocal from disguising an exact singular scalar or harmonic mode.
The actual constructed physical block also undergoes exact singularity and
Euclidean 2-norm conditioning checks. Normalized Gram PSD inequalities certify
SVD-proposed bounds; diagonal and 2x2 cases have exact condition-limit comparisons,
including equality. Higher-dimensional unresolved boundary enclosures reject.
Retained-space margins are never copied into physical coordinates.

The implemented Read-Back chain is evaluated on the solved J, and its actual
closure residual `J-J0-zeta*read_flux` must also satisfy the declared tolerance.
Tau-zero uses the proved identity response. No pseudoinverse, damping,
regularization, positivity floor, clipping, charge repair or alternate root is
introduced. Exp underflow/overflow, unrepresentable required inverses, singularity
and conditioning failures reject without mutating any input. The earlier
positive-matrix/nonpositive-computed-pairing witness reaches this owner and rejects
at its exact structural conditioning check across scale and sign variants; no
nonpositive rounded self-pairing is consumed as a valid norm.

These are small dense reference computations. Rational elimination and numerical
eigendecomposition do not establish large-graph performance or cross-platform
bitwise reproducibility. Nonunit positive diagonal H0 is exercised only by the
private finite-algebra witness; the shipped complete stage profile still admits
unit measure. No general dense-H0 selector is advertised.
