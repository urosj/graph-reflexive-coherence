graph-intrinsic induced-frame backend. Its positive ridge is reference data,

It remains a declared initializer operand, **not an authenticated solver result**.

## Numerical and ownership pressure

Normal equations use exact rational arithmetic on binary64 inputs, followed by
one round-to-nearest, ties-to-even binary64 rounding of each gradient component.
Gradient overflow rejects. Representable subnormal results are retained;
sufficiently small positive or negative components may round to canonical
positive zero. This does not permit nonpositive retained conductance or mobility.

The entire conductance exponent is accumulated exactly before one binary64
rounding, so finite signed-channel cancellations and disabled coefficients do
not manufacture nonfinite intermediates. The sequence is
`E_exact -> E64 -> math.exp(E64) -> max(W_floor, result)`; it is not a promise
of one correctly rounded `exp(E_exact)`. The selected modest-input Decimal
comparisons' four-ULP/relative tolerances are **not global error bounds**.
The large-exponent regression independently evaluates Decimal `exp` at `E64`
for both exponent signs; it also distinguishes the exact-exponent value without
requiring a platform-specific ULP count. Very negative exponents return the
normative positive floor, including subnormal floors. Exponential overflow and
nonpositive or nonfinite computed mobility reject. No scientific coefficient
is clipped; signed coefficients do not imply universally decreasing conductance.

`W_floor` bounds newly evaluated `G_W`; it does not authorize clamping an existing
strictly positive retained `W_A` below that floor. Hodge/reference weights never
replace retained mobility. Equal initialized and previously supplied values do
not turn initialization into history preservation or native formation.

The [independent tests](../../../tests/models/test_grc_v4_candidate_a.py) supply
closed-form one- and two-dimensional gradients and a 100-digit Decimal scalar
oracle. They pressure all three conductance channels, 40 seeded one-dimensional
path-graph/parameter cases, signed coefficients, separately rebuilt current/reset
states,
reset-only failure, orientation and vertex/edge permutation, host-frame rigid
motion, coincident coordinates, isolated/disconnected vertices, exponent and
mobility range boundaries, exact cancellation, fresh-differential overflow,
immutable inputs/outputs, strict reconstruction and forbidden claim promotions.
Edgeless differential evaluation is valid locally; the frozen complete-profile
reference-Hodge schema rejects its empty edge map. No schema exception is made.

If reset-stage construction fails, the combined value constructor returns no
completed pair. This is not evidence of rollback of a mutable lifecycle ledger,
graph, reset baseline or charge target; that belongs to the lifecycle owner.
