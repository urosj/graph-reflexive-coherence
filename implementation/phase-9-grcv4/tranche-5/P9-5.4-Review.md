## Concrete counterexamples and pressure

Three new probes in [the verifier](../verification/verify_p954_claims.py) make
otherwise tempting inferences directly challengeable.

**Positive construction can leave only reset current singular.** On one edge,
set zero current/reset resource, `gamma=-2*log(2)`, `chi=1`, `zeta=3`, with
other conductance channels disabled. Supply initializer reference flux 0
for current and 1 for reset. With the other conductance channels disabled,
the current contribution to the exponent is `-gamma * J_ref**2 / 2`.
For `gamma=-2*log(2)`, reference fluxes 0 and 1 produce initialized weights
1 and 2 under the declared binary64 recipe, both positive.
Current admission succeeds. At zero C, the current's instantaneous reference
is 1; reset has `q=(2-1)/(2+1)=1/3` and `1-zeta*chi*q=0`.
The zero-duration numerical step rejects at `pre_read_reconstruction` with
the typed singular cause and unchanged input payload. This proves that even
successful current admission plus positive reset construction is insufficient.
The supplied flux operands are deliberately unauthenticated: this is a
partial-constructor counterexample, not a lawful completed migration or live
rollback evidence. `test_positive_initialization_can_leave_only_reset_current_singular`.

**A zero-current writer and an initializer can produce the same state.** In
the same one-edge numerical profile, use `chi=0`, `zeta=1`, zero C,
`W_old=4`, `tau=1` and `dt=log(2)`. The actual OS step consumes zero current,
refreshes target `W_drv=1`, and writes `W_next=2`. The ideal real-valued
half-interpolation gives `sqrt(4*1)`; at the actual binary64 `dt=log(2)`,
independent high-precision evaluation gives approximately
`2.00000000000000003215`, which rounds to binary64 2. Thus zero current and read-off did not disable writing. An
initializer supplied flux +1 or -1 produces exactly the same C/W values;
the two initializer identities differ, and both still disclaim formation,
history preservation and lifecycle commit. No value-only comparison can
recover those causal origins. This example is not labeled native release.
`test_zero_current_writer_and_initializer_can_return_identical_values`.

**The instantaneous relation can become neutral without changing W.** Compare
two explicitly supplied fixed-stage states with `W=2` and
`gamma=-2*log(2)`: `C=(1,1)` gives `W_hat=1`, `q=1/3`, while `C=(2,1)` gives
`W_hat=2`, `q=0`. Retained W is identical. This is an algebraic comparison of
states with different C/charge, not an executed transition or a release
experiment. It demonstrates why a moving reference alone cannot establish
native release. `test_neutral_contrast_from_reference_motion_is_not_a_retained_write`.

The audit's additional same-charge comparison uses `C=(1.5,1.5)` and
`C=(2,1)`, both at charge 3. The fresh `--audit` check reproduces `W_hat=1`
versus 2 and `q=1/3` versus 0 with the same retained W. It remains a comparison
of supplied states, not evidence of a reachable transition or native release.
The original different-charge probe and its execution record stay unchanged.
