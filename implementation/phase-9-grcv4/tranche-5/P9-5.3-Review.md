The pass uses the existing exact reference-relative split admission:
`||Href^(-1/2) R Href^(-1/2)||₂ <= tolerance`, checked by the inertia of
`tolerance*Href ± R`. A bounded nonzero residual is lawful. It is independent
of duration and is not a time-truncation-error estimate. A has no C selector
or selector-path certification requirement.

Positive duration increments the safe integer index once and adds the clock
once using exact dyadic addition followed by binary64 rounding. A positive
subnormal duration remains a positive beat even if represented time or state
does not change. Zero duration readmits current/reset without an OS pass,
continuity evaluation or writer. Negative duration cannot enter typed stage
inputs; the existing external-request rejection remains with the request and
lifecycle owners. Unsupported writer policies reject even at zero duration.

otherwise it is explicitly `None`. `exact_values` always contains the complete
lossless defect. The sole publication consumer, the existing C lifecycle,
emits JSON `null` for the unavailable display and still emits
`exact_split_residual`. Normal finite displays keep their previous shape.
Nothing is clipped, repaired or fed back into another corrector.

`Href=diag(s/8,3*(s/8))`, `C=(1,2)`, `W=(2,3)`, `kappa_c=s/64`,
`kappa_Ah=1/256`, `kappa_H=s`, unit eta/chi/zeta and zero alpha/beta/gamma.

positive definite even though an off-diagonal raw residual exceeds binary64.

continuity evaluation, and finite consumed/reference post-writer currents.

**F2 — preserve the A stage around shared numerical errors.** The A pass
