## Executed pressure

[Four focused methods](../verification/test_p975_failure_sequences.py) cover:

1. A_PC ordinary evolution → reset → further evolution → preserving A_CI+PC
   migration → reset → further evolution → A-to-C_PC reconstruction with
   resource swap and increment → reset. Every reset starts with genuinely
   different live/reset authority. Literal expectations check identity migration,
   independently computed affine resources, absent target W and zero target Z.
   Successful ordinary/migration/event ledger growth equals their emitted delta;
   reset preserves the existing ledger prefix, appends a reset group, retains
   clock/Q/reference/archives, and restores exactly through the public loader.
2. Nine invalid declarations plus one stale request after a positive A_PC step:
   source/target resource ordering, exact charge-form violation, negative output,
   forged candidate/carrier source-history digests, unadmitted preservation,
   absent history policy and unresolved target reference. Corrected reconstruction
   remains possible after rejection, followed by a valid reset.
3. Representation with missing correspondence, missing edge mapping, wrong
   orientation or duplicate target mapping after positive evolution. Schema-valid
   defects have recomputed correspondence identities. They cannot silently turn
   into lossy reconstruction. A correct action subsequently preserves W/Z,
   transports current/reset C by the literal vertex permutation, and resets.
4. C_OS evolution → migration → reconstruction → attempted strict C_PC
   migration/event. The live-state-as-reset control passes, but the actual
   distinct reset resource lies outside the target chart. Both attempts fail at
   `target_readmission` with the entire previous publication intact; reset on
   that unchanged source succeeds afterward.

Five additional restore mutations in method 1 remove all crossing archives,
the migration archive, the event archive, an event source-reset preimage, or a
required receipt member. The complete snapshot is a passing restore control.

## Deliberate boundaries

- A valid fresh model may have an empty receipt history. “Missing lineage” here
  means required crossing or receipt evidence is absent from an established
  operation history, not that genesis needs a fabricated predecessor.
- C `W_C,tr` references are admitted before entering the typed registry. P9-7.4
  covers their malformed construction; P9-7.5 exercises invalid operation maps
  and unresolved target selection without bypassing that boundary.
- Missing receipt membership/archives is not proof of the full parent-DAG
  semantics (forward/self/cyclic references, ordering and duplicate ownership).
  Those and deep-copy ownership remain P9-7.6.
- No all-profile-pair, graph, parameter, failure-stage or specialization claim.
  Previous accepted numerical/evidence subjects and runtime permissions remain
  unchanged. P9-7.4's isolated readmission evidence is reused, not relabeled as
  this mixed-prefix run.

Development initially expected an unadmitted preservation policy to return a
runtime failure rather than fail the event-specific wire check, and an empty
edge correspondence to fail syntax rather than graph-aware admission. The tests
were corrected to require the actual contractual boundaries; production was
not changed. Reset checks also require nontrivial live/reset differences after
intervening evolution, avoiding vacuous reset-after-reset controls.
