The [six-test suite](../verification/test_p973_history_policy.py) separates:

| Coverage | What is checked | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |

Native receipt checks reconstruct history-content hashes independently from
actual current/reset arrays. They check the candidate-before-carrier loss tuple
on every emitted core, target histories, charge target, unchanged clock and
step index, and snapshot restoration. Present all-zero Z remains a history
channel and receives reconstruction loss; absent Z does not. Initialization
does not recover source history. Same-candidate preserving migration and
explicit reconstruction remain different policies even on matching graph sizes.

The existing accepted migration evidence covers other directional classes,
including P→NP removal. The accepted event evidence covers all ten target
families, changed graph sizes, initializer staging and replay pressure. They
are prerequisites and scope references, not rerun or relabeled as new 7.3
executions. P9-7.4 target-reference completeness, P9-7.5 broader failure
composition and P9-7.6 receipt lineage review remain separate leaves.
