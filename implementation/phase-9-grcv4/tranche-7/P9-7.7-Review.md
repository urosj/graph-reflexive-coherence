- **7.1:** ten concrete ordinary/replay/reset/rebase/duplication witnesses and
  family-specific pressure tests. These preserve exact seed IDs and endpoints.
  The compact family record does not itself retain a full catalog result row
  for every common negative. Test-roster evidence must not be presented as an
  observed solver/failure/result tuple that it does not contain.
- **Tranches 5–6:** accepted candidate equations, controls, stages, independent
  oracles and concrete realization results. The 6.5 routing gives source-bound
  numerical evidence, not a declaration that all common/candidate catalog rows
  executed through each nominated lifecycle owner. Control parameter variants
  need their own identities and justified relationship to the nomination.
- **7.2a:** thirteen original ordered migration witnesses plus five accepted
  C→A initializer witnesses are retained with both endpoint IDs. All seven
  registered classes stay visible. A positive example in a class does not
  establish every applicable ordered pair in a proposed support set. Source
  construction rejection, unsupported-target rejection, executed positive and
  justified nonapplicability remain distinct.
- **7.2b:** reconstruction and representation witnesses exist for every family.
  All five A reconstruction targets and the C_RG2b target differ from their 7.1
  seed profile IDs. C_CI/C_PC/C_CI+PC match the nominations, but that alone is
  not the full product. Representation source/target IDs are retained separately.
  An event receipt is not automatically a reset-after-event result.
- **7.3–7.6:** accepted history/charge, target-reference, failure/reset and
  parent/duplicate evidence is linked as bounded shared follow-through.
  The A_PC/A_CI+PC/C_PC mixed sequences and other finite pressure cases must not

  every unrelated profile. Sixteen symbolic parent rejections stay symbolic.

## Lean closure work

Use these three bounded obligations for each held child, without new scientific
investigations or a blanket full-suite rerun:

1. **G2-EXACT-PRODUCT:** reconcile the listed common/candidate/realization/lifecycle
   cells. Recover existing exact assertions where possible and bind the catalog's
   required result fields to actual input/output evidence. Missing retained
   fields are an evidence gap, not a diagnosed numerical bug. Where existing
   records cannot establish the assertion, add the narrow missing capture/test.
2. **G2-ORDERED-ENDPOINTS:** freeze the proposed profile/control population and
   applicable ordered migration/event pairs, including current/reset authority,
   reference/initializer, independent W/Z history and target readmission. Reuse
   matching evidence; execute only unresolved applicable crossings or failure/reset
   cases. Do not transfer one profile's positive or negative credit by family name.
3. **G2-INTEGRATED-REVIEW:** review the completed exact product and public-facade
   behavior, then propose support for only those identities. User acceptance is
   separate; it alone permits updating discovery/support. A sibling's HOLD does
   not block a complete profile. G3 remains P9-7.8 for the exact accepted set.

The first substantive continuation can be the A_OS child's existing numerical
and lifecycle evidence reconciliation. This is a scheduling choice, not a weaker
G2 criterion or a requirement that all nine children close together.
