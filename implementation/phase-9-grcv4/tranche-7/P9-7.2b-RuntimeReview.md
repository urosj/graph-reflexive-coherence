## Operations and boundaries

- `GRCV4.reconstruct_topology_event(GRCV4MappedTopologyEventRequest)` explicitly
  selects the accepted lossy policy and v2 event receipt. Both affine resources
  are computed with the existing exact-input/round-once rule; A targets use
  two mapped-resource reference-pass invocations, C targets rederive their
  fixed references, and persistent targets receive complete target-sized zero
  carriers. W and Z loss declarations remain independent.
- `GRCV4.transport_representation(GRCV4RepresentationRequest)` applies declared
  bijections and signs to both current/reset. It requires exact agreement of
  the complete transformed profile, K4/Hodge/W_C,tr references and registered
  A host-frame backend. Unsupported or incompatible recipes reject. It emits
  one primary with embedded numerical charge and history evidence, and advances
  the successful-primary head even for an identity coordinate map.
- Existing `apply_topology_event` retains the C_OS v1 path. A caller cannot
  accidentally opt into W/Z loss merely by supplying an isomorphic graph.
  The new explicit method is also required for C_OS v2 reconstruction.

The sole lifecycle owner retains atomic publication. Ordinary steps and reset
use the preceding operation's primary, independent of group size. New snapshot
layouts use the accepted joint release, while the predecessor codec's snapshot
entry still rejects those layouts. Lifecycle restoration dispatches explicitly,
recomputes archived maps and pairs, and retains old receipt versions unchanged.
Ordinary evolved histories are not forced to equal historical initializer output.

The coordinate implementation supports the existing fixed graph/constant-zero
context and host-frame backend actions only. Complete physical parameters and
chart/recipe declarations must agree under that action, then both targets must
pass their existing numerical owners. This is not arbitrary spatial rotation,
an inferred graph isomorphism, opaque chart transport or an all-pairs guarantee.
