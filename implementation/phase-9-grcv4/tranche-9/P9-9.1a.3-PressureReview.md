## Finding and correction

Repeated delivery of the same typed ordinary request previously committed a
second physical beat and advanced the window. The public transaction owner now
rejects an explicit operation ID already in the branch's successful ancestry,
before preparation and under the publication lock. This applies to ordinary
and expansion IDs. Failed requests remain retryable; fork/restore inherits the
ancestry. Untyped `step()` continues to represent a new requested physical beat.
The scope is branch ancestry, not a global delivery registry.
