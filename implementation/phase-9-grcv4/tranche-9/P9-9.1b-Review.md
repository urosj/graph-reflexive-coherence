## Implementation and purpose

[GRC9V4EnabledOperation](../../../src/pygrc/models/grc_9_v4_owner.py) combines
ordinary steps, explicitly requested expansion, reset and rebase under one
immutable publication and one successful receipt journal. Tranche 8's event
receiver and generic numerical kernels are reused unchanged. Its separate
event-then-generic-owner lifecycle experiment did not establish this shared
identity, journal or mixed-operation replay.

Every published step uses the complete nine-port model identity, including
specialization, and the nine-port reset identity. Kernel-local generic IDs
remain diagnostics. The generic result binder's deliberate specialization
rejection remains intact; the new owner binds actual specialization endpoints,
profile, clock, commit and receipt delta before its sole publication write.
Previous-successful-primary parentage crosses operation kinds, including zero
duration. Rejected and inconclusive operations do not append to history.

Expansion runs in a disposable transaction receiver seeded with the entire
current ledger. Fresh detection and both-role target admission use the
accepted receiver. Only its fully admitted publication is transferred to the
live owner. No allocator is selected automatically, no completion is counted,
and no autonomous topology law is introduced.

Reset restores C/W/Z from the independently transferred baseline and preserves
the current clock and receipt prefix. Rebase changes the baseline to current
authority after readmission. Snapshots retain the admitted initial seed,
ordered successful requests, final native state, receipts, carrier archives
and reconstructed reference-current evidence. Restore replays the mixed
journal and compares the complete canonical result. It does not trust derived
cache input or infer chronology before the seed. Numerical backend/policy is
explicit in the snapshot; inconclusive certificates propagate without fallback.

This is an internal enabled owner. Public facade/discovery, disabled GRC9V3
delegation and crossings remain later Tranche 9 obligations. Existing GRC9V3,
generic runtime, accepted numerical kernels, specs, scientific claims and debts
are unchanged.
