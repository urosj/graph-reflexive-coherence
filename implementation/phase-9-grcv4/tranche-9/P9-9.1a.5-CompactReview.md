## Retained evidence and exact reconstruction

The compact records have their own authenticated chains as well.

The expanded predictor/corrector/final/restart currents and flat vectors, generated H and split diagnostic are deterministic rational functions of these inputs. Conversion reconstructed **all 753 original JSON records exactly**, checked each original record hash and final original chain hash, and retained expanded-byte hashes. The conversion took **600.65 seconds**. No floating-point approximation replaces an exact operand.

The ten complete original samples cover, for each role, the first step, the step before entry, entry, the next window state and completion.
