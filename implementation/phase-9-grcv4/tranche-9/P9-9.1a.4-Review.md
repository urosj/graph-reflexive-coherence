After setup and one step per role, the measured estimate permitted 20 steps;
the subsequent costs permitted extending to **40 changing-state steps per role**.

2^-128 edge-flux rounding and Arb512 certificate/oracle arithmetic. Thread
environment, machine/Python version, per-step component times and memory are
retained. OS/library caches were not flushed; graph/proof caches were explicitly
cleared for the cold measurements.

The **combined planning estimate is 13.6 minutes, with a conservative allowance
of 24.8 minutes**, within a **30-minute combined campaign cap**. These totals
include setup, independent per-transition checks, complete transition
serialization/compression, synthetic window costs, 24 checkpoint captures and
eight warm restores. Checkpoint capture cost is scaled to 400 beats, and the
completed synthetic checkpoint is also measured. Local storage latency is
covered by the safety allowance; network/durable-storage latency and a complete
cold replay are outside this campaign estimate. Re-estimate if either is added.

The allowance uses 1.5 times each role's observed maximum step, twice maximum
oracle/transition-output costs, twice setup/persistence and three times the
synthetic window cost. It is a planning envelope, not a statistical confidence
interval. Current and reset estimates come from their own changing states on
this exact subject. No larger-graph timings are substituted.

Measured peak process memory was **203.5 MiB**. Resource representation remained
bounded at 128/129 numerator/denominator bits in these samples. There is no
full-horizon memory guarantee. Runtime publication, reset comparison and
independent proof checks are included separately from ordinary evolution in the
record; inverse timing is an inclusive subcategory, not added twice.

## Cache and arithmetic checks

Graph facts have actual hits during the public steps. A changed port chart
causes a fresh graph-cache miss. Both certificates are proved cold and reused
warm; proof-cache counters remain unchanged throughout the 80 target steps.
A changed certificate duration misses and rejects without caching a proof.
Every sampled step has a distinct generated-geometry key, four fixed-geometry
reads and only two selected inverse calls: the consumed-H and reference restart
reads reuse the already admitted operators within that step. Generated geometry
is freshly solved on every changing state.

Delegating spies observe active FLINT inside the reads. Every sampled state
keeps exact charge 21. Independent full-map Arb512 checks at steps 1, 20 and 40
of each role pass in the actual certificate norm. These bounded checks do not
establish the full campaign or the expected entry beat. The three-step window
cost probe is explicitly synthetic, supplied at the certificate center.

The numerical evolution dominates cost, and the existing reuse is active. No
speculative arithmetic/cache optimization is needed to meet the budget. A new
family, graph, profile, numerical policy or materially different evidence/replay
schedule requires its own preflight.
