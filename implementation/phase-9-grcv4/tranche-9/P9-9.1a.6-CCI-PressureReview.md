## What the checks establish

The independent root audit starts from the exact decoded native input. It rebuilds
H at the saved proposal and certifies the residual-to-root argument; it never
uses native residual or derivative functions as its oracle. Every coordinate of
the saved f/H/J enclosure must contain a fresh independently validated root
component. Supplied intervals merely overlapping fresh ones are insufficient.
The full returned tuple is checked against the coupled defining equations;
y is independently derived as `2Hf`, then tested in the linear-current and J
equations. Full conditioning and current-width budgets are checked as well.

For the consumed represented flux, the audit checks

    e_write = C_after − (C_before − 32 B J_flux) = 0
    E_J >= |J_flux − J_exact(C_before)|
    ||error||_K <= 32 || |UB| E_J ||∞ < exact native image margin.

The native certificate supplies its own exact q and margin, not rounded
reference display constants from the work order. The independently checked
source/target inner-root contraction bounds also remain separate from physical
contraction. Uniform Banach bounds dominate the reference homotopy
`H = I + t diag(f) O diag(f)/8`, `0≤t≤1`; uniqueness is confined to the selected
flat ball, not asserted globally or for arbitrary solver seeds.

Two false test requirements were deliberately excluded: a non-dyadic rational
input need not become a point Arb value, and symmetric exact geometry need not
have byte-identical transposed interval endpoints. Actual input containment,
frozen trial midpoints, independent whole-root containment and the geometry
equations establish the needed properties. A negative lower residual endpoint
is harmless when its independently verified finite upper bound is valid.

Synthetic completion windows exercise publication semantics near the domain
center. They are not actual source-to-entry runs. No pre-entry use of q_K, global
orbit-shadowing claim, hardcoded endpoint decision, warm-start authority or
aggregate native completion support is introduced. The current policy always
starts root proposals at zero. Arbitrary private-code mutations are detected by
the independent pressure oracle; explicitly rejected native admission failures
are tested separately for atomic rollback.
