## Delivered behavior

- `inspect` authenticates the accepted support identity and the selected
  input/campaign/checkpoint bytes. All ten family endpoints are available with
  exact identity, history-policy, role and certificate information. It executes
  no native step or certificate-proof reconstruction.
- `prepare` uses only public model operations to execute the actual source beat,
  explicit D11 expansion and ordered current/reset certificate admission. It
  checks exact target equality. It never initializes at the equilibrium or a
  saved successful endpoint and imports no test or investigation helpers.
- `run` advances ordinary target steps until the public completed decision and
  local increment, failing explicitly on rejection or an exhausted cap. New
  reports/checkpoints are compressed, never overwrite old results, and do not
  acquire historical acceptance. Each family selects its own pinned declaration.
- `replay` restores the complete public checkpoint. Cold restoration is an
  explicit numerical journal execution, separate from retained inspection and
  warm duplication. The guide explains process-scoped once-only accounting.

The guide distinguishes capability from current-occurrence completion; proof
applicability from actual domain entry; spatial basin organization from separate
full-state attractors; and the reset-history comparison endpoint from a new
completed spark. It covers full C/W/Z history, numerical policies, qualified
parent/local gain, positive-window timing, failed-operation rollback and virtual
evidence-object references. Current/reset endpoints, duration and family-specific
proof limits are listed for all ten accepted families.

The examples index and side-tool API/browser/notebook provide the new entry
point. The browser shows portable commands and links to the exact guide/code.
Reading those views never starts a run. The existing family views and accepted
ordinary-example publication retain their historical scopes and bytes.
