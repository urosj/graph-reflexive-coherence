## Independent review corrections

| Finding | Correction and retained check |

| BIF-1: terminal records hide upstream cycles | Require the full SCC partition, cyclic/terminal flags and each node's own component reference. The fixed-multigraph `(1,0,1)` versus `(2,1,1)` regression has equal terminal assignments and node divergence but distinct upstream-cycle evidence; singleton loops and upstream noncyclic nodes are distinguished. |
| BIF-2: state/tolerance does not bind an observation | Specify one fresh read, role, context/evaluator/numerical-policy binding, lossless operands, nonrecursive state projection and explicit exact-replay versus new-observation semantics. | BIF-3: D4 applies beyond direct causal feedback | Add the D4/D9 bridge for causal state, normalization, hierarchy, events and basin-indexed cross-state comparisons. Derived labels confer no interspace identity or physical birth; unchanged hashes do not transport changed membership. This is a contract clarification, not a numerical test claim. |
