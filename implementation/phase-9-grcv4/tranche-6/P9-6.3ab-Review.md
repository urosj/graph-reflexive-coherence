## Composite domain and uniform bounds

Let M be the resource radius, W in `[w_min,w_max]` for A, R the carrier radius,
k=`|kappa_H|`, and D the reference Hodge-ball radius encoded by
`CIBoundedDomain`. Admission requires **D >= 2*k*R**, not merely `D >= k*R`.
The full D ball must be SPD, conditioned, current-regular, and (for C) on one
strict-gap selector stratum. This explicitly admits the design's entire B_2R
geometry image, including signed histories and sources. Pointwise cancellation
between Z and S cannot hide a failed whole-domain obligation.

The accepted PC whole-chart norm proof is evaluated over this larger geometry
ball, rather than the old PC radius kR. It yields a flat-read bound V and a
source bound `m=|zeta|*V^2 < R`, with strict uniform source slack. This is a
computed bound for all base states in the declared chart and all trial h in the
ball; source containment is not imposed as an assumed root condition. Stored
binary64 source and written carrier also satisfy their exact rational ball
inequalities. Final C/W chart invariance is checked, not inferred from carrier
invariance.

The extension derives a uniform h-Lipschitz flat-read bound L on the same chart.
For A, let J0bar and L0 bound the baseline and its h derivative, b=`|zeta*chi|`,
and d=`1-b>0`. The existing baseline bound replaces reference-relative radius
by D, with `L0=eta*w_max*||B^T B||*|kappa_Ah|*||B^T||*M`.
The contrast satisfies `|q|<=1` and
`Lq<=|gamma|*J0bar*L0/2`: `max(log(W_floor), exponent)` is nonexpansive and the
contrast derivative with respect to log target is bounded by 1/2. Therefore
`Jbar=J0bar/d`, `LJ=L0/d+J0bar*b*Lq/d^2`, and
`L=|chi|*(Jbar/l^2+(Lq*Jbar+LJ)/l)`, where l is the Hodge lower bound.
The whole-chart bound is Lipschitz across a conductance floor. Numerical root
admission also retains the existing per-base-state CI floor-chart check; no
classical differentiability across a floor is claimed. The conductance finite
range bound still uses endpoint functional norm 2 on loops and sqrt(2) on
ordinary edges; the A law itself has not changed.

For C, the existing physical-coordinate CI derivative estimate is made uniform
by `||C||<=M`, `||B^T C||<=||B^T||*M`. With selector gap g,
`L_T<=2*||B^T B||*M/g`. The retained deformation/Hodge, Q and Q-inverse, and
resolvent product rules then bound the physical response and its derivative.
The current inverse margin is `1-|zeta*chi|*response_upper`; retained Hodge
positivity alone does not establish that margin. At tau_C=0 the physical
response is exactly identity and its derivative is zero. The flat derivative
includes both inverse-Hodge variation and current/response variation. This is
the same norm argument as the accepted CI proof, evaluated on the whole compact
base chart; no sampled certificate or caller-supplied Lipschitz assertion is
accepted.

The composite map then has displacement at most `k*(R+m)<=D` and contraction
`q=2*k*|zeta|*V*L<1`. These estimates hold uniformly for every old Z in B_R;
Z is a parameter, not an additional root unknown. They also hold while k is
scaled from zero to its declared value, establishing the reference connection.
The existing per-state candidate regularity checks remain additional numerical
admission. Conservative failure does not prove that the scientific profile has
no root. Multiple/disconnected branches are not searched or silently selected.
Neither this h-root contraction nor the carrier ball proves matched-future-
forcing contraction of different histories.

## Architectural review: shared ownership and coupling

CI+PC admission spans both modules. The temporal orchestration already has one
owner, `ProvisionalCandidateCIStep`; its certificate depends on PC's envelope.
The benefit is one implementation of each scientific obligation. The cost is
private cross-module dependencies and realization branches that must be reviewed
together. Reuse does not establish clean module independence.

| Obligation / entry | Existing owner and dependency |
|---|---|
| Typed CI solver declaration, reference Hodge domain | `grc_v4_ci._declarations`, `CIBoundedDomain`; PC's composite declarations call this parser, which does not call PC. |
| Compact base/carrier admission, B_2R coverage, uniform source and flat derivative bounds | `grc_v4_pc.PCEnvelopeCertificate`; composite contraction calls it once before solving. Plain PC already imports CI norm/exponential utilities and the stage-error type. |
| Old-Z offset, held S, analytic joint residual | `grc_v4_ci._old_carrier`, `_effective_source`, `CITrial`, `_analytic_residual`; effective `Z+S` remains distinct from the source used by the writer. |
| Composite displacement/contraction and canonical root | `grc_v4_ci.CIContractionCertificate`, `CandidateCIRoot`; the certificate consumes the PC envelope, the root additionally checks stored S with PC's ball predicate. |
| Beat ordering and independent final readmission | `grc_v4_ci.ProvisionalCandidateCIStep`; delegates continuity and A's W writer, then PC's `scalar_zoh` and carrier check, then a separate final root. |
| Standalone PC read | `grc_v4_pc.CandidatePCRead` requires plain `PCParams` and rejects composite declarations. |

The composite call chain terminates at the CI declaration parser; there is no
recursive certificate construction. Deferred CI-to-PC imports avoid a module
initialization cycle, but do not remove coupling. The architectural note's
claim that cross-imports are inactive for plain PC is too broad: its CI utility
and error-type imports are unconditional. Nor is "never build new layers" a
scientific requirement of these contracts.

Decision for this audit: retain the numerical owners and make the complete
composition contract explicit here. Extracting only the effective source and
certificate into a common function would not remove declaration, ball-check,
error-type and writer dependencies; these operations also have distinct inputs
and ordering. A future refactor may introduce explicit shared admission APIs
and a composition orchestrator without duplicating solvers. It should preserve
the pressure witnesses above and address the full dependency direction. This
review records the maintenance cost without treating such a refactor as a
scientific blocker or claiming it has already been resolved. Reassess the
boundary when introducing composite lifecycle/publication ownership.
