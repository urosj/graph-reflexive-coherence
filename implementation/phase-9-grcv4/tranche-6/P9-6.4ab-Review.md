## Ownership and frozen declarations

`grc_v4_rg2b.py` owns one graph-transform evaluator, its computed certificate,
and provisional A/C beat orchestration. It reuses CI's rational interval and
exponential enclosures and common structural-source assembly, and delegates
native currents, continuity and A's writer to their existing owners. No CI or
PC module imports RG2b. Candidate A only gains recognition of the RG2b family
and its selected writer stage. No second production current or writer is
introduced: scalar analytic reductions supply the certificate and are checked
against the native equations and independent matrix/current oracles.

The existing `RG2bParams` binds these versioned algorithms:

| Field | Binding |

The eight coordinates are resource center, A mobility center (canonical 1 for
C), inner/core/outer state radii, Hodge value radius r, section Lipschitz ceiling
L, and positive beat duration. Unknown/noncanonical IDs reject. Error tolerance
and total inverse-evaluation budget remain profile parameters. Every recursive
inverse evaluation spends that shared budget, with at most 64 iterations per
inverse and 16 graph-transform levels. No arbitrary callable, fitted section,
stored geometry, previous root, branch token or continuation state is accepted.
The sole constant cache is the exact logarithm enclosure of 2.

Coordinates are `(C_tail,C_head)` for C and `(C_tail,C_head,W)` for A, with
the declared coordinate sup norm; geometry uses absolute scalar Hodge error.
The frozen center has equal resource coordinates, so relabeling/reorientation
permutes the resource coordinates without selecting a preferred vertex.
The typed K4-to-Hodge operator norm is `abs(kappa_H)` in these specific scalar
coordinates. This does not identify K4 and geometry units in general.

## Candidate reductions and completion

Write `d=C_tail-C_head`, `h0=h_ref`, and `dt=beat_dt`. The repository incidence
has +1 at the tail and -1 at the head; continuity is `C-dt*B*J`.

For A:

```text
J0 = -2 eta W [kappa_c W + kappa_Ah (h-h0)] d
W_hat = exp[-(alpha(C_tail+C_head) + gamma J0^2)/2]
q = (W-W_hat)/(W+W_hat)
J = J0/(1-zeta chi q)
S = zeta (chi q J/h)^2
f_C = (-dt J, dt J)
v = -(alpha(C_tail+C_head) + gamma J^2)/2
a = exp(-dt/tau_A)
f_W = W [exp((1-a)(v-log W))-1]
```

The certificate requires both conductance floors to be strictly inactive on
the whole outer chart. The writer uses the selected total J and final C;
the analytic resource sum is conserved, which gives the displayed v. Native
binary64 conservation/writer discrepancies are checked separately. The stable
increment form bounds f_W at order dt instead of subtracting unrelated wide
intervals for W_next and W. No mobility is redefined by the section.

For C, `T=(mean(C),mean(C))` in rank one and `T=C` in rank two:

```text
hM = h exp[(kappa_M/2)(tanh(T_tail/C_ref)+tanh(T_head/C_ref))]
R = 1/(1+2 tau_C hM)
J0 = -2 eta_C W_tr kappa_Phi hM d
J = J0/(1-zeta_C chi_C R)
S = zeta_C (chi_C R J/h)^2
f_C = (-dt J, dt J)
```

Scalar physical identification is a similarity and therefore cancels in the
response; the native current still reconstructs the full C package at the
section geometry. The declared selector ball must satisfy either
`0 < Lambda < 2(h0-r)` or `Lambda > 2(h0+r)`. Denominators must remain strictly
positive and conditioned over the entire chart. Conservative rejection is not
a proof that the wider mathematical family lacks a section.

Let K_minus and K be closed coordinate boxes of inner and core radii, and U
the open outer box, with strict nesting. On each coordinate, the cutoff is 1
through the core radius, 0 at/outside the outer radius, and
`1-3u^2+2u^3` in the intervening shell. Multiply the coordinate cutoffs to get
beta(X). The extension is exactly

```text
Phi_ext(X,h) = X + beta(X) f(X,h)
G_ext(X,h) = h0 + kappa_H beta(X) S(X,h).
```

It agrees with the candidate maps on K and becomes identity/reference outside
a compact neighborhood. Its cutoff is C1 with zero endpoint derivative and
coordinate-sup Lipschitz bound `D_cut=3*dim(X)/(2*(outer-core))`.
The completion is equivariant and fixed by profile identity, not by a trajectory.

## Computed theorem obligations

Interval chain rules enclose **candidate-map** derivatives on the complete
outer box times `[h0-r,h0+r]`; they do not differentiate the invariant section.
Let M_f,F_X,F_h and M_G,G_X,G_h bound the unextended increment/source and their
state and geometry derivatives in the indicated norms. Extension changes the
state bounds to `F_X+D_cut*M_f` and `G_X+D_cut*M_G`; the other bounds persist.
Below F_X and G_X denote those extended bounds.

The certificate checks each obligation using rational upper bounds:

```text
ell = F_X + F_h L < 1
M_inverse = 1/(1-ell)
M_f <= core-inner                         [K_minus containment]
epsilon_H M_G <= r                        [value self-map]
epsilon_H (G_X+G_h L) M_inverse <= L       [Lipschitz self-map]
q0 = epsilon_H [G_h+(G_X+G_h L) M_inverse F_h] < 1.
```

It also checks the full Hodge ball, C selector gap, A positive W/floor charts,
native policy/backend and whole-chart current block. These are sufficient
computed conditions, not caller-supplied constants or sample-based admission.
`X+f_Gamma(X)` is globally invertible because `Lip(f_Gamma)<=ell<1`.
The complete bounded Lipschitz section class is a closed sup-norm space, and
Banach's theorem supplies one section relative to this frozen completion.
Uniqueness and covariance are restricted to that completion and chart.

## Finite evaluation and independent error

Start every query from `Gamma_0=h0`, and evaluate `Gamma_n=T^n Gamma_0` by
recursive inverse evaluation. For a proposed inverse point x at target y,
an interval enclosure of `x+f(x,Gamma_(n-1)(x))-y` with radius/residual eta
gives inverse-coordinate error at most `M_inverse*eta`. The graph-transform
output error is at most

```text
epsilon_H (G_X+G_h L) M_inverse*eta.
```

This converts inverse error to the declared geometry norm before allocating
precision. Binary64 inverse iterates are only numerical work; exact interval
residuals decide success, including at floating stagnation. Interval arithmetic
and the existing exponential enclosure use a 160-bit outward grid. The new
log enclosure uses dyadic range reduction and 64 positive atanh-series terms
with an explicit geometric tail. Ambient Decimal precision and libm never
decide a section certificate.

The theorem supplies `||Gamma_n-Gamma||_sup <= r*q0^n`. Add this tail to the
finite evaluation enclosure, round its midpoint once to binary64, and include
that final rounding in `error_upper`. Admission requires `error_upper <=`
the profile error tolerance and the stored point in the Hodge ball. Inverse,
depth, total-work or precision exhaustion rejects without reference/CI fallback.
Zero gain or an identically zero bounded source gives the exact neutral section.

Independent tests include a 100-digit C cubic preimage construction and an A
explicit inverse/log-writer construction, with their own truncation bounds;
they call no production section or inverse helper. Strong nonzero-coupling
witnesses separate RG2b from a same-beat CI root by more than the combined
certified/tolerated errors. A small invariance residual alone is not used as a
certificate of closeness to the section.

## Native beat and claim separation

Current and reset states independently enter K_minus. Reconstruct the section,
solve the native fixed-h current, execute continuity once, then execute A's
refreshed writer once. Generated geometry uses the selected current's source;
it supplies no same-beat corrector and is never written as history. The final
state must enter K and receives an independent section reconstruction.

Let e be the initial section error and e_final the final section error. Native
arithmetic is independently compared to the analytic scalar maps at the stored
h, giving state discrepancy mu_X and generated-geometry discrepancy mu_G.
The current discrepancy must pass the declared solver absolute/relative norm
budget; `mu_G` and `L*mu_X` must each pass the RG geometry-error budget. The
final stored invariance residual is checked against

```text
e_final + mu_G + epsilon_H G_h e + L (mu_X+F_h e).
```

The current comparison is necessary: reversing the current sign while also
reversing the continuity convention leaves the even structural source and base
map unchanged, so geometry-only checks would miss a wrong physical current.
A retained fault-injection regression pressures this distinction.

At zero requested duration, admission still reconstructs the section for the
frozen positive-beat profile, but performs no continuity/history write or clock
advance. It does not assert the positive-beat invariance equation for an identity
transition. No provisional output is returned on failure; live atomic commit
and rollback remain lifecycle work. Programmer exceptions propagate.
