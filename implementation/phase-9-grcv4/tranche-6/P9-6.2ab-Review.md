## Declared domain and sufficient certificate

`PCBaseChart(M,w_min,w_max)` is encoded canonically in `source_envelope_id`.
Together with `PCParams.radius=R`, `carrier_norm_id=symmetric_star_frobenius_v1`,
the fixed reference/profile, and the versioned recipe, it declares:

- `K_X = {C >= 0, ||C||_2 <= M}`; A additionally has every retained W in the
  closed positive interval `[w_min,w_max]`. C's unused bounds canonically equal 1.
- `B_R = {Z in the symmetric star-supported structural space: ||Z||_F <= R}`.
- The geometry image `h = h_ref + kappa_H Z`, with radius
  `r = |kappa_H| R`. Its analytic eigenvalues are bounded by
  `l = min(w_ref)-r > 0` and `u = max(w_ref)+r`, with `u/l` below the
  declared conditioning limit. All varying bounds enter complete profile identity.

The certificate is uniform over **all C/W in this chart**, not inferred from
the submitted point or a solver neighborhood. Exact rational arithmetic and
outward norm/exponential bounds provide the following sufficient conditions.

Let B be incidence and `D_C = ||B^T||_2 M` (bounded above by
`sqrt(||B^T||_1 ||B^T||_inf) M`). A's baseline satisfies

`||J0|| <= eta*w_max*||B^T B||*(|kappa_c|*w_max + |kappa_Ah|*r)*D_C`.

Since conductance and W are positive, every contrast has `|q_e| <= 1`.
With `beta=|zeta_A chi_A|<1`, the current inverse is bounded by
`1/(1-beta)`, its condition by `(1+beta)/(1-beta)`, and flat read by
`|chi_A|*||J0||/((1-beta)*l)`. Exact WLS normal matrices bound each
node gradient over the C ball: `||grad_i|| <= ||normal_i^-1|| sqrt(2) M
sum_j w_ij ||position_j-position_i||`. Loops have zero endpoint displacement
and contribute zero to this gradient bound. The endpoint **sum** in conductance
has a different norm: `sigma_e=||e_u+e_v||`, equal to `sqrt(2)` for distinct
endpoints and `2` for a self-loop. With `sigma=max_e sigma_e`, the conservative
absolute exponent bound is
`(|alpha| sigma M + |beta| (2*gradient_upper)^2 + |gamma| current_upper^2)/2`.
Admission requires that bound to be at most 700. It covers current and refreshed
writer targets, including either side of the conductance floor; the floor
never clamps old W. This is a finite-range admission condition, not a smooth
floor-chart or maximal-domain claim.

C uses `d >= exp(|kappa_M_C|/2)`, so retained Hodge lies between
`l_M=l/d^2` and `u_M=u*d^2`. A strict-cut selector stratum is certified by
`g = 1/||(B h_ref B^T - Lambda I)^-1|| - ||B^T B|| r > 0`.
This is one whole-ball stratum, not permission to cross an unresolved cutoff.
If the shifted reference matrix is exactly singular, the cutoff lies on the
reference spectrum: admission raises a selector `domain_failure`, before
constructing any physical current block. It is not a `singular_solver` report
about the consumed current.
For positive tau_C, the physical response `Q^-1 R_C Q` has upper bound
`P=(u^2/l_M)*sqrt(u_M/l_M)*(u_M/l^2)`; for tau_C=0 it is exactly identity.
Retained positivity alone is not a physical current margin. Require
`beta=|zeta_C chi_C| P < 1` and the same inverse/condition bounds.
The full C baseline norm is at most
`max(eta_C W_C_tr)*|kappa_Phi_C|*||B^T B||*D_C*u_M`.
Thus flat read is bounded by `|chi_C| P ||J0||/((1-beta) l)`.

Normalized star assembly multiplies each entry of `v v^T` by a coefficient
of magnitude at most one (shared-star count divided by the geometric mean of
star incidences). Its Frobenius norm is therefore at most `||v||_2^2`.
For both candidates the source envelope is
`m_upper=|zeta|*flat_upper^2 <= R`. Chi is already in the read; zeta is applied
once outside the quadratic assembly. The convex ZOH law then preserves the
analytic carrier ball for a held source. Conservative certificate failure
rejects a domain; it does not prove the underlying scientific profile impossible.

Stored geometry/current use the existing admitted binary64 candidate solvers.
Stored sources and Z are separately tested against the exact rational
Frobenius inequality. Final C/W must remain in the declared base chart; base
state invariance is **not** inferred from carrier invariance. Rounding or an
out-of-chart poststate can therefore reject a beat. Nothing is clipped,
projected, silently renormalized, or transported across a selector boundary.

## Read/write order and numerical writer

`CandidatePCRead` derives h from old Z and rebuilds the full candidate chain.
For C this includes selector, retained Hodge, Phi0, J0 and physical current.
The captured source is the once-gated accepted read of that current.
`ProvisionalCandidatePCStep` admits independent reset/current states, selects
one current, executes common continuity once, then A's refreshed-C/J W writer
(if applicable), then one Z writer from **old Z and the held pre-continuity
source**. A's intermediate writer preserves Z. Final h/current reconstruction
uses the final state as a postcondition; it cannot feed back into that beat.
Failures expose no partially published authoritative state. This is immutable
provisional computation, not a live atomic-commit/rollback implementation.
Zero duration admits both states and policy, advances no clock/index/resource,
and invokes neither history writer.

`scalar_zoh` encloses `exp(-dt/tau)` using correctly rounded Decimal exponential,
adjacent Decimal argument/value bounds, and exact rational affine evaluation.
It requires both interval endpoints to round to the same binary64 number,
with bounded 800/1600-digit precision refinement, otherwise rejects. It never
first divides dt/tau in binary64 or forms an overflowing binary64 endpoint
difference. At ratio >=1600, even the maximum finite endpoint separation times
`exp(-1600)` is less than half the smallest subnormal, so returning the source
is justified. In particular ratio 1000 must still preserve a representable
release from a very large carrier. Mutable Decimal contexts cannot change the
recipe. Equality and zero duration preserve the supplied values exactly.
Computed rounded zero is canonicalized to +0 by the established numerical
boundary, both at the Z writer and C's external source-gain multiplication.
Negative-zero **inputs** remain forbidden, including every early-return branch;
canonicalization is neither a carrier repair nor a change to the ZOH law.

Finite binary64 underflow may erase a mathematically nonzero formation/release
term; it is not a finite-time release theorem or administrative reset. Repeated
held-source steps agree with the exponential semigroup up to storage rounding.
No matched-forcing contraction constant has been certified here: a carrier
ball/source bound alone does not establish the strict coefficient-plus-source-
Lipschitz inequality. No downstream endpoint hysteresis is claimed.

## P9-6.2c scientific reconciliation and audit disposition

**F1 (A_PC and C_PC): closed.** The existing float-equality assertions could
not distinguish -0 from +0, and ordinary-scale orientation pressure did not
exercise signed underflow. A negative scalar release and its positive sign
counterpart therefore differed in acceptance despite equal numeric zeros.
C's source gain had the same originating conversion defect. The correction
uses `_computed` at both sites, while strict numeric input admission is
unchanged. Native regressions now check sign bits, consumption by the state
boundary, a second PC step, and signed-edge transformed counterparts. Further
pressure covers negative off-diagonal source entries with **positive** zeta,
so the fix is not limited to negative global gain.

**F2 (A_PC): closed.** The initial proof silently assumed distinct endpoints
when bounding `C_u+C_v`. On a loop this is `2*C_u`; zero loop incidence and
zero loop gradient do not remove its resource-dependent conductance term.
The corrected graph-based maximum endpoint-functional norm preserves the
loopless sqrt(2) bound. A loop with alpha=-600 now certifies the actual exponent
600; alpha=-800 rejects the entire radius-one chart even when submitted C=0.
Mixed graphs, either alpha sign, the exact threshold 700 and its immediately
larger binary64 neighbour are separately checked. G_W and the retained W
writer have not changed. The original descriptor-difference, baseline,
contrast-margin and star-norm arguments were re-examined: loop differences
vanish there, parallel edges remain explicit coordinates, and no analogous
endpoint-sum substitution is used in those bounds.

**Selector failure provenance: hardened.** Exact singularity of the shifted
reference stiffness is caught only at that certificate operation and reported
as `domain_failure`. Tests prohibit current construction in that case and
assert the reset/pre-read failure stage at zero and positive durations.
Adjacent exact cutoffs have positive certificate gaps at zero geometry gain;
this does not promise that every extremely close floating projector evaluation
will pass its separate numerical admission.

**Shared ownership and history coverage: PASS.** The audit and the preserved
original tests agree on old-Z read, one selected-current continuity, A-only W
update preserving old Z, one held-source Z update, and independent final
readmission. New reset pressure deliberately differs in C, W and Z; it tests
invalid reset resource/weight charts even when current state admits, at both
zero and positive duration. No independent C sector is stored. Reconstruction
remains a detached recipe with current/reset profile identity and declared tau;
full ledger authentication and live commit/rollback stay with lifecycle owners.

**Matched forcing and endpoint claims stay bounded.** The new paired-history
pressure keeps external data and base C/W equal, changes only old Z, and
observes different generated sources for both A and C. The carrier-difference
update includes `(1-a)*(S1-S2)` as well as `a*(Z1-Z2)`. A same-held-source
exponential estimate is therefore not a matched-future-forcing contraction
certificate. Neither that estimate, carrier invariance, nor reconstruction
establishes a nonzero committed base-state endpoint witness. No such scientific
claim or native formation provenance is promoted by c.
