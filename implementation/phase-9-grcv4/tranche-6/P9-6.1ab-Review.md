## Concrete admitted domain and algorithm

`CIBoundedDomain(radius).identity` encodes the exact positive binary64 radius
in `CISolverParams.contraction_domain_id`. The domain is the closed Frobenius
ball of symmetric H1 matrices about the profile's reference Hodge. Its centre,
norm and algorithm cannot be replaced by a prior root or caller certificate.
`ci_reduced_fixed_point_v1` explicitly selects an outer geometry fixed point
with direct elimination of the current block at each geometry. Both declared
iteration limits must agree. The joint residual uses
`joint_current_geometry_l2_v1`; existing current-block residual and physical
conditioning checks also apply. No fallback solver or warm-start history exists.

The current concrete C domain contains one certified fixed-rank stratum.
Both candidate implementations require at least one live edge; disconnected
graphs, parallel edges, isolated vertices and self-loops remain typed inputs.
An empty edge space is rejected by the inherited reference-Hodge declaration.
Strict-gap certification covers the entire ball, including its interior; the
root selector therefore accounts for the whole declared domain. This does
not implement arbitrary multi-stratum search. A domain touching an unresolved
cutoff, a broader stratum-union ID or a disconnected seed fails closed. Such a
failure is not a proof that the equations have no root elsewhere.

The following sufficient conditions are conservative. Failure to certify them
does not establish physical singularity, nonexistence, instability, or an
optimal domain radius. Numerical iteration and residual admission are separate
from this analytic existence/regularity argument.

### Bounds shared by A and C

Write r for the declared radius, m = min(diag(H_ref)) - r > 0 and
M = max(diag(H_ref)) + r. Then ||H^-1||_2 <= 1/m and ||H||_2 <= M.
Every bound uses rational arithmetic on the declared finite operands.
`sqrt(||A||_1 ||A||_infinity)` bounds a physical matrix's Euclidean operator
norm. Rational square-root upper bounds and outward exponential intervals
avoid floating equality decisions. Exponentials use a positive Taylor tail,
dyadic range reduction, outward rounding after each square and reciprocal
intervals for negative arguments; no mutable caller Decimal context participates.

Let R bound the Euclidean norm of the causal flat read and L_R its Lipschitz
constant with respect to the geometry Frobenius norm. Normalized star assembly
satisfies ||Star(r_flat)||_F <= ||r_flat||_2^2 and has derivative bound 2 R L_R:
each entry multiplier is at most one by Cauchy–Schwarz on the normalized star
restrictions. The geometry map consequently has displacement bound
|kappa_H zeta| R^2 and Lipschitz bound 2 |kappa_H zeta| R L_R. Admission requires
the former <= r and the latter < 1.

These same inequalities hold along the whole gain interval from zero to the
declared kappa_H, on the same ball and candidate inputs. Banach uniqueness
therefore supplies the connected reference branch. Current invertibility and
the reduced-map derivative bound make the joint root Jacobian regular through
its Schur complement. No global uniqueness or temporal stability follows.

### A-specific bounds

The reference baseline and its affine H derivative are computed from frozen
retained inputs, incidence, incoming C/W and the separately bound differential
recipe. Mobility products and differential linear solves are evaluated as exact
rationals before their binary64 output rounding; the residual enclosure uses
these same analytic operands. Per-edge baseline intervals bound the entire conductance exponent.
Each edge must remain strictly floor-active or floor-inactive throughout the
ball, or have a constant exponent. A possible moving floor crossing rejects.
Monotonicity of q=(W-W_hat)/(W+W_hat) gives exact endpoint intervals. The bound
|dq/d(exponent)| <= 1/2 includes the refreshed baseline's gamma J0 derivative.
Every denominator interval 1-zeta chi q must exclude zero; its minimum absolute
value and the maximum absolute value certify a physical diagonal condition
bound. Standard inverse/product derivative bounds then give R and L_R.

### C-specific bounds

For L_ref=B H_ref B^T and A=L_ref-Lambda I, exact inversion bounds the reference
distance from the cutoff below by 1/||A^-1||_2. Subtracting ||B^T B||_2 r gives
a strictly positive whole-ball cutoff gap. The separated-spectrum Sylvester
equation bounds the projector derivative; 2/gap is a conservative bound for
its off-diagonal blocks. This bounds T=P C, the tanh deformation D, and H_M=D H D.

The remaining bounds retain Q=H_M H^-2, Q^-1=H^2 H_M^-1 and
R_M=(I+tau B^T B H_M)^-1. Its retained SPD similarity bounds
||R_M||_2 <= sqrt(M_M/m_M). The physical response is Q^-1 R_M Q; its inverse
current bound uses 1-|zeta chi| ||Q^-1 R_M Q||_2 > 0. No retained-space
positivity margin is substituted for physical conditioning. At tau=0 the
physical response is exactly identity and its derivative vanishes. Product
and inverse derivative bounds give R and L_R without finite-difference guesses.

## Analytic residual acceptance — F1 correction

The audit correctly held both children: subtracting an already rounded
`H_profile` output can produce a zero residual for a different map from the one
certified above. Its scalar C witness has the exact constant map
`T(h)=2^100+3*2^46`. Binary64 instead fixes `h=2^100+2^48`, leaving the analytic
geometry defect `2^46`, despite the previous reported zero. The A companion
has `T(h)=2^100+3*kappa_H/(16*h^2)` and the same large-scale failure.
Both are now retained in the common CI test owner and reject at tolerance
`1e-11` with `no_admitted_root`. An analytic root may exist even when the
specified representation and iteration cannot provide an admissible approximation.

The v2 recipe `ci_analytic_residual_enclosure_binary64_v2` keeps the reduced
binary64 iteration. `CITrial.structural_source` retains its separate increment;
the diagnostic geometry defect is formed as `h-h_ref-kappa_H*increment`, before
final Hodge rounding. These numerical operand diagnostics **do not grant
acceptance**. Independent rational interval evaluation encloses the analytic
`F_J(J,h)` and `F_h(J,h)` at the represented trial pair. For intervals `[l_i,u_i]`,
`residual_squared` is the exact rational sum of `max(abs(l_i),abs(u_i))^2`.
Acceptance requires this upper bound to be at most the declared absolute joint
tolerance squared, with no scale factor, tolerance floor or relative substitute.
The original numerical recipe ID is rejected rather than reinterpreted.

The enclosure reconstructs A's unrounded differential linear systems, mobility,
baseline, real exponential, floor, contrast, causal read and structural inverse.
For C it reconstructs the analytic projector, selected field, real tanh and
exponential deformation, retained Hodge, potential/baseline and physical
`Q^-1 R Q` response. The star coefficient `count/sqrt(m_i*m_j)` is itself
enclosed; chi remains in the read and zeta outside its quadratic assembly.
Thus neither rounded candidate operators nor star/Hodge outputs are mistaken
for exact analytic ones. The domain proof and residual evaluator use the same
unrounded declared-input equations; the proof bounds do not claim to enclose
every rounded intermediate operator.

Exact point arithmetic stays rational. Nonpoint intervals round outward to a
160 significant binary digit grid, preventing denominator growth without
losing small or large exponents. Real exponentials reuse the exact Taylor-tail
bounds; tanh uses the monotone exponential identity and a real enclosure beyond
magnitude 512. Matrix inversion uses a rational midpoint inverse M and a
verified `q=||I-M A||_infinity<1`: the Neumann bound
`q*||M||_infinity/(1-q)` encloses each inverse entry's displacement. An unresolved
enclosure fails closed; it does not widen the user's tolerance.

For a nontrivial C selector, rounded numerical columns only suggest a subspace.
Pivoted rational Gram–Schmidt forms an exact orthoprojector E. Exact inertia
checks both compressed signs relative to the cutoff. Let
`S=E A E+(I-E) A (I-E)`, `g=1/upper_norm((S-Lambda I)^-1)`, and
`e>=||A-S||_F`. The verified condition `e<g` prevents cutoff crossings along the
segment from S to A. The two separated spectral Sylvester equations bound
`||P(A)-E||_F` by `2e/(g-e)` (a conservative bound), providing entry intervals.
The kernel/full/empty projectors use their exact formulas. A rank-correct but
wrong subspace fails the independent sign check. No claim rests on finite
sampling or on trusting the numerical eigensolver's output.

These are sufficient, conservative evaluation enclosures. Failure can mean
insufficient enclosure precision or an unattainable absolute tolerance, and
is not proof of analytic nonexistence. Broader C stratum unions remain outside
the declared domain. The accepted child scope remains separate from pending P9-6.1c reconciliation.

## Provisional transition and claim boundary

The selected analytic residual enclosure binds the solved current and actual trial Hodge.
Only that current enters common continuity. A then rebuilds final-C descriptors
and the existing drive law and executes its existing log writer once. New W
never enters the successful earlier root. Both candidates re-admit the entire
CI root/domain for final state; the reset prestate is independently admitted.
A step validates its writer declaration before any root, including at zero
duration. Reset readmission has stage `pre_read_reconstruction`; the consumed
root has `candidate_solve`; continuity, history write and final root readmission
retain their own failure stages. Safe-index overflow has an admission-stage
`ResourceBoundaryError`. Zero duration performs algebraic admission but preserves C, W, reset state,
time, step index and history without invoking continuity or the writer.

Outputs are provisional recipe objects. No live-owner mutation, authenticated
initializer provenance, snapshot/receipt lifecycle, CI G2, formation, spectrum,
global branch or stability is established. Exact C_OS support remains unchanged.
