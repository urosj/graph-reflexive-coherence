`l=min(diag H0)-r>0`, `u=max(diag H0)+r`, and
`dc=||B^T||*s*outer`, `dc_X=||B^T||*s`.

SPD and conditioning requirements. Sup changes in W have diagonal operator


`q=tanh((log W-exponent)/2)`, use
`q_X<=1/(2w-)+(|alpha|+|beta|contrast*contrast_X+|gamma|J0*J0_X)/2`

`J<=J0/a0`, `J_i<=J0_i/a0+J0|zeta*chi|q_i/a0^2`.

