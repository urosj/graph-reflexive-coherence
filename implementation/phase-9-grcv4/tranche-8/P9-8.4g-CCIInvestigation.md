# C_CI larger-graph investigation: exact-real feasibility, not native closure

Status: research result pending review. All four unchanged source/target ×
current/reset declarations have a unique exact-real reduced CI root in their
declared Frobenius ball. This does not yet certify represented roots, resource
positivity, native admission or P9-8.4h readiness.

## Why the C_OS result does not transfer

The [retained C_CI declaration](./larger-graph-examples/C_CI.json) has a cyclic
100/400 source and 107/407 target, radius r=2^-24, kappa_H=2^-16, and joint
residual tolerance 1e-8. The four original certificate probes timed out; they
did not reject these states. The independent tree17/tree23 CI proof excludes
these graphs and must not be silently extended. The production C certificate
also computes a shifted-stiffness inverse for its own whole-ball selector-gap
estimate; the accepted C_OS performance work did not replace that CI bound.

This investigation reuses the equations and graph hypotheses, not another
realization's accepted root or execution. No production code is changed.

## Exact-real whole-ball argument

Let B be the outward incidence, D=B^T B, H_ref=I, and
\(\|H-I\|_F\le r<1\), with H symmetric. Set l=1-r, u=1+r.
Then lI <= H <= uI. For a connected simple unweighted n-vertex graph,
the path/Poincare bound gives lambda_2(BB^T) >= 1/[n(n-1)]. Consequently

$$
0<\Lambda_C<\frac{1-r}{n(n-1)}
$$

keeps the strict selector exactly the constant-mode projector throughout
the ball, including its boundary. Its rank is one; its sector is mean(C).
Thus the exact-real retained Hodge is tH, where

$$
t=\exp\left(\kappa_{M,C}\tanh(\overline C/C_{ref})\right),
\qquad 0<t\le \exp(|\kappa_{M,C}|).
$$

With unit transport weights, beta=zeta_C chi_C in [0,1), s=1-beta,
and tau_C >= 0, the physical current reduces algebraically to

$$
j_0=-\eta_C\kappa_{\Phi,C}tDH B^TC,\qquad
v=(sI+\tau_C tHD)^{-1}j_0,\qquad
J=j_0+\beta v,\qquad f=\chi_C H^{-1}v.
$$

Similarity of HD to the symmetric positive-semidefinite H^(1/2) D H^(1/2)
gives the Euclidean inverse bound a=sqrt(u/l)/s. This is an exact-real
identity, **not permission to fuse the native rounded stages**.

Write d=||B^TC||_2 and b=2 maximum_degree >= ||D||_2. Use an outward upper
bound t_bar on t and define

$$
L_0=|\eta_C\kappa_{\Phi,C}|\,\bar t\,b d,\quad
V=aL_0u,\quad L_V=a(L_0+\tau_C\bar t\,bV),
$$

$$
F=|\chi_C|V/l,\qquad
L_F=|\chi_C|(L_V/l+V/l^2),\qquad L_J=L_0+\beta L_V.
$$

These bound respectively v, its H-Lipschitz constant, f, its H-Lipschitz
constant and J's H-Lipschitz constant, using Frobenius geometry distance and
Euclidean vector distance. The resolvent identity proves the Lipschitz bounds;
the constant selector makes t independent of H.

The unit exact-overlap star is K elementwise-multiplied by ff^T, with
K=|B|^T|B|/2, every entry between zero and one. Its Frobenius norm is at most
||f||_2^2; the difference bound is (||f||+||g||)||f-g||. Hence for

$$
T(H)=I+\kappa_H\zeta_C(K\circ ff^T),\quad
A=|\kappa_H\zeta_C|F^2,\quad
q=2|\kappa_H\zeta_C|FL_F,
$$

A <= r and q < 1 prove a self-map and contraction on the complete symmetric
Frobenius ball. No tree restriction or target-success selection is used.
The physical current block is also nonsingular and, when
beta sqrt(u/l)<1, has condition number at most
(1+beta sqrt(u/l))/(1-beta sqrt(u/l)). The retained declarations satisfy the
original conditioning limit without changing it.

For an exact eliminated-current pair (J(H),H), put e=||H-T(H)||_F.
Banach's residual bound gives

$$
\|H-H_*\|_F\le\frac{e}{1-q},\qquad
\|(J(H),H)-(J_*,H_*)\|_{joint}
\le\frac{\sqrt{1+L_J^2}}{1-q}\,e.
$$

This last bound **assumes an exact reconstructed current**. A rounded current
and geometry require an additional staged arithmetic/current-residual budget;
the native joint residual alone is not being substituted into this formula.

## Result and important limitation

The [FLINT/Arb checker](../verification/p984g_cci_investigation.py) retains
exact rational upper endpoints in [the result](./P9-8.4g-CCIInvestigationResults.json).
The following decimals are approximate summaries of those certified bounds.

| Subject | Geometry displacement upper A | Contraction upper q |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Source/current | 3.598e-14 | 1.439e-12 |
| Source/reset | 1.439e-13 | 5.756e-12 |
| Target/current | 1.420e-10 | 5.109e-9 |
| Target/reset | 1.420e-10 | 5.112e-9 |

All are well inside r=5.960464477539063e-8 and q<1. The target is not excluded
merely because the old independent proof was restricted to small trees.

However, **A is already below the declared joint residual tolerance 1e-8
in every case**. The exact-real reference pair (J(I),I) therefore meets the
residual threshold without incorporating generated geometry into another
current read. It need not be the exact fixed point. This predicts a possible
mechanism-discrimination weakness in the unchanged native first-pass recipe,
not a demonstrated native failure. Roundoff and native decisions have not been
measured here. Neither changing the tolerance nor adding an iteration silently
is justified by this result.
