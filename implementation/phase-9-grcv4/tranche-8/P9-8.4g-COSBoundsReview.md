## What was established

The [derivation](./P9-8.4g-COSBoundsPlan.md) replaces an inverse used only to
decide regularity with a positive-definiteness argument when
$1-\zeta_C\chi_C>0$. A small exact research kernel also handles the zero
case by incidence rank and the negative case by a vertex-space determinant.
Direct exact determinant pressure covers noncommuting SPD operands, cycles,
forests, disconnected/zero incidence, singularity and zero tau. Unsupported
SPD witnesses reject rather than being declared singular.

FLINT/Arb encloses the complete pointwise OS equations and separately the
implementation's staged linear/read/geometry arithmetic. In all four cases,
the corrector current resolves to unique binary64 values. Literal continuity
then includes the actual builtin summation, multiplication and subtraction.
This corrects a checker-development assumption that an exact dot rounded once
would represent the native differential; the final checker makes no such
assumption. A small cyclic native fixture checks both OS stages and Read-Back,
generated geometry and continuity, with tau zero and one.

## Limits and next action

No production source, parameter, tolerance, prior result or accepted support
was changed. No large native owner, event or step completed in this successor.
The host's actual scalar `tanh`/`exp` outputs are explicit dyadic operands in
the staged calculation; the exact-real calculation encloses transcendental
functions separately. A different runtime can verify retained rational
evidence; numerical replay also checks whether its scalar/staging outputs
agree, rather than silently substituting a different run.

These are pointwise OS-output bounds, not uniform parameter/trajectory bounds.
They do not prove final-C readmission, exact represented charge admission,
ten-step continuation, lifecycle rollback or other profile families.

A subsequent 900-second FLINT source/current diagnostic timed out before the
first predictor completed. The discarded regularity inverse completed in
52.4 seconds; the retained-resolvent inverse took 57.3 seconds and its
conditioning certificate took 362.0 seconds. The physical current-block
inverse was still running after at least 390.6 seconds. This incomplete probe
is a performance observation, not a mathematical rejection or native pass.
It shows that removing only the regularity inverse does not resolve the cost.

The proposed next work is a shared certified direct-solve/conditioning
component, together with the proved C regularity shortcut, preserving staged
rounding, singularity, conditioning and residual guards. A's already-diagonal
physical current remains separate; its structural Read-Back uses the shared
matrix API. No optimization is implemented or started in this checkpoint.
Then complete native admission on these same subjects. Only the remaining
prerequisite checks can authorize the larger P9-8.4h campaign.
