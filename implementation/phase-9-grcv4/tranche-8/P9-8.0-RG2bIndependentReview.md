returns. But returning the previous iterate with the new residual/error also
passed all five original tests. The larger section-displacement bound masked
the false query certificate while preserving the negative-core conclusion.

For $F_0=\operatorname{Id}+f(P\cdot,I)$, interval residual lower bounds give

$$
\|x-x_0\|\ge\frac{\|F_0(x)-X_+\|}{1+A_X}.
$$

The stale-return mutation reported errors around $4.46$–$6.69\times10^{-13}$,
but its proven error lower bounds were $3.85$–$5.79\times10^{-11}$, more than
86 times the reported errors and above the $2^{-36}$ query budget.

A history coordinates. The reported residual must enclose the recomputed
interval norm, and the error must enclose that norm divided by $1-A_X$ while
remaining strictly below the unchanged query budget. Four genuine queries are
checked against stale points, zero residuals, zero errors and a corrupted final
coordinate. The same substitutions are exercised through the actual evidence
consumer so removing its validation cannot leave only an unused helper test.
