# C_CI represented joint-root bridge and feedback discrimination

Research successor to the [exact-real ball investigation](./P9-8.4g-CCIInvestigation.md).
The [plan](./P9-8.4g-CCIStagedPlan.md) keeps all declarations, tolerances and
timesteps unchanged. No production code, native root, step or event is executed.

## The residual being tested

The production CI recipe uses an enclosure of the analytic equations
F_J and F_H at its represented pair, not just the rounded generated-H difference.
The investigation therefore computes these independently at (J_hat,I):

$$
R_H=(I+\tau_C tHD)^{-1},\quad
F_J=\widehat J-j_0(H)-\beta R_H\widehat J,
$$

$$
F_H=H-I-\kappa_H\zeta_C\bigl(K\circ ff^T\bigr),
\qquad f=\chi_C H^{-1}R_H\widehat J.
$$

Here t uses the **exact-real** exp/tanh of the exact declared resource mean,
enclosed by Arb. Native-style scalar exp/tanh outputs are separately captured
as represented operands for the staged read. No assumption that host libm
results are correctly rounded is used to replace the exact-real law.

F_J need not vanish when J_hat is a freshly reconstructed binary64 current.
Likewise F_H need not equal I minus the rounded generated matrix. Both
distinctions matter at these small couplings.

## Joint-root error bridge with current defect

Reuse only the earlier exact-real whole-ball bounds. Write l=1-r, u=1+r,
a_0=sqrt(u/l), q for the contraction constant, F for the uniform flat bound,
and L_J for the current's geometry-Lipschitz bound. For residual norm upper
bounds e_J and e_H, define

$$
\delta_J=\frac{e_J}{1-\beta a_0},\qquad
\delta_f=\frac{|\chi_C|a_0}{l}\delta_J,
$$

$$
\epsilon_T=|\kappa_H\zeta_C|(2F+\delta_f)\delta_f,
\qquad
E_H=\frac{e_H+\epsilon_T}{1-q},\qquad
E_J=\delta_J+L_JE_H.
$$

Then the represented pair's distance to the unique exact-real joint root is
at most sqrt(E_J^2+E_H^2). This is a conditional exact-real-root error bound
for the supplied represented pair, not a claim that rounded fixed-point maps
are contractions. The extra source defect epsilon_T must not be omitted.

## Feedback discrimination independently of stopping

Evaluate the exact first Picard image H1=T(I). The remaining geometry tail
from H1 to H_star is at most q ||H1-I||_F/(1-q), and the current tail is at
most L_J times that bound. Enclose J(H1), then widen by this current tail.
Subtract the exact reference read J(I): an interval excluding zero proves a
genuine exact-real CI current effect. This is stronger than comparing two
floating-point approximations or declaring a residual small.

Separately, a counterfactual **staged** read consumes the rounded generated
geometry. Its binary64 current is compared coordinate-by-coordinate with the
reference read. Count changed diagonals and surviving off-diagonals as well.
This is not an extra solver iteration granted to the unchanged native recipe.

The reference-current continuity output is only a proposal. Its sign does not
certify native charge admission, final-C reconstruction or a later trajectory.

All represented reference pairs satisfy the **independent analytic** residual
bound at the unchanged 1e-8 tolerance. Exact-real feedback is provably nonzero
in every case, but its source-current magnitude is below the whole represented
current-defect bound; source-reset has the same limitation. Counting changed
binary64 coordinates therefore does not, by itself, prove reliable physical
feedback discrimination. At the target the approximately 3.214e-13 maximal
exact feedback effect exceeds the approximately 4.14e-15 reference-current
defect bound, though no uniform all-coordinate effect margin is claimed.

Source geometry has zero changed diagonals and 5,602 nonzero off-diagonals;
target geometry has 46 changed diagonals and 5,598 nonzero off-diagonals.
The independent residual admits a reference pair, but we have **not** observed
the native solver selecting it. The stopping implementation may use different,
more conservative enclosures and must be checked separately.

### Target continuation is excluded, not a rounding ambiguity

Both target roles have a negative first-step proposal. This prompted a separate
[exact-root continuity bound](./P9-8.4g-CCIContinuationBounds.json), computed by
[`p984g_cci_continuation.py`](../verification/p984g_cci_continuation.py), without
repeating the interval solves. If E bounds ||J_hat-J_star||_2, each exact-real
poststate coordinate lies in

$$
C_i-dt\,(B\widehat J)_i\;\pm\;dt\,\|B_i\|_2 E.
$$

The center is computed from exact dyadic operands, **not** from the rounded
continuity output. At target vertex index 100 (the retained event's
`extra/1/1` node), the enclosures are approximately:

- current: [-9.99235e-15, -9.52772e-15];
- reset: [-9.99313e-15, -9.52847e-15].

Both upper endpoints are strictly negative. The counterfactual feedback reads
also give negative proposals, approximately -9.7564e-15 and -9.7572e-15.
Consequently neither early stopping nor floating-point uncertainty accounts
for this entire failure: even the unique exact-real root exits the nonnegative
resource domain on the declared first step. This is an independent mathematical
exclusion, **not a fabricated native rejection receipt**.

That vertex starts at exactly zero resource and has certified positive outward
divergence. At this fixed state the exact-real sign fails for **every positive
timestep**; reducing dt merely scales it. This is not repaired by improving the
root solver. It also does not prove that C_CI has no admissible larger-graph
configuration, or that root admission itself fails.

Next derive the zero-resource target constraints (B J_star)_i <= 0 before
selecting any separately declared source/allocation companion. Preserve these
original exclusions and the fresh-trigger/transfer obligations. Do not clip,
inject uncontracted resource, retune tolerances, or claim positive target
continuation from a small root residual. Native certificate/performance work
remains separate; this configuration cannot be credited as a successful `.h`
continuation witness.

Four source/target × current/reset records are produced by
[`p984g_cci_staged.py`](../verification/p984g_cci_staged.py), using fixed
192-bit FLINT/Arb research enclosures, no approximate matrix solves or fallback.
Each worker is capped at 600 seconds. Full matrices are replaced by vectors,
exact bound endpoints and geometry digests. The [result summary](./P9-8.4g-CCIStagedResults.json)
binds case bytes.

Nine focused scientific/retained tests cover current-defect propagation,
uncertain rounding, small cyclic full-matrix identities, wrong-stage/scope
mutations, zero-gain/read-back controls, tolerance boundaries and exact nominal
continuity signs. A pilot completed the mathematics but failed JSON serialization
of NumPy counters; the counters were converted to ordinary integers and a
serialization regression added before rerunning the pilot. No law, numerical
tolerance or recorded successful case was changed to obtain completion.

`check` validates source bindings, recorded scope, regenerated staged geometry,
literal continuity, current-defect bridge and Picard-tail arithmetic; it does
not rerun interval solves. `run-case --graph source|target --role current|reset`
requires single-threaded settings and an absent destination. A rerun is new
execution evidence, never a silent replacement for the retained run.

Native whole-domain certificate computation and the native analytic residual
implementation have not been timed or compared here. An independently proved
admissible represented pair does not establish that native CI has selected it.
P9-8.4g C_CI and P9-8.4h readiness remain open until that bridge and poststate
admission are established. No all-family, uniform trajectory or ATC claim follows.
