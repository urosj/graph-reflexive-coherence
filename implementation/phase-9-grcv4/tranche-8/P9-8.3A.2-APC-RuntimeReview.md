## Whole-chart proof and numeric ownership

`PCEnvelopeCertificate` now accepts the closed fixed-row A_PC descriptor in
addition to its existing WLS descriptor. Each row is a positive-weight convex
average of resource differences. For `||C||2 <= M`, each difference is at most
`sqrt(2) M`, so the three-component descriptor has norm at most `sqrt(6) M`.
Empty rows and loops contribute zero. This bound is uniform over all positive
incoming W; it uses neither sampled gradients nor a frozen reference W.
The implementation computes an outward square-root bound with `ExactScalar`.
The independent A.1 proof uses rational/interval equations.

The explicitly accepted native chart is R=2048, kappa_H=2^-14, M=16 and
W in [1/2,513/512]. Its Hodge eigenvalue enclosure is [7/8,9/8]. The Candidate A
contrast has magnitude below one, yielding the current inverse margin 31/32
and conditioning upper bound 33/31 throughout the chart. The existing exact
PC norm, source, conductance-exponent and geometry-image checks then give:

| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Source, either role | 1424.7900160867441 | 2048 |

These displayed decimals summarize the retained exact upper bounds. Both
current/reset charts satisfy the source-envelope criterion. The scalar-ZOH
convex combination therefore remains in the carrier ball while the base
operands remain admitted. This does not establish indefinite C/W invariance,
