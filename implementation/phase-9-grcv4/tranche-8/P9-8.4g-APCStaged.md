## What was executed

Four prepared source/target x current/reset histories, each with one independent
represented read, continuity, post-C W writer, held-source Z writer and fresh
final C/W/Z read. Each case took about ten seconds. Computation used FLINT
rationals and fixed 256-bit Arb LU/enclosures, one thread, unique binary64
rounding or explicit failure, no precision escalation or exact-inverse fallback.
No large native owner, native step, event or admission receipt was executed.

The declared represented conductance uses host `math.exp` after exponent
rounding, matching the production recipe; it is not asserted to be correctly
rounded transcendental arithmetic. Independent Arb comparison bounds its
contribution to the fixed-read error. Unique-rounding certificates apply to
the flat solve and real writer interpolations, not silently to every primitive.

The fixed-stage A arithmetic is reused from the independent A_CI oracle;
**no CI root theorem or iteration is used**. PC's input geometry is derived
from its old carrier. The final geometry uses its actual newly written,
non-diagonal carrier, not the old diagonal simplification.

The represented pipeline is:

$$
H=\operatorname{RN}(I+\operatorname{RN}(\kappa_H Z)),\quad
J=\operatorname{RN}\!\left(\frac{J_0}{1-\zeta_A\chi_A q}\right),\quad
f=H^{-1}\operatorname{RN}(\chi_A qJ).
$$

The flat solve has certified rounding and a residual check. The held structural
source uses the star overlap coefficient and the declared two-rounding order.
Continuity retains graph-edge summation order and binary64 multiply/subtract.
W uses **post-continuity C, incoming W, selected J**; Z holds the **incoming
read's source**, not the final read's source:

$$
W'=\exp(a_A\log W+(1-a_A)\log W_{\rm drv}),\qquad
Z'=a_{PC}Z+(1-a_{PC})S.
$$

Each writer's real interpolation is enclosed and uniquely rounded. Fresh final
current, Read-Back, flat and structural source are then independently evaluated
from C'/W'/Z'. Resource/history/carrier chart, Hodge conditioning and represented
solve/closure residuals pass. Positive resource values are not clipped.

Current errors compare the represented chain with the full analytic fixed-read
equations at the **same stored C/W/Z**, including affine-H rounding. These are
not a whole-trajectory or end-to-end exact-state error enclosure. Writer rounding
errors separately compare against real interpolation of their **represented**
inputs: W error <1.104e-16, source Z error <1.289e-20, target Z error <4.263e-32.
They do not include all upstream input errors.

Source carrier effects on current are resolved: about 5.68e-14 / 1.14e-13 at
the initial read and 5.79e-14 / 1.16e-13 at the final read, above the selected
coordinate errors. Read-Back effects are resolved in all four cases at both
reads (source scale 3.38e-8–6.91e-8, target scale 1.27e-6–1.29e-6).

**Target first-step carrier-to-current response is not resolved.** Target Z
starts at zero under the existing explicit loss policy; the first write creates
nonzero off-diagonal history. Its final exact-real carrier effect is about
1.88e-23–2.59e-23, below represented current error. A stale-Z final read gives
identical represented target currents. Thus stored carrier creation and full
final reconstruction pass, but this fixture does **not** demonstrate a resolved
first-step target carrier feedback effect. No tolerance relaxation or silent
substitution is made. The small discriminating control detects stale-Z reading.

Exact stored-resource charge drifts are retained, not labeled exact conservation:
source current −2^-51, source reset −3*2^-52; target current
13575/2^64, target reset −2857/2^61. Native charge admission remains untested.

These are the four **prepared** declarations. The event-current target built
from actual represented source postbeat data still belongs to 8.4h. Reset-as-
current diagnostics do not evolve the stored reset in a live event.

## Pressure and continuation

Four focused tests pass. A three-node control matches the production PC step
exactly for initial/final point fields, structural source, C, W and Z. It also
distinguishes stale-C writing, fresh-source carrier writing and stale-Z final
reading. Writer tests include zero duration, signed Z, exact unchanged endpoints
and native scalar parity. Ambiguous rounding, an inadmissible Hodge, duplicate
sparse coordinates and forged scope/stage/output records reject.

Lossless sparse matrices and complete vectors occupy about 304 KB XZ (1.72 MB
decoded). `check` authenticates retained records without dense solves;
`recompute` deliberately repeats the oracle. The side-tool exposes the source
successes **and** unresolved target response. Original evidence is not rebound.
