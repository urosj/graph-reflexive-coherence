and one BLAS/OpenMP thread each. A matrix-operation cache is local to each
step/read; reuse never substitutes for fresh residual or admission checks.

Already completed steps remain resumable; never rerun or overwrite them.

Each successful step retains stage vectors/geometry digests, chronology/charge,
the complete authoritative poststate and predecessor identity in compact XZ.

if unexpectedly exceeded, inspect the complete output before adding more runs,
