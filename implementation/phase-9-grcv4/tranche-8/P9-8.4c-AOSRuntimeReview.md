## Evidence storage and checking levels

The retained captures are needed by the independent interval and actual-consumer
checkers without rerunning the native trajectory. The source is shared once;
exact repeated references and geometry matrices are interned by content digest.
Expansion verifies each context, rejects unused/unknown contexts and round-trips
the canonical captures. JSON key order and equivalent integral-number spelling
are not scientific identity; deduplication uses the canonical scientific digest.
No numerical rounding, precision reduction or scientific field pruning is used.
D45 observations stay in their accepted predecessor.
The 64 MB completed-evidence budget applies before byte-exact XZ storage.

An initial preparation was stopped before completing any case to correct the
storage design: C_OS-style reference-only sharing did not remove repeated A_OS
geometry matrices. No incomplete dump is retained. The final prepared manifest
pins the corrected exact matrix-sharing implementation before execution.

`--run` alone executes a new native campaign and refuses overwrite. `--check-retained`
reconstructs targets, compares saved outputs and checks consumed stages and
certificate bindings; it does not rerun native trajectories or interval equations.
`--recheck-numerics` additionally recomputes the independent interval equations
at saved native operands, still without a native rerun. Side-tool normal status
checks pinned retained identities/rosters, not those numerical equations.
The predecessor checker is reused through an explicit oracle-data adapter;
no current, solver, capture or scientific comparison is mocked by that adapter.
