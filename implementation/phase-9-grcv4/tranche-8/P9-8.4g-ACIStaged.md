## What is checked

Four source/target × current/reset controls. Each has an independently staged
reference read and a confirming read at its generated geometry. Native fixed-row
CI requires **two consecutive** analytic joint residual passes; a reference
residual pass alone is not its stopping rule. The oracle checks the whole
current/geometry pair and binds its digest to graph, profile, C and W.

Exact rational potential accumulation, rounded potential/baseline, rounded
fixed-row descriptors, rounded exponent then host `math.exp`, exact diagonal
current solve and rounded read flux are reconstructed independently. Host exp
is not assumed correctly rounded: its actual binary64 output is compared to
the unrounded exponential by the subsequent analytic residual. Structural
lowering uses a fixed 192-bit **rigorous Arb LU single-RHS solve**, with unique
binary64 rounding required. No approximate solver, retry or exact-inverse
fallback. Structural source and H generation preserve the declared rounding
boundaries. Small cyclic cases compare these primitives against production
without constructing a native root or taking a native step.

## Full-joint error bridge

At a represented pair $(\widehat J,\widehat H)$, independently enclose the
analytic residuals $F_J$ and $F_H$. Let $m>0$ be the whole-ball diagonal block
margin, $r<1$ its radius, $F$ the flat-current bound and $\lambda<1$ its
contraction bound. Positive W and G imply $|q_e|<1$. Set

$$
\delta_J=\|F_J\|_2/m,\qquad
\delta_f=|\chi_A|\delta_J/(1-r),\qquad
\delta_S=|\kappa_H\zeta_A|(2F+\delta_f)\delta_f.
$$

Then

$$
\epsilon_H=\frac{\|F_H\|_F+\delta_S}{1-\lambda},\qquad
\epsilon_J=\delta_J+L_J\epsilon_H.
$$

The current defect must be corrected **before** using the reduced geometry
contraction theorem. Neither a small geometry residual alone nor equal rounded
currents establishes a full joint root. Both represented H and its generated
successor belong to the declared reference ball in this calculation.

## Feedback and continuation interpretation

All four independent two-trial calculations pass. Approximate displays below
summarize the exact rational/interval endpoints in the compressed record:

| Prepared case | Confirming joint residual upper | Feedback-separated coordinates | Changed H diagonals / off-diagonals | First-step resource lower |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Source current | 4.20e-17 | 116 | 0 / 1456 | 1.98410 |
| Source reset | 7.76e-17 | 127 | 0 / 1440 | 1.96820 |
| Target current | 1.55e-15 | 176 | 9 / 1550 | 1.83016e-4 |
| Target reset | 1.42e-15 | 177 | 9 / 1534 | 1.83016e-4 |

Here "feedback-separated" means the lower bound on an exact root's current
change from its exact reference read exceeds the upper bound on that
coordinate's represented error. For example, source-current edge 300 has
effect at least 1.66e-21 versus error at most 2.78e-33. This does not establish
all-coordinate separation: its largest-effect coordinate remains below its
own represented error. Both source diagonal changes disappear in binary64;
off-diagonal feedback survives. These limitations are retained, not replaced
by the passing residual (whose declared tolerance is 1e-8).

The four calculations took about 30 seconds total with single-threaded FLINT /
192-bit Arb. This is research-oracle cost, not a native runtime benchmark.

A separate exact-real first Picard image $H_1=T(I)$ yields
$\|H^*-H_1\|_F\le\lambda\|H_1-I\|_F/(1-\lambda)$. Propagating that
tail through $L_J$ encloses the exact root current. Differences from the exact
reference read measure geometry feedback, independently of represented equality.
The evidence separately reports the largest effect, a coordinate selected by
effect/error separation, changed rounded current entries, erased diagonals and
surviving off-diagonals. A changed binary64 entry is not by itself a certified
scientific effect; a largest effect below its coordinate error remains a limit
even if another small-current coordinate separates.

Literal represented continuity is compared against exact-root resource boxes.
This is first-step resource evidence, not the history writer/final-C admission,
represented charge closure, fresh source trigger or a lifecycle event. These
prepared targets are prebeat role controls; `.h` must use actual postbeat
current transfer with unchanged reset. Feasibility's interval chronology proof
does not silently turn them into that native event.
