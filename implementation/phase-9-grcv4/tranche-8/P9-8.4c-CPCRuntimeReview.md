## Scientific scope

The [manifest](./P9-8.4c-CPCCases.json) binds D36/37/38/43/44/45, both
chiralities and the applicable phases. It inherits the accepted C_PC R=1,
kappa_H=2^-16, zeta_C=2^-30 declaration and unchanged comparison ceilings from
[P9-8.4b](./P9-8.4b-CPCRuntimeReview.md#scoped-user-acceptance). The source beat
and both D45 executions are exact predecessor reuse; each new event reads the
source afresh. No production code, share recipe, numerical parameter, tolerance
or scientific authority is changed.

Before native execution, independent target construction checks the port graph,
complete C references, exact charge transport and zero whole-target carriers.
The connected-tree spectral lower bound verifies the constant retained sector
over the declared carrier ball, rather than assuming the selector remains
valid at its center. Independent dense/Decimal predictions check ten-step
resource positivity for both roles on every layout. These predictions are not
a native trajectory or a uniform continuation theorem.

Native admission and saved-operand checks cover both-role source/target reads,
old-publication visibility until admission, the complete current/reset carrier
archive, source edge order, explicit carrier-loss receipts and deterministic
replay. Candidate C is rederived without W history. Target carriers start at
zero; ordinary steps then read the old carrier, compute one structural source,
apply one held-source exponential write, and reconstruct a fresh restart.
The final read must match the final restart, while detached continuation leaves
the event owner's publication unchanged.

The independent checker evaluates full-formula current, baseline, geometry,
structural source, resource and carrier errors at actual represented entries.
It separately certifies signed Read-Back and lowered-read vectors: a global
sign reversal can leave a structural outer product unchanged. Read-path
geometry, feedback and modulation controls remain distinct from an endpoint
hysteresis experiment. Neither pointwise certificates nor nominal comparisons
establish accumulated trajectory-error bounds, a uniform parameter tube,
arbitrary-graph support or general ATC.
