## Mathematical review and pressure

The review uses the [paper](../../investigations/grc9v4-constitutive-design/drafts/2026-09-GRC-V4.md)
Appendix A's fixed rows, D.2's A potential/current/Read-Back/source/log writer,
OS realization, and §§12.7 / E.10's event lifecycle, together with the
[GRC9 specification](../../../specs/grc-9-v4-spec.md),
[D11-G9 resolution](../../investigations/grc9v4-constitutive-design/decisions/D11G9CanonicalExpansionPortAllocationResolution.md)
and [accepted A companion](./P9-8.0-AEventConstruction.md).
The AEC-F1 ownership correction remains in force: entry operands are saved
before invoking a producer; output can never redefine its own input truth.

`PaperAOS` directly evaluates the displayed equations with its own mpmath
context. No production candidate, realization, writer, differential evaluator
or expansion planner produces any expected value. A test disables all those
production entry points while rebuilding the complete oracle. Identity and
typed graph/reference validation reuse the admitted codec and structural
owners; those are not numerical oracles.

The separate `IntervalRows` checker bounds complete formula errors, including
nonlinear conductance, both OS current reads, generated geometry and the log
writer. Each point comparison uses its saved C/W. Resource and current errors
must be below `2^-40`; W and represented Hodge-entry errors below `2^-48`.
Both the predicted and regenerated Hodge matrices are checked against their
own formula enclosures before checking the exact represented split inequality.
Regularity exceeds 1/2 and generated-geometry norm remains below 1/2 at the
checked points. These are pointwise expectation/error certificates. They are
not a recursive enclosure of all possible native trajectories or proof of
arbitrary continuation.

Both roles remain positive after each of ten target steps. Observed minimum C
is approximately `1.06390e-5` for current and `1.22070e-4` for reset; maximum
C stays below 3.165 and all W stay in `(0.999,1.001)`. Final reads use each
role's own actual oracle step-ten state. The independent postbeat row expectation also identifies exactly `source-s` as
the saturated candidate; the accepted row bridge and predicate match that
expectation using committed W. The 25 positive numerical comparisons
represent 21 physical-step expectations and four read-only probes; discarded
writer results in a read probe are not extra physical evolution.

Pressure checks rehashed source/target/policy/reference/receipt substitutions;
old-W swaps in each role; stale source reference current; C/W/current/baseline
and either Hodge matrix corrupted by `2^-20`; missing exact G2/G3; and WLS
substitution. Six source/target edge-order/reorientation cases check equation
covariance without claiming native runtime covariance. Geometry and Read-Back
controls at both source and first target, fresh-C versus stale-C and selected-J
versus baseline-J writers, and next-read new-W versus held-W consumption are
compared against `4*(both full formula errors)+8*maximum output ULP`.
A blanket writer-error budget was too coarse to certify one small effect;
the final test uses actual independently enclosed formula errors with the
same strict full-error/ULP gate. No physics parameter or admission tolerance
was loosened to pass that check.

The negative fixture replaces one role's old W with 0.99 while retaining the
small-gradient source resources. Source OS readmission passes. Target currents
remain regular and hypothetical C/W updates positive, but an interval-enclosed
entry of the target split defect gives spectral-norm lower bound
`>2.86447e-12`, above tolerance `2^-40 ~= 9.09495e-13`. The exact represented
PSD split test also rejects. An upper bound merely exceeding tolerance would
not establish this failure. Both current-only and reset-only placements are
A.2 obligations. The negative is stored in normalized role coordinates;
A.2 must derive fresh source/policy/event/target identities for each placement.
This record does not claim a native rejection or rollback was executed.

Five source-bound side-tool queries are retained with source pointers, edge
references, support dispositions and trace digests in the oracle record.
