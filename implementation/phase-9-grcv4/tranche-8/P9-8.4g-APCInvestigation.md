## Independent mathematics and staging

Use outward-tail incidence B, fixed incoming W and diagonal incoming Z. Then
H=I+kappa_H Z, d=B^T C and the fixed-row descriptors are weighted means of
neighbor resource differences. The closed A law is

$$
j_0=-\eta\operatorname{diag}(W)B^TB
 [\kappa_c\operatorname{diag}(W)+\kappa_{Ah}(H-I)]d,\quad
G_e=\max\{W_{\rm floor},\exp[-(\alpha(C_u+C_v)+\beta\|g_u-g_v\|^2+\gamma j_{0,e}^2)/2]\},
$$

$$
q_e=\frac{W_e-G_e}{W_e+G_e},\qquad
J_e=\frac{j_{0,e}}{1-\zeta_A\chi_Aq_e},\quad
f_e=\frac{\chi_Aq_eJ_e}{H_{ee}},\qquad
S=\zeta_A[K\circ ff^T].
$$

For these simple loop-free graphs, K is 1 on the diagonal, 1/2 on pairs sharing
an endpoint and zero elsewhere. This is an **old-carrier PC read**, not a CI
simultaneous root or an OS predictor/corrector. It needs no large inverse.

The independent whole-chart proof uses r=|kappa_H|R, H lower bound 1−r,
|q_e|≤1, current margin m=1−|zeta_A chi_A| and the induced-norm bound
sqrt(||A||_1 ||A||_infinity) for B^T and B^TB. With resource radius M and W upper w:

$$
J_{0,\max}=\eta w\|B^TB\|\bigl(|\kappa_c|w+|\kappa_{Ah}|r\bigr)\|B^T\|M,
\quad J_{\max}=J_{0,\max}/m,\quad
\|S\|_F\le |\zeta_A|\left(\frac{|\chi_A|J_{\max}}{1-r}\right)^2\le R.
$$

Positive-weight fixed-row gradients obey ||g||≤sqrt(6)M, supplying the finite
conductance bound over the **entire** chart, not merely these four samples.
Carrier invariance is conditional on the resource/history chart remaining
admitted; it is not a proof of resource/history invariance.

Continuity is C'=C−dt BJ. The A history target uses C', descriptors rebuilt with
C' and incoming W, and the **same selected J**, not j0 or a poststate re-read.
The log-history update is W'=exp(a_A log W+(1−a_A)log G(C',W,J)). The carrier
writer separately holds S from the incoming read:
Z'=a_PC Z+(1−a_PC)S, with a_X=exp(−dt/tau_X). Triangle/Frobenius bounds certify
its norm; no clipping, projection or hidden target read is used.

All inputs are exact binary64 rationals; exponentials and square roots use
192-bit outward Arb bounds. Shared code supplies fixed-row assembly and scalar
interval operations only. No production current/certificate/writer/step or CI

## Mechanism and next feasibility work

Removing source carrier changes exact-real currents by at least approximately
5.68e-14 / 1.14e-13 for current/reset. Removing readback changes them by about
3.38e-8 / 6.76e-8; target readback differences are about 1.27e-6. Target Z is
zero, so zero-carrier comparison is algebraically identical (a small interval
subtraction width is not a physical effect). These are exact-real differences,
not yet certified visible effects in the production represented arithmetic.

For zero-carrier targets the leading resource rate is eta L_W^2 C, where
L_W=B diag(W) B^T. The result retains its exact affine coefficients in the
three satellite shares for all five initially empty target nodes, separately
for both histories. For `extra/1/1`, the baseline rate divided by eta is

$$
\text{current: }\frac{196609-393217s_1}{32768},\qquad
\text{reset: }\frac{196607-393215s_1}{32768}.
$$

The frozen s1=1/2 straddles the two zero-rate thresholds. The actual nonlinear
readback corrections are enclosed at that original state and preserve the
opposite signs. These local correction bounds must **not** be reused uniformly
over the allocation simplex. Next derive a both-role interior allocation using
all five inequalities, certify full-law continuation at the selected companion,
then check fresh source-trigger and current-postbeat/unchanged-reset transport.
Any new event identity/profile bindings must be regenerated explicitly.
Only after that comes the represented two-writer/final-read oracle and bounded
native conformance. No adoption of another realization's passing shares follows.
