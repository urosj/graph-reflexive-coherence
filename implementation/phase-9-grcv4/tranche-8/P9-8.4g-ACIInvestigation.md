## Independent equations and sufficient bounds

Use outward-tail incidence $B$, $D=B^TB$, $d=B^TC$ and $H_0=I$.
Fixed-row descriptors are computed exactly from incoming $W$: each node/port-row
is the weighted mean of neighboring resource differences (empty row is zero).
No production root, current, interval inverse or descriptor evaluator is used
by the certificate. A separate test compares descriptors and backend identities
with the public fixed-row implementation.

$$
j_0(H)=-\eta\,\operatorname{diag}(W)D
  [\kappa_c\operatorname{diag}(W)+\kappa_{Ah}(H-I)]d,
\qquad
G_e(H)=\exp[-(\alpha(C_u+C_v)+\beta\|g_u-g_v\|^2+\gamma j_{0,e}(H)^2)/2].
$$

The whole ball is proved to stay strictly above the conductance floor. Define
$q_e=(W_e-G_e)/(W_e+G_e)$ and $b=\zeta_A\chi_A$:

$$
J_e(H)=\frac{j_{0,e}(H)}{1-bq_e(H)},\qquad
f(H)=\chi_A H^{-1}\operatorname{diag}(q(H))J(H),\qquad
T(H)=I+\kappa_H\zeta_A[K\circ f(H)f(H)^T].
$$

For the admitted simple graph, $K_{ee}=1$, $K_{ef}=1/2$ for distinct edges
sharing an endpoint, otherwise zero; hence the Hadamard map is Frobenius
nonexpansive. This is the CI simultaneous equation, **not an OS substitution**.

For radius $r$, set
$\ell_e=|\eta W_e\kappa_{Ah}|\|D_{e,:}\|_2\|d\|_2$.
Then $j_{0,e}$ lies in $j_{0,e}(I)\pm\ell_e r$. Outward exponential bounds
give floor separation, $q_{\max}$, a positive diagonal block margin $m$ and
conditioning upper bound. Since
$|dq_e/d\log G_e|\le1/2$,
$L_q=\max_e |\gamma|\sup|j_{0,e}|\ell_e/2$ suffices. With
$j=\|\sup|j_0|\|_2/m$:

$$
L_J=\frac{\|\ell\|_2}{m}
 +\frac{\|\sup|j_0|\|_2|b|L_q}{m^2},\quad
F=\frac{|\chi_A|q_{\max}j}{1-r},\quad
L_f=|\chi_A|\left[\frac{q_{\max}j}{(1-r)^2}
 +\frac{L_qj+q_{\max}L_J}{1-r}\right].
$$

Writing $a=|\kappa_H\zeta_A|$, the self-map bound is $aF^2\le r$;
the contraction bound is $\lambda=2aFL_f<1$. Completeness of the closed
symmetric Frobenius ball and its SPD containment give a unique fixed point
there; diagonal block invertibility supplies its unique associated current.
No global uniqueness is claimed.

At $I$ the current block is diagonal and flattening is the identity. Thus the
outward bound $s=a\|f(I)\|_2^2$ requires no large inverse. Banach gives
$\|H^*-I\|_F\le\min(aF^2,s/(1-\lambda))=:\epsilon_H$ and
$\|J^*-J(I)\|_2\le L_J\epsilon_H=: \epsilon_J$.
Each continuity coordinate is enclosed around $C_i-dt(BJ(I))_i$ with radius
$dt\|B_{i,:}\|_2\epsilon_J$, plus Arb's outward reference-evaluation error.
Exact binary64 input values are interpreted as rationals; 192-bit Arb bounds
enclose exponential and square-root evaluations. There is no precision retry,
approximate inverse, clipping, or fallback.
