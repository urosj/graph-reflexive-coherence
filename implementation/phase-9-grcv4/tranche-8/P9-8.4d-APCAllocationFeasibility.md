# P9-8.4d A_PC D94 allocation feasibility

**Allocation alone cannot repair the frozen D94 A_PC fixture.** For each of
the six declared D94 layouts, no tuple in the entire closed simplex can give
two nonnegative current-history updates under the fixed source, transferred
W history, zero target carrier and existing law. This excludes the required
both-role ten-step continuation for that source. It does not exclude other
sources, other depths, A_PC generally, or the mechanical expansion contract.

The [certificate](./P9-8.4d-APCAllocationFeasibility.json) supplies an exact-real
exclusion and a separate exclusion allowing the existing full-formula numerical
error budgets. The latter is conditional on those budgets being met; it is
not a new native execution or an all-implementation rounding theorem. The
earlier [failed pilot and balanced control](./P9-8.4d-APCRuntimeReview.md) remain
unchanged. This result is pending review; no native history cells are closed.

## Why the allocation constraints conflict

Write the shares as $(x,y,1-x-y)$ with $x,y\geq0$ and $x+y\leq1$.
For the D94 chirality −1, phase-three layout, the first update at the first
extra node of each shorter branch imposes necessary upper bounds on $x$ and
$y$. The second update at `extra/3/04` imposes an incompatible lower bound
on their sum. With the represented-error allowances included, the relaxed
necessary bounds are approximately

$$
x+y\leq0.723838292874,
\qquad x+y\geq0.738496123418.
$$

Thus a balanced tuple can fix the first update without fixing the second.
The terminal second-step resource upper bound is at most about
$-2.83369\times10^{-8}$ across the six layouts after allowing the errors.
The stored rational bounds, not these rounded display numbers, decide the
contradiction. The current history alone suffices: a shared allocation cannot
satisfy both histories if it cannot satisfy current.

## Full enabled equations and certificate construction

Let $B$ be the fixed oriented incidence matrix, $W_0$ the transferred positive
edge weights, $L=B\operatorname{diag}(W_0)B^\mathsf{T}$, and
$M=I+\Delta t L^2$. The positive sign follows the accepted baseline current
$J_0=-\operatorname{diag}(W_0)B^\mathsf{T}LC$ and continuity $C'=C-\Delta t BJ$.
The target resource vector $C_0(x,y)$ is affine in the shares, so $MC_0$ and
$M^2C_0$ have exact rational coefficients.

The checker does **not** substitute this frozen-W linear system for A_PC.
It encloses the full enabled corrections with outward interval arithmetic:

$$
C_1=MC_0+E_1,\qquad C_2=M^2C_0+E_2.
$$

The corrections include the constitutive drive, Read-Back denominator,
fresh-resource logarithmic W writer, held-source Z writer, and the geometry
$H_1=I+\kappa_H Z_1$ consumed at the second read. In particular, the exact
second baseline is decomposed into the frozen linear baseline plus the
fresh-W and generated-geometry differences; those differences are enclosed,
not discarded. The source at the first read uses $H_0=I$ because the accepted
target policy sets Z to zero.

Intervals cover the entire share simplex, including its edges and vertices.
When enclosing the second step, negative lower endpoints of $C_1$ are
intersected with zero **only as a necessary first-admission condition**.
Trajectories with genuinely negative $C_1$ are already excluded. This is not
resource clipping in the law or a repair of any trajectory.

In the represented variant, this nonnegative intersection applies to the
represented next state. The exact continuity reference used by the W-error
certificate is separately allowed to be negative by the resource error bound.
Its full writer formula is enclosed on that extended interval, with the
inactive floor still certified. It is not silently replaced by the clipped
represented state.

For each coordinate, $C_{k,i}\geq0$ implies the affine necessary inequality
$(M^kC_0)_i+\sup E_{k,i}\geq0$. Exact rational polygon clipping applies these
halfplanes to the closed simplex. The resulting feasible polygon is empty by
the second update. The record retains the coefficients, correction intervals,
first-step polygon, terminal node and strictly negative terminal bound for
each layout. This is a continuous-domain exclusion, not a grid search.

Changing the simplex tuple changes event identity but not the declared
role-labelled port layout. Shares enter only the three satellite resources;
the proof keeps the graph, external resources, W transfer, new-edge seeds and
zero-carrier policy fixed, identifying corresponding role labels.

## Represented arithmetic and pressure

The represented variant allows $2^{-48}$ absolute initial resource error on
each satellite. This safely encloses correctly rounded binary64 multiplication
of a simplex share by the frozen source resource, which is less than four.
Unchanged coordinates and transferred W remain exact inputs. It then permits
the existing per-step full-formula bounds: resource and carrier error
$2^{-40}$, and W-history error $2^{-48}$. These are enclosure allowances,
not changes to admission tolerances. Error propagation includes both the W/Z
writers and the second current consumer. The exclusion survives them.

Focused pressure compares the decomposition with a separate literal full-matrix
interval evaluation at interior points and simplex vertices. It rejects a
frozen-W alternative, checks exact rational halfplane replay and rehashed
source/role/coefficient/roster/scope/enclosure mutations, and prevents a false
exclusion when uncertainty is deliberately made too large. The same test on
the reset history does not produce an exclusion. Native producers are disabled
during retained certificate recomputation.

replay of the retained halfplanes. The numerical option also reconstructs the
enabled interval corrections. Neither mode runs native events or trajectories.
