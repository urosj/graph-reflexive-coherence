## Exact proof and numerical recipe

`GRC9V4CandidateADifferentialReference.rebuild_exact` uses the accepted
mechanical incidence terms and `ExactScalar` accumulation. It returns exact
incoming-C/W weighted row ratios, including exact zero for empty rows.
`rebuild` rounds those same ratios once to binary64 under the existing recipe.
Neither a stored gradient nor a rounded point descriptor supplies proof data.
The existing WLS descriptor/initializer and its wire identity are preserved.

`grc_v4_ci._a_descriptors_exact` explicitly dispatches the fixed-row owner.
Both the analytic joint residual and the whole-ball contraction certificate
use those exact ratios. CI root and step replay use the closed descriptor
parser. A fixed-row backend is enabled only for A_OS and A_CI; CI+PC retains
its existing WLS contract and other native A profiles remain unopened.

Pressure found a real accuracy gap in first-pass root selection. On source
reset, the second trial's analytic joint norm was about `2.2494e-14`, below
the declared `2^-44` tolerance, but its Hodge coordinates differed from the
accepted oracle by up to `4.4409e-15`, exceeding the `2^-48` geometry budget.
Convergence under the declared residual tolerance alone did not meet this
fixture's independently accepted comparison requirements.

The new fixed-row numerical recipe is
`grc9v4_ci_fixed_rows_confirmed_joint_residual_v1`. It requires two consecutive
joint-residual passes: after the first, perform one more fresh fixed-point
update, then independently check the new current and geometry again. Both
passes count against the existing iteration limit. A failed confirmation or
exhausted budget returns no root. Root/step recipes bind this behavior and
reject the older numerical identifier for fixed-row replay. Existing WLS/C
recipes retain first-pass selection and their original identifiers.

This refines numerical selection without changing the profile, `2^-20`
Frobenius domain, `2^-44` tolerance, 60-evaluation limit, constitutive law or
oracle. The specification requires an admitted root, not termination at the
first tolerance pass. A dedicated regression reconstructs the earlier trial,
proves it passed the joint tolerance while failing the immutable oracle,
then checks the confirmed result and its replay identity. A two-evaluation
budget cannot silently publish the unconfirmed result.

All four nominal source/target role roots now select in three evaluations.
