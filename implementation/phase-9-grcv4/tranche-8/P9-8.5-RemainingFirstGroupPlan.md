single-thread processes, one per family, with separate input/result records.
No shared mutable cache, automatic precision change, numerical fallback or
repeated trajectory/covariance campaign. This is the remaining first group,
not the remaining six families or all-ten closure.

Reuse the accepted C_OS source-self-loop/allocator rejection and its proof that
failure receipts are distinct from persisted successful receipts. Execute the
three malformed-phase/chirality classes on each actual family owner; execute
family-specific target-domain, history/reference, late-publication and reset
failures separately. A passing shared allocator cannot close these obligations.

Each family's small suite uses the existing accepted native fixture under
explicit FLINT / `legacy_exact_v1`, matching its existing rejection tests:

   fail after prospective publication construction and verify the complete owner.
4. Replayed receipt, identity and reference-current/history mutations, including
   rehashed authority substitutions. A_PC retains the full two-role carrier

5. Event admission does not run ordinary continuity or W/Z writers.

   The event checkpoint and generic ledger remain explicitly linked and distinct.
