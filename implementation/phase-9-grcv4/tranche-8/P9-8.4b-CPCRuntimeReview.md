## Mathematical and scientific checks

The [runner](../verification/p984b_cpc_runtime.py) checks the
[paper](../../investigations/grc9v4-constitutive-design/drafts/2026-09-GRC-V4.md)
Appendix D.4/D.7 and E.10 against the
[GRC9 contract](../../../specs/grc-9-v4-spec.md#whole-lifecycle-completion-and-legacy-boundary)
and the accepted fixed-H C equations. A separate 60-digit outward interval
evaluator reconstructs incidence and star overlap from explicit endpoints;
it calls no production candidate, carrier, continuity or ZOH evaluator.

For these connected n-vertex trees, the nonzero unit-weight Laplacian
eigenvalue is at least 4/[n(n-1)]. The whole carrier ball gives
H >= (1-|kappa_H|R)I. Its nonzero stiffness spectrum therefore stays above
the selected cutoff, while the constant sector remains exactly zero. The
oracle verifies the graph assumptions and strict inequality before using
the reduced constant-sector C formula. Native admission independently
supplies the full base-chart/carrier source envelope, selector-gap and
conditioning bounds for every required read, including reset and restart.

At each actual stored input the oracle encloses H=I+kappa_H Z, the full
modulated baseline, physical response/current solve, lowered Read-Back,
star structural source, resource output and scalar held-source carrier
output. Maximum-coordinate absolute error budgets are fixed before execution:

| Quantity | Strict error ceiling |
|---|---:|
| H and stored Z | 2^-48 |
| Current, baseline and resource | 2^-40 |
| Structural source | 2^-60 |

These are pointwise full-formula error bounds at the actual binary64 input;
they are not solver-residual substitutes or an accumulated trajectory tube.
A separate dense/150-digit-Decimal preflight predicts each role's trajectory;
resource and carrier comparisons use 2^-39 and 2^-48 respectively.

The capture verifies the actual continuity selection and intercepts every
native scalar-ZOH call. It requires exactly one call with the complete old
carrier and the incoming read's structural source, then independently
reconstructs its scalar ODE result. A post-continuity source, reset-role
source, extra write or stale restart cannot satisfy the check. Continuity
uses the declared sum, product and subtraction rounding stages. The
structural adapter likewise preserves its assembly-then-gain rounding.

A separate [signed-stage checker](../verification/p984b_cpc_pressure.py)
encloses the Read-Back flux and lowered vector directly, with absolute
ceiling 2^-36. An outer-product source alone cannot detect simultaneous sign
reversal; its regression deliberately flips those intermediates while
leaving the source unchanged. The checker covers all 1,125 captured reads
without rerunning the native campaign and pins the D10 claim source as well.

Each final role read is compared to three path controls: no old-carrier
geometry, no feedback, and no modulation. Each displayed effect must exceed
four times the combined full evaluation error plus eight ULPs. Neutral
target Z has no immediate carrier effect; the focused test checks formation
on its first beat and the subsequent geometry effect. These are read-path
controls, not a paired committed-endpoint hysteresis experiment.
