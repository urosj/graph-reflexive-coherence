containment and norm identities. It implements the signed auxiliary construction
explicitly accepted under P9-8.0 R6. The [completion proof](./P9-8.0-RG2bCompletion.md),
[finite-chain proof](./P9-8.0-RG2bNumericalFeasibility.md) and

The [paper, section 10.4](../../investigations/grc9v4-constitutive-design/drafts/2026-09-GRC-V4.md#104-reconstructed-geometry--rg2b)
describes a compact-support completion that returns the state map and geometry
to identity/reference outside a compact neighborhood. This new completion is
**different**: it has globally bounded increments evaluated after an argument
retraction. It does not have that exterior compact-support property. Its
accepted separate proof establishes global inversion, section self-map and
contraction directly. We do not describe it as the original paper construction
or silently widen the old native positive-resource chart. The
[generic specification](../../../specs/grc-v4-spec.md#reconstructed-geometry-rg2b)
requires a bound frozen completion, K_minus/K/U, containment and C0 contraction;
those are proved and checked here. Both constructions realize the same lagged
invariant-section equation, without becoming CI or acquiring a C1 derivative.

## Completion and arithmetic

The closed profile fixes dt=2^-12, kappa_H=1/2, chi_C=16, zeta_C=2^-37,
kappa_M,C=2^-24, Lambda_C=1/512, and unit eta, kappa_Phi, tau, C_ref and
reference weights/measures. K4_base is zero. Graph admission requires a
connected port-owned tree, 2..17 vertices, with actual incidence infinity norm
at most 9, edge-Gram norm at most 10 and normalized star-mask norm at most 5.
These bounds are checked on the actual graph. The 10-vertex/9-edge source and
17-vertex/16-edge D52 positive-chirality phase-3 target form the numerical
continuation witness. Its dyadic satellite shares (1/2,1/4,1/4) are distinct
from the P9-8.0 equal-third research witness. Parameters and graph preimages
are fully bound into the new profile identities; this is not arbitrary-size
or arbitrary-parameter numerical admission.

C is auxiliary on [-1,5]; K_minus=[-1/4,17/4] and K=[-1/2,9/2]. For every
inverse depth, Fhat(X,H)=X+f(PX,H), Ghat(X,H)=I+S(PX,H)/2. Only f and S
arguments are clamped; the outer X, returned predecessors and physical C are
never clipped. Physical authority continues to enforce nonnegative C.
Geometry is symmetric, vertex-star supported and lies within radius 2^-12 of
identity in the **maximum absolute row-sum norm**, not maximum entry or
Frobenius norm. The section Lipschitz bound is 2^-10.

Production proofs use ExactScalar and the existing outward 160-bit interval
arithmetic. The uniform C estimates reproduce the independent exact research
bounds, including the commutator term in the causal value estimate. With
D=B^T B, t=exp(kappa_M tanh(mean C)), p=zeta_C chi_C, the native auxiliary
reduction uses J0=-t D H B^T C, (I+t H D-p I)u=J0, J=J0+p u, and
v=16 H^-1 u. This is algebraically the full noncommuting response; DH and HD
are never interchanged. The independent oracle evaluates the full normative
Q-similarity chain rather than using this reduction on both sides.

The binary64 proposal has six inverse levels and eighteen forward-state /
backward-geometry sweeps, ending in H_6=I. It is always recertified: exact query
binding, depth, shapes, finite float64 values, terminal identity, symmetry,
support and row-sum bounds precede complete state/geometry residual evaluation
at every level. A valid returned iterate is not assumed to have converged.
For global bounds A_X,A_H,B_X,B_H and depth N=6, let

- a=sum((1-A_X)^(-i), i=1..N);
- d=1-kappa_H B_H-kappa_H B_X a A_H > 0;
- e_H=(r_H+kappa_H B_X a r_X)/d;
- e_X=a(r_X+A_H e_H).

The section-output error is e_H+q^N V, with V=kappa_H M_S. It includes
returned-H rounding through the residual and the tail to the infinite section.

using the output tail q^N there would be incorrect. The declared recipe counts
