## F1 — regenerated geometry can be replaced by the predictor without test failure

**Classification: checker stage-binding gap; not an error in the submitted formulas.**

The mutation leaves current/history evaluation intact but replaces the returned field:

```python
stages['regenerated'] = stages['H'].copy()
```

All three successor tests still pass under this mutation. The nominal split becomes identically zero. `advance()` checks the resource/history errors against independent point enclosures, and independently checks the interval exact-law split bound, but it does not authenticate the returned nominal regenerated geometry against its intended source stage. The exact PSD decision cannot detect a substitution of its operands.

The actual unmutated regeneration is nontrivial. Exact represented row-sum defects at the source and first target were:

The independent interval split proof remains valid; the mutation demonstrates missing protection of the nominal evaluation record, not failure of split feasibility.

**Correction:** bind H to the predictor source and regenerated H to the fresh corrector source under the declared represented assembly rule. At this binding, kappa_H=1/2. `review_controls.py` computes the expected represented entries from exact dyadic source operands and independently rounds the assembly. Unchanged outputs pass at all four named stages; the mutation is rejected at all four. Incorporate this assertion in the existing owner near the nominal split check. Retain the mutation as a negative control. Do not require nonzero split everywhere or change the tolerance.

## F2 — effect helpers silently truncate mismatched vectors

**Classification: minor helper validation gap; no observed invalidation of fixed-shape witness outputs.**

`full_error()` and `separation()` use non-strict zip. Both of these malformed cases are accepted with a positive certified-effect report:

1. An enabled vector of length two compared with a control vector of length one.
2. Two represented vectors of length two supplied with length-one expected enclosures.

The fixed witness constructors produce the expected dimensions, and no actual truncated output was observed. Nevertheless a reusable whole-output comparison must reject these shapes.

**Correction:** require nonempty one-dimensional represented vectors of equal size, full matching column-vector enclosures, and finite operands; use strict zip. The proposed controls reject both length errors and a row-matrix-as-vector error, while preserving a valid full-vector control. No new numerical threshold is required.
