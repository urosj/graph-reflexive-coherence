  radius `3e-6` admits target current but fails target reset. These are failures

- A one-evaluation solver with tolerance `6e-9` admits both source roles but
  exhausts at both targets; tolerance `9e-9` admits target current and exhausts

  reference currents, wrong history policy and extra W/Z fail closed.
- Injected current/reset charge failures and failures during receipt/reference
  evidence construction preserve the entire checkpoint and original state.
  These injected late failures complement the genuine numerical failures.
- Replay rejects altered resources, either role's reference current, graph/
  state/receipt identities, role swaps and rehashed target states. Concurrent
  duplicate requests publish once. The receiver retains its captured exact
  backend when called from a different backend context.
- Simplex vertices fail the fixture's `2^-18` native self-map certificate and
  preserve the checkpoint. A separately declared `2^-17` Frobenius domain
  admits the zero-resource vertex targets, whose roots also pass independent
  interval checks. Domain widening is never automatic. Event success is not
  a guarantee of nonnegative subsequent resource continuity.

This is local D52 C_CI evidence. It does not prove global roots, arbitrary
selector strata, all graph sizes/weights, a formed branch, stability,
completed-spark semantics, cross-backend bitwise conformance, public lifecycle
support, or acceptance of another profile. The internal seed admits its
numerical state but does not authenticate earlier operation chronology.
