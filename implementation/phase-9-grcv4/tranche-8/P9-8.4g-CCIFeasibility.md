## Bound, rather than assume, the full law

For outward incidence B, L=BB^T, beta=zeta_C chi_C and s=1-beta, the reference
divergence is

$$
B J(I)=-\eta_C\kappa_{\Phi,C}t
\left[I+\beta(sI+\tau_C tL)^{-1}\right]L^2 C.
$$

One 100/107-vertex Arb solve evaluates it. No 400/407-edge inverse is needed
for this research question. The earlier Frobenius-ball theorem supplies a
self-map bound A and current Lipschitz bound L_J. Since the unique root is
itself an image of that ball,

$$
\|J_\star-J(I)\|_2\le L_J A.
$$

The reference divergence intervals are widened by ||B_i||_2 L_J A and propagated
through C_i-dt(BJ_star)_i. This conservative bound is adequate for the new
interior margins; it does not purport to resolve the old near-zero exclusion.

The exact-real source-step enclosure is also transferred. Its correlated total
is exactly 200, fixing t at the target mean 200/107. The calculation bounds a
