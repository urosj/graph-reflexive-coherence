## Lower effect margins

At each named nominal source/first-current-target stage, the checker computes
the exact dyadic difference $\widehat\Delta$ between represented outputs.
Independent complete interval evaluations bound each branch's error by
$E_{\rm on}$ and $E_{\rm off}$. Acceptance requires

$$\widehat\Delta>4(E_{\rm on}+E_{\rm off})+8u,$$

where u is the largest local output ULP across both vectors. In particular,
the exact effect is at least $\widehat\Delta-E_{\rm on}-E_{\rm off}>0$.
Identical-output and one-ULP controls are rejected by this criterion. The
interval evaluation covers coefficient construction and transcendentals, not
merely a represented current matrix. It certifies the observed run without
assuming a universal libm rounding guarantee on other machines.
