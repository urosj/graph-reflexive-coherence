### A read and single writer

magnitude is at most eight on the read chart. After continuity, the general

$$0\le E\le E_*:=2^{-49}(10+1200+2048^2)<w.$$

Therefore $G_W\in[1-w,1]$ because $e^{-E}\ge1-E$; the floor is inactive.
Logarithmic convex interpolation preserves $W\in[1-w,1+w]$ for the declared
writer. This is a genuine delayed history estimate, not setting W equal to
the drive or to one at every step. Set

$$q_*:=\frac{w}{1-w},\qquad
\theta_A:=\zeta_A\chi_Aq_*<1.$$

Then $|q_e|\le q_*$ and the diagonal current block has a uniform regularity
margin $1-\theta_A$. With $L_W=B\operatorname{Diag}(W)B^T$, the baseline

$$L_W^2C+\kappa_{Ah}L_WB(H_1-I)B^TC.$$

Its deviation from $L^2C$, including explicit Read-Back, is bounded by

$$\epsilon_A:=1296w(2+w)+2592(1+w)\rho
+\frac{9\cdot1024\,\theta_A}{1-\theta_A}

The two baseline terms respectively bound the changed physical W mobility/
