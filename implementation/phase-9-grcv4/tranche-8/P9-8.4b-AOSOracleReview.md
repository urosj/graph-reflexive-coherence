## Numerical results and limits

[Results](./P9-8.4b-AOSOracleResults.json) retain 355 independently checked
entry evaluations: one shared source-step expectation, two fresh source
reference reads, and 22 evaluations per target (ten predicted steps and one
final read for each history). The first target step's read stages also supply
the target-entry readmission expectation at the same saved C/W; this is not
an extra step. Read-only probes compute diagnostic candidate updates for
checking, but their C/W outputs do not advance the retained sequence.

Each evaluation retains predictor current, predictor and corrector Read-Back,
corrector current and baseline, predicted C/W, generated H and regenerated H.
The interval checker bounds complete formula error at the saved input—not just
a linear-solve residual—and checks geometry, current regularity, inactive
conductance floor, output domain and exact represented OS split admission.

The preregistered maximum-coordinate absolute budgets are `2^-40` for C,
baseline/current and both Read-Back outputs, and `2^-48` for W and both Hodge
matrices. Relative slack is not added. Across target evaluations, the largest
full-error bounds are approximately `2.217e-16` for C, `5.551e-17` for W,
`8.881e-16` for current, `1.111e-16` for H and `2.711e-20` for Read-Back.
The largest interval split upper bound is approximately `2.617e-15`, below
the unchanged `2^-40` tolerance. Minimum interval lower endpoints are about
`1.06390e-5` for predicted C and `0.999023675` for W. All sixteen cases pass;
none times out. Their summed evaluation time is 118.88 seconds.

These are full-formula error certificates at the recorded binary64 entry
points, not a uniform parameter tube, an accumulated trajectory-error bound,
new effect-separation margins, native admission, or indefinite continuation.
New native results must be checked again against the equations at their own
saved incoming C/W. A rounded native source beat must generate its own fresh
source, history-policy, event and target identities; it cannot borrow the
nominal oracle seed's identities merely because it lies within an error budget.

## Focused scientific pressure

The user requested a separate scientific pressure pass before oracle acceptance.
The [pressure checker](../verification/p984b_aos_pressure.py),
[eight tests](../verification/test_p984b_aos_pressure.py) and
[retained results](./P9-8.4b-AOSScientificPressure.json) now supply it. All eight
tests pass, with no errors or skips; the retained run took 28.04 seconds. The
sixteen-case campaign was not rerun or changed. These tests use the accepted
paper and interval equations, not production trajectories or a new external
scientific review.

**Numerical agreement alone does not establish every stage's consumption.**
Six wrong-law controls were tested at the first target step of D30 positive
and D52 phase one in both chiralities, for both histories: 36 comparisons.
Each alternative is calculated from its own equation and independently
enclosed, rather than made by adding an arbitrary output error.

| Wrong alternative | Rejected by frozen output budgets | Separated beyond combined formula errors and 8 ULPs |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Use predictor current instead of the fresh corrector | 6/6 | 6/6 |
| Disable Read-Back in the corrector | 6/6 | 6/6 |
| Freeze W instead of writing history | 6/6 | 6/6 |
| Write history from incoming rather than final C | 6/6 | 6/6 |
| Write history using predictor rather than selected current | 0/6 | 0/6 |
| Reuse predictor geometry as regenerated geometry | 0/6 | 0/6 |

The last two rows are real limits, not rejected cases. Predictor-current
substitution changes represented W by at most `1.111e-16`, sometimes zero;
geometry substitution changes the represented matrix by `4.441e-16` to
`1.111e-15`. Their exact-law differences are nonzero, but the differences fit
within the existing `2^-48` output budgets and do not clear the stated
whole-output effect margin. Budgets were not tightened to force rejection.

A separate research consumption test observes the actual paper evaluator's
two reads and single history write. It checks the writer's exact incoming W,
final C and selected corrector J operands, rejecting incoming-C and
predictor-J substitutions. The accepted staged binary64 geometry-assembly
check independently rejects substituting predictor H for geometry regenerated
from the corrector's source. **The native adapter must perform corresponding
checks on actual native operands and sources.** It cannot infer those facts
from small output error or split admission. Research consumption success does
not discharge that native obligation.
