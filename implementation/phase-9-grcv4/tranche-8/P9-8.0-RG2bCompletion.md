## Self-contained section theorem

For either graph and candidate, let $\mathcal L$ be all maps from the complete
auxiliary state space to $\mathcal H_\rho$ with Lipschitz constant at most L.
It is complete in uniform distance. For any $\Gamma\in\mathcal L$,

$$
\ell=A_X+A_HL<1,\qquad M_{\rm inv}=1/(1-\ell).
$$

For every destination $X_+$, the map $X\mapsto X_+-f(PX,\Gamma(X))$ is a contraction on the
entire state space. Its unique fixed point is the inverse of
$\widehat F_\Gamma$; subtraction of the fixed-point equations bounds that
inverse's Lipschitz constant by $M_{\rm inv}$.

Define the graph transform by

$$
(\mathcal T\Gamma)(X_+)=I+\kappa_H S(PX,\Gamma(X)),\qquad
X=\widehat F_\Gamma^{-1}(X_+).
$$

It has values in $\mathcal H_\rho$ and Lipschitz constant at most L provided

$$
\kappa_H M_S<\rho,\qquad
\kappa_H(B_X+B_HL)M_{\rm inv}\le L.
$$

For two sections, their inverse points differ by at most
$M_{\rm inv}A_H\|\Gamma_1-\Gamma_2\|_{\sup}$. Therefore

$$
\|\mathcal T\Gamma_1-\mathcal T\Gamma_2\|_{\sup}
\le q_0\|\Gamma_1-\Gamma_2\|_{\sup},\quad
q_0=\kappa_H\left[B_H+(B_X+B_HL)M_{\rm inv}A_H\right]<1.
$$

All three inequalities hold for both candidates. Banach yields a unique
section in this bounded Lipschitz class, satisfying

$$
\Gamma(\widehat F(X,\Gamma(X)))=\widehat G(X,\Gamma(X)),\qquad
\|\Gamma-I\|_{\sup}\le\kappa_H M_S.
$$

This is precisely a **completion-relative Lipschitz** conclusion of the kind
required by the [RG2b contract](../../../specs/grc-v4-spec.md#reconstructed-geometry-rg2b).
It grants no $C^1$ Jacobian/spectrum or completion-independent uniqueness.
