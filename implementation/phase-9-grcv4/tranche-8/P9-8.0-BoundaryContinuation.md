### A strict selector neighborhood

All target role graphs are connected unit-weight trees, with at most 17
vertices and maximum degree five. The source is a ten-vertex connected star.
For any connected unit-weight graph on $N$ vertices and mean-zero $x$,

$$2N\lVert x\rVert_2^2=\sum_{i,j}(x_i-x_j)^2
\le N^2(N-1)x^TLx.$$

The inequality bounds each difference along a simple path of at most $N-1$
edges. Hence $\lambda_2(L)\ge2/[N(N-1)]$. If the edge-form metric obeys

$$\lambda_2(BH_1B^T)\ge\frac1{N(N-1)}
\ge\frac1{272}>\Lambda_C=\frac1{512}>0.$$

The zero eigenvalue remains exactly the constant sector. Thus the proposed
reference cutoff has a proved nonzero geometry neighborhood, not a cutoff
