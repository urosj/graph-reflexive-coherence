## Resource and charge compatibility

For either role define the exact `17 x 10` matrix P by exterior identity
rows, three satellite rows with `1/3` in the source-center column, and zero
core/extra rows. Thus

\[
P\ge0,\qquad \mathbf1_{17}^{T}P=\mathbf1_{10}^{T},\qquad C^+=PC^-.
\]

P preserves nonnegativity and charge on both role neighborhoods. It implements
the [unit-measure resource contract](../../../specs/grc-9-v4-spec.md#whole-lifecycle-completion-and-legacy-boundary)
with zero affine increment and `Delta Q_event=0`. Each role retains its own
charge target; reset does not inherit current's different total. At the nominal
exact initial inputs those totals are `30+9/64` and `20+9/64`, respectively.
The exact ordinary continuity map `C_next=C-d B J` also preserves unit-measure
charge because each incidence column has one +1 and one -1. History and
carrier writers do not change resources. This proof is independent of the
selected current, geometry realization and history policy.

Exact charge preservation is distinct from represented arithmetic. The
companions enforce exterior copies and five zero faces exactly and certify
all event-resource errors against P applied to saved entry values. For any
operation with coordinate error at most e on n vertices, the discrepancy of
the exact sums of its represented operands is at most `n e`. Every represented
resource step and event already passes `e < 2^-40`. Consequently telescoping
the charge discrepancies from the represented initial state bounds the current
path by `(10+17+10*17)*2^-40 = 197*2^-40`, and the reset path by
`(17+10*17)*2^-40 = 187*2^-40`. The source step is counted only for current;
reference probes and final reads are excluded. This is a scalar conservation
bound, not an accumulated state-trajectory error estimate.

The [generic charge contract](../../../specs/grc-v4-spec.md#common-resource-and-transport-contract)
additionally requires the identity-bearing stable pairwise binary64 sum,
resolved absolute/relative tolerances, signed residual and no resource repair.
The research bounds do not test that native reduction or choose its tolerances.
Later native bindings must account for initial encoding, charge-target encoding
and sum rounding as well as the certified write errors, and reject an
inadmissible residual without mutation. Exact-zero tolerance is not inferred
from P's exact identity. No renormalization or redistribution is authorized.
