## What generalizes, and what remains conditional

The [specialization specification](../../../specs/grc-9-v4-spec.md#conditional-phase-and-arbitrary-size-tree)
and retained D11-G9 authority define the recursive mechanical law. A new tree
node adds nine ports and one internal edge consumes two endpoint ports, giving
seven additional free ports. A connected tree with n vertices has n-1 internal
edges, hence capacity 9n-2(n-1)=7n+2. The creation-order rotor maintains available
branch valence, so the declared allocator does not stall at a finite admitted
module size. Explicit phase/chirality and transported namespace/roles determine
the operation; a target-success search is not its selector.

These general structural arguments explain the implemented allocator and its
recursive/covariant tests. They do **not** prove numerical readmission for every
graph/state produced by that allocator, nor formally verify every possible
implementation execution. Deep and larger native examples now meet the selected
first-group evidence obligations, while **unqualified arbitrary-size numerical
or full-specialization conformance remains held**. No further first-group
campaign is needed merely to repeat these accepted bounded obligations.

The shared record preserves five D11-G9 contract traces and the allocation-debt
trace, with their original claim classes, support dispositions and source/edge
references in [the authority record](P9-8.4i-FirstGroupAuthority.json). The
resolved design debt and historical runtime-pending statements are not rewritten
as universal runtime closure. This is downstream implementation evidence, not
a new scientific claim or an amendment to the paper/specification/claim ledger.

- **C_OS:** original phase-one/deep negative-resource and represented-charge
  controls remain exclusions. The larger witness uses the admitted second beat.
  Its first-step minima near 1e-13 are supported by certified bounds, not by the
  later dense-comparison tolerance. Exact split rejection and Arb certification
  inconclusive remain distinct outcomes with rollback.
- **C_CI:** both original larger target roles had negative exact-root first
  steps; the allocation-only companion failed source eligibility. The accepted
  source/allocation companion is a different named subject. Local root bounds
  certify the observed inputs, not an unrounded shared ten-step trajectory.
- **A_CI:** the original larger reset-as-current target was negative. The
  history/allocation companion and actual event chronology matter. The accepted
  charge-check successor removed an extra zero-residual assertion; it did not
  relax the profile tolerance. Full-root accuracy is not blanket mechanism
  separation.
- **A_PC:** original D94 and resource-only/history-only companions remain
  excluded. The failed 11/16 and successful 7/8 histories are distinct. Whole-Z
  archive/drop and zero target initialization are explicitly lossy; subsequent
  carrier effects are resolved at the tested points but small.

All families distinguish represented charge admission from exact sums of stored
binary64 resources. Nonzero stored-sum drift is not erased. Fixed FLINT/Arb
policies, explicit inconclusive outcomes and absence of automatic fallback remain
part of the evidence domains. There is no new performance claim.
