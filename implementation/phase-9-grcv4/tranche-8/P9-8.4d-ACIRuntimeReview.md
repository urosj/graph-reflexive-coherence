## Execution and independent checks

The [native inputs](./P9-8.4d-ACICases.json) bind the accepted actual source
and the oracle's D59/66/73/94 targets: both chiralities, applicable phases,
18/19/20/23 vertices and core depths three/four. The accepted source beat
is reused exactly. Each case begins from that source and executes one event;
this is not a sequence of expansions consuming one another.

The adapter changes only subject binding and the lookup of precomputed
independent expectations. Native roots, steps, event owners and scientific
checks are unchanged. No production, specification, law, timestep, solver
tolerance or numerical error ceiling changed.

Each event checks the independent graph/port/reference reconstruction, exact
initial resource and W transfer, new-edge seeding, both-role joint-root and
writer-surface admission before publication, source-current detection,
reference lineage, receipts and deterministic checkpoint replay. Detached
continuation leaves the published event owner unchanged.

Every ordinary update consumes the selected current for continuity, fresh
post-update resources for the W writer, and the written W for restart.
Current, reset and restart roots retain their complete current/geometry
points and independent interval certificates. Each role advances ten steps,
then a fresh full root must equal the saved restart. Native resources/history
are compared with the accepted independent predictions at every step.
Capture-time flags remain unaccepted; a committed event without successful
continuation would be an incomplete case, not a pass.

## Tiny resources and charge limits

The smallest represented resource is **3.678606801938831e-22**, at D94
E-1 phase three, reset role, first update. It remains strictly positive in
the native run, including the subsequent continuation and fresh reads.
Focused pressure independently recomputes both first-entry roles at this
largest target. This does not establish a robust positivity neighborhood or
an accumulated exact-real trajectory tube; the oracle's tiny lower margin
remains a material restriction on the scientific claim.

The frozen charge policy uses the canonical adjacent balanced binary64 tree
with absolute tolerance **1e-11** and no resource repair. Across all 400
updates the largest reduced-charge residual is **7.105427357601002e-15**.
All 400 updates nevertheless have nonzero exact coordinate-sum changes;
the largest absolute per-step change is about **1.329260466415085e-15**.
Thus native charge admission passes, but exact coordinate conservation is
not claimed for represented ordinary updates. Exact initial event transfer
and rounded ordinary continuation are distinct statements.
