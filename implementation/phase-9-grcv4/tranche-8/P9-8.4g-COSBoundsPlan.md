## Regularity without constructing an inverse

Write $s=1-\zeta_C\chi_C$. The native exact regularity test concerns

$$
A=sI_m+\tau_C B^T H_0^{-1} B H_M.
$$

For $H_0,H_M\succ0$, conjugating by $H_M^{1/2}$ gives the symmetric matrix
$sI_m+\tau_C H_M^{1/2}B^TH_0^{-1}BH_M^{1/2}$. Thus $s>0$ and
$\tau_C\ge0$ prove invertibility, without building an inverse. This is an
equivalent decision on that domain, not a numerical approximation.

At $s=0$, invertibility requires $\tau_C>0$ and full column rank of B
(apart from the empty edge space). Cycles therefore matter. For $s\ne0$,
Sylvester's identity gives

$$
\det A=s^{m-n}\frac{\det(sH_0+\tau_C B H_M B^T)}{\det H_0}.
$$

The research kernel checks positive s directly, rank at zero, and an exact
FLINT vertex determinant for negative s. Its supplied SPD witness is strict
symmetric diagonal dominance; failure to obtain that witness is **unsupported**,
not a singularity verdict. It is not a replacement for production conditioning,
residual checks, or actual current solves. Pressure exact singular examples,
cycles, forests, disconnected graphs, zero incidence, zero response parameter,
and invalid SPD operands against small direct exact determinants.
