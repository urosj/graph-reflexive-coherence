## Mechanism of the construction

SC-1 allows the surviving parent plus one new module-anchored basin to give local gain one. It does not require two new satellites when the parent survives. The earlier source has no private neighbor of the parent: all its neighbors also connect to the event module. A parent peak can consequently lose its guaranteed multi-vertex all-route domain even while its seed inequalities remain true.

Keep the preflight's eleven-vertex source and all its old edges. Add one exterior vertex `private`, connected by parent port 2 to `private` port 1. Parent ports 1,4,7 retain their old neighbors n1,n5,n9. Vertex v remains exactly saturated. This added old edge is not involved in replacement of v.
