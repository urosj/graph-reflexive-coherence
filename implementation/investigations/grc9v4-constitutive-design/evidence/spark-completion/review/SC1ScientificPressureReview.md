# SC-1 scientific pressure review

## Disposition

The spatial-descent lemma and exact-real full-state contraction theorem pass as conditional mathematics. The definition is an explicit new geometric/routing identity policy, not a deduction from the accepted physical-flow diagnostic. Its scientific admission remains a separate choice.

The principal new result of this audit is an applicability obstruction: on the closed, zero-site-derivative, zero-forcing tree bindings represented in the retained C_OS, C_CI, A_CI and A_PC evidence, every exact full-state fixed point has constant resource. Such a point has no SC-1 qualified anchor. Therefore those bindings cannot supply the invariant contracting domain with persistent qualified child basins required by SC-1. This does not invalidate earlier finite-horizon mechanical continuation or the SC-1 implication; it identifies an empty intersection of their hypotheses.

No native trajectory or event was executed in this audit. The two implementation helpers imported by the submitted runner were not supplied or retrieved. Their fifteen geometric and twenty-five finite-reference groups were not replayed.

## 1. What is established by the proposed mathematics

Let Psi(i)=s_H C_i and take all strictly descending neighbors. Every route is finite because Psi decreases strictly; repeated vertices are impossible. Terminal reachability is nonempty on a finite graph. If B_s is the set of vertices having s as their unique reachable terminal, each successor of a vertex in B_s is also in B_s. Different B_s are disjoint. Every maximal route beginning in B_s reaches s in at most |V|-1 moves.

The proposed barrier follows from the strict local-neighbor condition. On any path joining distinct qualified anchors, choose the endpoint with the larger Psi. The adjacent vertex on that path has value greater than that endpoint by Delta_Psi. Hence the path maximum exceeds max(Psi(s),Psi(t))+Delta_Psi. Adjacent strict-minimum anchors are impossible; disconnected anchors are separately covered by topological separation.

These are theorems about the declared routing system. They do not make physical J follow descent in Psi and do not turn Psi into constitutive potential, transport work or an energy functional.

For the temporal result, an actual exact map T_h taking a nonempty complete K into itself and satisfying a global Lipschitz constant q<1 has a unique fixed point and geometric convergence. Uniformly certified strict basin predicates and fixed descent relations on K persist along that convergence and at the limit. The strict relative image margin additionally places the fixed point inside the relative physical neighborhood. Full C/W/Z dependence is essential; a current solve or CI internal root contraction is not the required contraction.

The conclusion is one attracting full-field pattern with several persistent routing basins. It is not several alternative full-state attractors, independent subsystems or causal autonomy. The proposal explicitly preserves these distinctions.

## 2. New bounded equilibrium obstruction

### Assumptions

Use the accepted consumed-current continuity equation, a positive ordinary duration, closed/no-external-exchange context, a connected tree, positive transport/Hodge data, zero site-potential derivative, and an admitted exact read/root. For A_CI/A_PC additionally use zero base structural field, the retained zero-preserving quadratic star adapter, and the standard finite-time PC writer when present. These are the relevant mathematical premises, not universal premises of every possible V4 profile.

The earlier supplied four-family pressure records were inspected again: all 112 role-read declarations use connected trees (10/9 or 17/16 vertices/edges), zero context, zero K4_base and `quadratic_site_potential_zero_derivative_v1`. This is inspection of retained declarations, not authentication of current native code or a new run.

### Common first step

At a fixed point of the actual full ordinary map,

    C+ = C - h B J_use = C,

so B J_use=0. The incidence matrix of a connected tree has full column rank. Therefore J_use=0. This uses the consumed current, not a fresh observation current.

Both candidate read contributions vanish at J=0 under the retained linear-in-current read laws. Thus J0=0 as well.

### Candidate C: C_OS and C_CI

At the consumed geometry, write

    J0 = -M B^T Phi,
    Phi = kappa B H_M B^T C,

where M is positive diagonal and H_M is positive definite. J0=0 gives B^T Phi=0. Connectivity makes Phi constant, while 1^T Phi=0 makes that constant zero. Therefore

    0 = C^T Phi = kappa (B^T C)^T H_M (B^T C),

so B^T C=0 and C is constant. No equality between C_OS's consumed corrector and its fresh restart was assumed.

### Candidate A: A_CI and A_PC

The quadratic structural source vanishes at J=0. A_CI consequently has reference geometry at a fixed point when K4_base=0. For A_PC, stationary Z obeys

    Z = a Z + (1-a) S,  0<a<1,

so S=0 implies Z=0, and the geometry is again the reference geometry. Hence

    J0 = -eta diag(W) B^T Phi,
    Phi = kappa L_W C.

Positive W and connectivity imply Phi=0 and then C is constant. The W writer separately fixes W to its zero-current drive; it does not permit a nonconstant resource equilibrium here.

For A the tree restriction can be dropped whenever the effective current multipliers are positive. Indeed

    J = -diag(eta W_e / (1-zeta chi q_e)) B^T Phi

and B J=0 yield

    0 = Phi^T B J
      = -sum_e [eta W_e/(1-zeta chi q_e)] (B^T Phi)_e^2.

Thus J=0 on any connected graph in that positive-denominator domain, followed by the same source/carrier/reference-geometry argument.

### Consequence

Constant C has g=0, signed row Hessian=0 and zero neighbor contrast. It cannot be a qualified anchor for positive epsilon_H and Delta_Psi. A complete invariant contracting K containing a fixed nonempty anchor roster would contain its unique fixed point and thereby contradict this result.

The obstruction is stronger than a failed trajectory search. It does not exclude other constitutive profiles, nonzero site potentials, external exchange, nonzero permanent structure, or scientifically distinct nonstationary completion criteria. It also does not contradict finite-horizon enabled evolution or mechanical refinement results.

### Additional A stability pressure

On the smooth uniform equilibrium branch, W*=w*1 with w=max(W_floor,exp(-alpha c)), q_A=0, and the generated source has zero first derivative in current. The resource block of the full derivative is

    D_C T_h = I + h eta kappa w^2 L^2.

The C-to-W and C-to-Z dependencies do not remove its eigenvalues, because the reverse W/Z-to-C derivative is zero at the uniform field. On any nonconstant graph Laplacian mode lambda>0, the multiplier is

    1 + h eta kappa w^2 lambda^2 > 1.

Such a mode lies in the fixed-charge tangent space. A change of norm or a smaller positive h cannot make this eigenvalue less than one. This is a further obstruction to the proposed contraction certificate in this binding, not a proposed correction to the substrate sign convention.

## 3. Definition and source feasibility

On a loop-free positive-weight graph, the seed requires occupied support in all three rows. The strict neighbor barrier also implies s_H g_a>Delta_Psi in every occupied row. Consequently

    epsilon_g > sqrt(3) max(epsilon_H, Delta_Psi)

is necessary for an anchor. This strengthens the seed-only necessary condition in the proposal. It is a useful pre-search exclusion, not sufficient feasibility.

The previously used nine-leaf source has no qualified parent when its center is a spark candidate with epsilon_spark<=epsilon_H. Leaves have empty rows and fail the seed; the center's strict spark inequality conflicts with its being a seed. Allowing the parent anchor to differ from the expanded vertex is mathematically correct, but that particular source has no other eligible anchor. A larger source may fix this parent-topology issue; it does not by itself remove the zero-site A equilibrium obstruction.

The gain Delta N=N_desc-1 is local to the lifted parent footprint and the proposed descendant eligibility. It is not a theorem that the total number of basins on the entire graph increases. Exterior organizations may change or disappear. Likewise, source qualification is instantaneous, not an additional certificate of a stable pre-event full-field parent attractor.

No counterfactual event-causation claim follows from ancestry and gain alone. The supplied contract does not need such a claim to define its local completion policy, but downstream descriptions must not silently add it.

## 4. Numerical lifting needs two explicit choices

### Fixed-charge geometry

A strict image margin relative to an exact charge manifold does not bound error normal to that manifold. For

    K={(x1,x2):x1+x2=1, |xi-1/2|<=1/4}
    T(x)=x*/2+x/2, x*=(1/2,1/2),

the relative margin is 1/8. An output error (1/32,1/32) has norm smaller than that margin but immediately leaves the charge slice. Even an error of 2^-50 in each coordinate produces a charge error of 2^-49: below the retained 1e-11 charge tolerance, but still outside the exact slice. This is a mathematical control, not a demonstrated native error. The proposal already warns that error bounds do not enforce exact charge. Actual certificates must resolve the issue, not treat an existing nonzero-tolerance charge gate as exact slice preservation.

Sound routes include a proved constraint-preserving represented update, or a validated family of charge-slice invariant sets/fixed points with an explicit charge-error bound. Extending K to a charge slab does not restore a single globally contracting charge-conserving map: different exactly conserved charges cannot converge to one common fixed point. No resource repair or new runtime policy is selected by this review.

### Exact versus represented seed qualification

The previous basin interface intentionally uses represented row operands. The continuum exact-map proof and toy use exact row functions. Their strict predicates can disagree even with correctly rounded rows.

Example: exact g=(1/3,2/3,2/3), epsilon_g=1. The exact squared norm equals one, so the strict gradient test fails. Binary64 rounding gives every coordinate a common factor (1-2^-54), and the exact sum of squares of those represented coordinates is smaller than one. The represented test passes. All three Hessian coordinates can be above epsilon_H=1/4. These rows can be produced by equal-weight dyadic neighbor differences (1/4,1/4,1/2), (1/2,1/2,1), (1/2,1/2,1), with a neighbor barrier 1/8.

This is not a bug in the accepted represented classifier. It demonstrates a separate proof/interface choice. SC-1 should name whether its scientific anchors are exact-row objects, represented-row objects, or certified agreement of both. To certify agreement, retain uniform row-evaluation bounds below the strict predicate margins. Exact squaring of already-rounded rows does not remove their prior evaluation error. A full-step C/W/Z bound does not automatically bound a separately reconstructed row diagnostic.

## 5. Capacity claim: make the asymptotic premise precise

The summation inequality

    iota T_n <= Q_max - C_s0 + E_n

is correct. The phrase "E_n/T_n stays below iota" needs a uniform asymptotic gap. Merely being strictly below at every finite n is insufficient.

Countermodel: h=1, iota=1/4, C_s0=1, Q_max=3. After the first step, numerical error cancels each subsequent inflow, with the opposite error at a second vertex to keep total charge three. Then

    E_n=(n-1)/4, E_n/T_n=(1/4)(1-1/n)<iota,
    C_sn=5/4

for every n>=1. The error ratio tends to iota and positive consumed inflow can persist indefinitely under this deliberately adversarial recurrence.

A precise sufficient condition is T_n->infinity and limsup E_n/T_n<iota. More generally it suffices that iota T_n-E_n eventually exceed Q_max-C_s0. This is a sharpening of the wording, not a failure of the displayed inequality.
