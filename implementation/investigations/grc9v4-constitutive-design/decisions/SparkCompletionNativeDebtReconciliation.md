## Evidence-to-obligation reasoning

Numerical discharge uses each accepted full campaign's complete physical-map
certificate, represented error inside its image margin, exact charge preservation
and actual predicate checks, together with the corresponding accepted scientific
contract. An orbit, root residual, overlap or successful decode alone would not
satisfy this obligation. CI includes the selected joint current/geometry root;
PC includes its signed carrier and writer; Candidate A includes W history and
its writer. CI+PC includes their coupling. RG2b uses its admitted unique
Lipschitz section and whole physical map, without inferring C1 regularity.

Lifecycle discharge uses the family's actual source/event and both-role
continuation, its complete cold replay, and the accepted shared integration
suite. Entry is observation zero, followed by two positive transitions. Current
completion earns local gain one; the independent reset comparison earns zero.
The shared lifecycle tests exercise rollback, concurrent duplicate accounting,
reset/rebase/policy invalidation, stale import and retrieval of proof operands.
Their short synthetic windows are not substituted for the ten accepted actual
source-to-completion campaigns. All-ten wire/persistence checks likewise do not
stand in for full-event replay.

The five earlier endpoint obligations select the adopted successor endpoints,
not the original longer research endpoint as a new requirement. Earlier research
trajectories remain evidence for their original subjects. Native acceptance does
not relabel those runs or produce another birth.
