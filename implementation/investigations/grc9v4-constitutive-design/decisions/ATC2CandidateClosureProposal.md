## 1. Result and recommendation

Carry two **separate**, concrete derivative-free research policies forward for
review: CAN-A represented-node-coupling edge birth and CAN-C local residual
coarsening. They test increasing and forward-simplifying construction without
calling either a core spark, an exact quotient, restoration or formation.
Neither requires acceptance of the other.

CAN-B's graph/resource/reference builder can be specified and pressure-tested
given a half-edge partition. Its **structural trigger is held**: this work does
not supply the qualified reduced functional, regularity, tangent/metric,
critical branch, simple mode and equivariant mode-to-partition lift needed to
generate that partition. A supplied partition is not autonomous splitting.

The positive native probes use the existing caller-supplied event owner.
Generation occurs in this research script, after a separately committed
ordinary step, against a finite predeclared target catalogue. This establishes
bounded compatibility of generated requests with the frozen owner, **not** a
native automatic dispatcher, dynamic target registration or K0's single-owner
serialized after-beat wrapper. Those require the scientific-to-specification
sequence before 7T implementation.

## 2. Exact selected law and numerical meaning

The common input and realization-dependent present-state read are exactly
[K0 §§2–3](./ATC1CausalBoundaryProposal.md#2-proposed-physical-input-a-narrower-explicit-first-law).
Read the committed current state; retain existing W/Z; do not write, use a
zero-duration lifecycle operation, substitute current for reset, or use the
preceding step's consumed current. The first domain remains the shipped
constant-zero/no-external-input context, unit vertex measure and nondimensional
reference units. Base-profile domain and numerical declarations remain fixed.

For these research variants, finite binary64 H, C and J returned by the admitted
native read are **the defined numerical operands**. Subsequent sums, products,
ratios, eligibility and dominance comparisons use exact rational arithmetic on
those operands. This removes summation-order ambiguity in the discrete score;
it does not enclose error relative to an ideal CI root, RG section or real-valued
constitutive solution. A future ideal-solution predicate needs its own error
certificate and must not inherit the word “certified” from these comparisons.

### CAN-A: represented squared coupling

For each unordered distinct nonadjacent pair, form

$$
K_V=BHB^T,\qquad s_{uv}=\frac{(K_V)_{uv}^2}{(K_V)_{uu}(K_V)_{vv}}.
$$

Both diagonal operands must be positive and the represented score must lie in
[0,1]; undefined normalization is `uncertified`, not zero or epsilon-regularized.
The guard is $s_{uv}>2^{-50}$. Among eligible pairs, require a unique largest
score separated from every other eligible score by **more than** $2^{-60}$.
This is an explicit **squared-score variant**: a gap in q-squared is not the
same law as an equal numerical gap in q. No square-root approximation is used.

Empty eligibility means `no_event`; ties or insufficient separation mean
`unresolved`. A sole eligible candidate needs no invented runner-up. With
diagonal reference H, all defined nonedge scores are zero: the selected OS
stage therefore cannot activate this law. This is not a ban on every OS law.

### CAN-C: local state/vector-field residual coarsening

Consider each unordered adjacent distinct pair. With the same present read,
$f=-BJ$ and

$$
r_C^2=(C_u-C_v)^2/2,\qquad r_f^2=(f_u-f_v)^2/2,
$$

$$
a_C=r_C^2/\epsilon_C^2,\quad a_f=r_f^2/\epsilon_f^2,\quad
z=\frac{1}{1+a_C+a_f},\qquad \epsilon_C=1/2,\quad\epsilon_f=16.
$$

The constants have the corresponding C and C-per-time units in the selected
nondimensional convention; the ratios and score are dimensionless. Eligibility
is $a_C\le1$ and $a_f\le1$. Select one unique maximum with gap greater than
$2^{-20}$, with the same empty/tied/singleton conventions. This supplies a
specific score where the source left a family of choices. These constants are
research-policy choices, not derived universal physical scales or a fit proving
the trigger's physical interpretation.

This tests **local similarity**, not complete-state quotient compatibility.
It does not establish $H\Phi^- = \Phi^+H$, matching W/Z histories, approximate
future behavior, preserved attractors, a viable core collapse or restoration.
No global trajectory optimization or ranking across CAN-A/C is performed.

### Bounds and identity

Each policy permits at most 16 vertices and 32 edges; target construction must
remain in those bounds. Reference seeding uses at most 32 weights and a
131072-bit bound on the exact product numerator/denominator. Graph bounds and
finite input formats also bound score arithmetic; the explicit product-bit
check belongs to the geometric-mean routine, not every rational operation.
Inherited CI/RG/domain budgets remain binding. Exceeding a bound, read failure,
or target rejection does not trigger a fallback law, runner-up, changed chart,
smaller time step, clipping or an ID-based physical tie-break.

The script's POLICY plus selected law, immutable script content and full base
profile/reference are the reproducible **research** policy subject. Wire names
are minted only after physical selection. The target carries the same selected
research law/constants rebound to its new base/reference, outside the native
archive. No new profile schema, accepted declaration, archive field or runtime
authorization is invented here. Installing that identity/transport belongs to
ATC-3/specification/7T, not a claim that this research dictionary is already a
complete admitted production identity.

## 3. Complete conditional target recipes

These constructors preserve the candidate and realization. They do not search
over target profiles or enlarge the inherited admission domain. Construction
success is distinct from full current/reset target admission.

| Edit | Graph and lineage | Resource map |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CAN-A birth | Preserve all vertices/edges and add one edge between the selected nonadjacent vertices. Existing loops/parallel edges remain. New orientation is a wire coordinate choice, not a physical winner. | Identity T; zero additive resource. |
| CAN-C merge | Replace the adjacent pair by one quotient vertex. Drop **all** edges internal to the pair, including internal loops; map every surviving endpoint through the quotient; preserve surviving edge IDs and parallel edges. | Sum the two source coordinates into the quotient coordinate; identity on other coordinates. |
| Conditional CAN-B split | Replace one vertex by two children and add one child edge. Partition every incident **half-edge** into two nonempty blocks; reroute its endpoint to its assigned child. A loop has two endpoint roles: together it remains a loop, separately it becomes a child-to-child edge. Old edge identities survive. | Distribute the parent resource by the dyadic shares below; identity on other coordinates. |

Edgeless targets are explicitly outside this constructor's executable claim;
a two-node one-edge merge therefore does not prove a viable target. Source and
target graph bounds apply. There is no inverse-expansion archive lookup.

For the conditional split, sum $|J_e|$ over each block's half-edge roles; both
ends of a loop count separately. Let those exact nonnegative activities be a,b.
Use $r=a/(a+b)$, or $r=1/2$ if both vanish. With $N=2^{16}$, ties-to-even
$k=\operatorname{round}(Nr)$ gives shares $k/N$ and $(N-k)/N$. Their represented
sum is exactly one, each lies in [0,1], and the error is at most $1/(2N)$.
Zero allocation to a child is allowed. Complementing the blocks complements
the shares; IDs do not pick a side. This is a resource constructor, not a
derivation of the missing partition.

Apply the same T independently to current and reset; accumulate each output
exactly from represented inputs, then round once using the existing event
owner. Exact column conservation **does not** imply exact stored total after
separate rounding: splitting the smallest subnormal can lose stored charge.
Retain the owner's actual charge checks and reject when they fail; do not
repair a distinguished coordinate. The pressure includes nonconservative
represented thirds and this subnormal counterexample.

Target references are supplied, not left implicit:

- Surviving edges retain their positive reference weights. A new edge receives
  CAN-REF-GM of the distinct incident source-edge weights at the birth pair or
  split vertex. Empty/invalid seed sets reject. The mean is the correctly
  rounded positive binary64 root of their **exact** product, obtained by bounded
  adjacent-float search and exact midpoint/ties-even comparison; not a log/exp
  approximation or an overflowing floating product.
- CAN-KMASK carries an old-old K4 entry only when both edges survive and share
  a target vertex; all new-edge rows/columns are zero. Symmetry/support and a
  Frobenius bound survive masking; positivity or final geometry admission is
  **not** inferred from masking and must pass the native target owner.
- Candidate C gets complete target W_C_tr equal to the declared target
  reference weights, with its content binding. No T_C cache is transported.
- Candidate A preserves host dimension, regularization and unaffected host
  positions. Merge uses the exact coordinate sum divided by two, rounded once;
  split duplicates the parent's position; birth preserves positions. Rebuild
  the target differential reference and rebind its identity. Collocation is
  permitted by this recipe but does not guarantee full target admission.
- Rebind K4/reference/parameter hashes and only the affected reference fields.
  Keep all scientific gains, domain bounds, solver choices, history policy and
  constant-zero context unchanged. A target requiring different scientific
  parameters is a different proposal, not an automatic repair.
- Select CAN-HRESET explicitly: initialize **all** target A W by the accepted
  target-only reference-pass initializer, separately for current/reset target C;
  C has no W slot. Set the whole persistent target carrier to zero independently
  in each role. Retain existing explicit-loss receipts; this is not lossless
  lineage transport, a continuity step or a carrier-formation beat.

The target-only initializer's provenance remains optional bounded authority,
not a universal target-existence theorem. The pressure compares its A outputs
with the existing independent reference-pass oracle and then uses normal
whole-target admission. The historical D10.2 structural-role/projector queries
remain `indeterminate_requires_review`; their provenance is not a license to
identify K4 with the core structural K or to assert a full-state orthogonal
projector without its metric. The five retained typed queries preserve source
references, edge relations, support dispositions and trace digests.

## 4. Structural CAN-B disposition

Draft 8's structural proposal concerns a **reduced constitutive functional** on
a declared smooth chart and charge tangent, not any convenient matrix called
“Hessian.” A complete pullback includes the induced geometry response and its
second derivative where required. A field-contribution term is not the whole
functional. In A's C variation W is fixed; in C the constitutive selector is
not frozen merely for convenience. A critical branch and simple selected mode
must precede an equivariant half-edge lift and a certified uniqueness margin.

The existing C derivative contract is accepted only on its smooth fixed-rank
SPD-selector stratum. RG's section remains Lipschitz-only for this purpose;
the typed regularity debt is narrowed, not resolved. Neither fact supplies the
missing universal functional/lift. No classical RG structural spectrum is
computed. Conversely, that debt does **not** prohibit derivative-free A/C
policies merely because they use an admitted RG present-state read.

This proposal does not reject structural splitting scientifically. It holds
its endogenous law and records a tested graph/resource/reference builder
conditional on a complete supplied partition, including loops and parallel
edges. No native triggered split or structural instability result is claimed.
