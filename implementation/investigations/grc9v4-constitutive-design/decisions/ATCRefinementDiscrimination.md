## 3. A local conservative split is not automatically neutral refinement

The presently implemented resource chart has

$$
Q=\sum_v C_v.
$$

Suppose all n source coordinates equal c > 0. Replace one coordinate with two
children, leave the other n-1 coordinates unchanged, and preserve Q. Then

$$
C_{v_0}+C_{v_1}=c.
$$

Both children cannot retain value c. In particular, an equal split gives c/2
at each child, while unchanged neighbors still have value c. More generally,
one cannot simultaneously preserve charge, retain a positive uniform value,
and increase the number of unit-measure coordinates. This arithmetic statement
does not depend on the candidate or realization. Native execution below is
only an A_OS control, not all-ten implementation evidence.

More generally, a linear map from n to m unit-measure coordinates satisfying
both charge conservation and preservation of the same constant field would obey

$$
\mathbf1_m^\top T=\mathbf1_n^\top,\qquad T\mathbf1_n=\mathbf1_m,
\qquad m=\mathbf1_m^\top T\mathbf1_n=n.
$$

It therefore cannot increase dimension while retaining both properties.

This is not a defect in conservative allocation. It means that the operation
changes the constitutive resource configuration; it is not automatically a
neutral subdivision of the same continuum field.

## 4. Symmetry also constrains the missing lift

For d indistinguishable incident ports, enumerate complete nontrivial unordered
binary partitions. Under the full port-permutation action:

| d | Unordered partitions | Partitions fixed by every permutation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 1 | 1 |
| 3 | 3 | 0 |
| 4 | 7 | 0 |

Swapping child labels is already quotiented out. Thus a fully symmetric
three-arm source cannot equivariantly select one binary partition while keeping
that symmetry. A genuine physical asymmetry could resolve it; IDs, enumeration
order and an arbitrary eigenbasis cannot. This is exact abstract combinatorics,
not a claim that a particular native A/C source realizes the full symmetry of
all its reference, backend, W/Z and context operands.

## 5. Consequence for the next scientific design

The refinement front now needs an explicit meaning, not another positive scalar:

- **Fixed-unit-measure graph growth/fission:** accept that adding a resource
  coordinate changes the physical resource configuration. Justify its source
  guard, unordered half-edge partition, allocation and future behavior as a
  new constitutive operation. Test constructor-induced contrast separately
  from source-developed organization. This stays closest to the current
  accepted state/charge contract and is the working direction to investigate.
- **Representation-neutral field refinement:** if preservation of a uniform
  underlying density is intended, a measure/density distinction is needed.
  For example, splitting a reference measure while preserving density can
  conserve its weighted charge. That would require a separately justified V4
  measure, constitutive and lifecycle extension across the affected products;
  it is not an allocator correction or an already authorized implementation.

These are different scientific assertions. Neither is accepted by this note.
The first direction does not rule out a later measure-aware extension; the
second is not introduced merely to make this control look neutral.

For a *declared* lift T and aggregation P with PT = I, useful future diagnostics
are the resource-sector quantities

$$
d_{\mathrm{coarse}}=P f_{\mathrm{target}}-f_{\mathrm{source}},\qquad
d_{\mathrm{fiber}}=(I-TP)f_{\mathrm{target}}.
$$

The target histories/reference must be stated, and the lift may be frozen only
at the tested source. These are diagnostic residuals, not an exact quotient,
an optimization objective, or permission to choose a target by trial search.
The symmetric split above has a nonzero coarse residual and zero child-fiber
residual. It is a concrete negative control for mistaking event-generated flow
for differentiated child continuation.

For this fixed lift/aggregation the decomposition is exactly

$$
f_{\mathrm{target}}-T f_{\mathrm{source}}
=T d_{\mathrm{coarse}}+d_{\mathrm{fiber}}.
$$

Zero fiber does not imply a neutral event; nonzero fiber does not prove a stable
child identity. The [modal split follow-up](./ATCModalSplitDiscrimination.md)
tests the review's more discriminating prediction with this fixed construction,
without selecting a target using its future response.

Next derive a source-side distinction and covariant partition for the fixed-
measure growth interpretation, then test whether the selected construction
expresses that distinction beyond the matched controls. Keep the qualified
structural-functional route open: temporal growth does not discharge its
functional, tangent, branch or mode-to-partition debts. No post-event effect
may serve as circular evidence for its own source guard.
