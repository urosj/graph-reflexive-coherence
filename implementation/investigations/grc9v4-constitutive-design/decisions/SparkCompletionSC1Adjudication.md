## 1. Selected scientific meaning

Select **grc9v4-certified-geometric-child-basins-v1** as one sufficient,
bounded child-organization policy for this investigation. A child identity is
a qualified geometric anchor with a nontrivial guaranteed all-route descent
domain in the signed-resource landscape. Uniform preservation of these spatial
domains during full-state dynamical return supplies the stability requirement.

This selection deliberately permits persistent geometric organization at zero
physical current. The routing landscape is a diagnostic object; its paths need
not coincide with constitutive flux. The existing physical-flow basin classifier
continues to mean exactly what its own contract says. Several spatial basins can
coexist within one attracting full-field pattern; they are not thereby several
alternative full-state attractors or independent causal subsystems.

This is an explicit scientific choice, not a theorem forcing this interpretation
from the old A.7 wording. It chooses a bounded sufficient interpretation of
stable child organization. It does not make the policy universal or a default
for existing profiles. Moving, oscillatory, plateau and noncontractive
organizations fall outside this certificate, without being declared impossible.

The complete selected definition is SC-1 §§2–5, with no weakened premises:

- One globally declared sign, fixed-row convention and predeclared positive
  thresholds; exact scientific rows with certified represented agreement for
  native observations. All strict descents count, ties create no edge, ambiguous
  destinations remain unassigned, and no external tie-break chooses a child.
- A qualified anchor has strict seed, positive-resource and every-neighbor
  barrier margins, with at least two vertices in its guaranteed domain.
- The same classifier identifies a unique pre-event parent basin containing
  the expanded vertex. Missing parent means inapplicable, not a zero count.
- Actual event lineage lifts the parent footprint. Entire descendant basins
  lie inside it; at least two qualify and at least one has a new-module anchor.
  Gain is local relative to that parent, not global basin growth or a
  counterfactual proof that the event caused organization.
- The complete exact ordinary map, on every independent C/W/Z coordinate
  present in the realization, has a full-dimensional relative trapping and
  contraction domain with uniformly preserved spatial predicates. Neither an
  inner solver contraction nor a finite observation window replaces this proof.
- At least one positive admitted post-event ordinary step reaches that domain.
  Numerical observations require the full-step, constraint and diagnostic
  certificates of SC-1 §5, plus the declared positive observation window.
  Both-role admission, rollback, reset/window invalidation and at-most-one gain
  per event lineage remain required. Read, replay and duplication earn no birth.

The policy is parameterized; the toy's thresholds, charge, graph and affine map
are not selected as a production binding. No new resource law, current law,
