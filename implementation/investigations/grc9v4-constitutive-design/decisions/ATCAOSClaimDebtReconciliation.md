## Meaning of the three local states

- **Closed:** the obligation activated by the precise accepted A_OS research
  claim is met, subject to that claim's domain and authority ceiling.
- **Partial:** earned scope coexists with an identified broader or downstream
  obligation. This does not reopen the accepted G1–G7 program.
- **Not activated:** this bounded law makes no claim activating that obligation.
  It is not a negative scientific result or permission to omit the capability
  from the intended wider ATC program.

## Exact support and preserved limits

accepted-scope pointer, predecessor claim IDs and proposed origin IDs. Each
debt carries its original activation/closure requirement and reciprocal
accepted-claim references. Earlier preparation, unsuccessful alternatives
and counterexamples remain in the hash-bound evidence inventory; a stored
experiment is not automatically an accepted claim.

[G1–G4 §§2–5](ATCSectorStateDomain.md), the active-channel/potential/request
extension in [G5–G6 §§2–5](ATCSectorChannelsAndRequests.md), and both-role
transfer, lifecycle and finite numerics in
[G7 §§2–5](ATCSectorReferenceConformance.md).
The [sixth adjudication](ATCSectorReferenceAdjudication.md) controls G7's
research-versus-native boundary and precise independent-review attribution.

DB-11's previous transfer/replay links did not establish batch/sequential
equivalence. DB-22's ordinary request and replay evidence did not establish
repeated-event or non-Zeno behavior. These are explicitly not activated here;
their original obligations remain available to future claims. Resource
contraction is not joint-state contraction; exact-real conservation is not
exact binary64 conservation. No G7-N research gate is reintroduced.
