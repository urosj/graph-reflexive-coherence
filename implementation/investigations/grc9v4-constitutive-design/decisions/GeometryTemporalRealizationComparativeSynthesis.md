## Lawful Comparisons

### CI And OS

Within the same candidate, CI and OS use the same committed state. Comparison
adds an **analysis lift** to common derived `(J,h)` coordinates on a shared local
chart; it does not make `J` or `h` committed state. The lawful comparison then
inserts the OS final pair into the CI residual.

That comparison already establishes:

```text
F_CI(J_OS, h_OS) = (0, r_h_OS)
```

and a conditional local quadratic bound in the typed profile coupling. It does
not establish a numeric uniform defect envelope or superiority of either
realization.

### CI/OS And RG-2b

RG-2b supplies a local analysis lift:

```text
X -> (X, Gamma_a(X))
```

on a frozen family-local completion. This is not an expanded committed-state
identity. Comparison is lawful only on that admitted section. RG's
fixed-extension-relative uniqueness, Lipschitz ceiling, and local domain remain
part of the comparison; they cannot be erased to call RG globally equivalent to
CI or OS.

### PC And Nonpersistent Rows

PC projects to the base candidate state through:

```text
pi_X : (X, Z_4) -> X
```

This projection is many-to-one and discards independent structural history. A
reverse embedding needs a declared `Z_4` initialization rule and is not
canonical. PC and nonpersistent rows can therefore be compared for capability,
state cost, and lifecycle cost, but not declared transition-equivalent through
that projection.

### Candidate A And Candidate C

A and C do not have a lawful common state isomorphism. A owns `W_A` and its
writer; C derives `T_C` through a selector/Hodge chain. Their dimensions,
domains, metrics, and lifecycle obligations differ.

The lawful cross-candidate comparison is limited to:

```text

candidate-local debt
```

Raw spectra, margins, state counts, or proxy scores cannot rank A against C
without an explicit cross-candidate model.

## PC Minimal Carrier Audit

The accepted enabled profile is affine:

```text
H1_form+ = H1_form_ref + kappa_H Z_4
```

on the candidate-specific `K_4` carrier space. Therefore:

```text
D_K H_profile = kappa_H I
```

For the positive enabled profile, `kappa_H != 0`, so:

```text
kernel(D_K H_profile) = {0}
K_4 / kernel(D_K H_profile) = K_4
```

There is therefore no nontrivial exact profile-kernel quotient for the **current
enabled affine profile**. Full `K_4` is irreducible by this audit. At
`kappa_H = 0`, the quotient is trivial, but the structural geometry channel is
disabled and no positive PC row remains.

This is not yet a proof that full `K_4` is the absolutely minimal reachable-state
representation. Such a proof would also need to exclude a lower-dimensional
reachable source/carrier subset, an exact writer-invariant subspace, runtime
equivalences finer than profile equality, and a nonlinear lossless coordinate
chart on the reachable carrier manifold. If the entire `K_4` ball is admitted as
independently settable lawful state and every distinct affine profile state must
remain distinguishable, then full `K_4` is necessary under that stronger
premise.

A future noninjective, nonlinear, projected, or reachable-subset profile must
repeat three parts of the audit:

1. compute its exact profile equivalence classes;
2. prove that every runtime consumer and the source writer are well defined on
   the quotient;
3. distinguish profile-kernel irreducibility from absolute reachable-state
   minimality.
