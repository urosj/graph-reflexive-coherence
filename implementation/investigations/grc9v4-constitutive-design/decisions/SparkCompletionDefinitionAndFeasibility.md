## 2. First feasibility result: seed thresholds can exclude every state

In the baseline fixed-row chart, the signed Hessian is

$$
\widetilde H_i=\operatorname{diag}(s_H g_i[1],s_H g_i[2],s_H g_i[3]),
\qquad s_H\in\{-1,+1\}.
$$

The existing seed test requires

$$
\sum_{r=1}^{3}g_i[r]^2<\varepsilon_g^2,
\qquad s_Hg_i[r]>\varepsilon_H>0\quad\text{for every }r.
$$

Consequently, a necessary condition is

$$
\boxed{\varepsilon_g^2>3\varepsilon_H^2.}
$$

Each squared component strictly exceeds $\varepsilon_H^2$, proving necessity.
For unrestricted **real row vectors**, this is also sufficient: choose
$a\in(\varepsilon_H,\varepsilon_g/\sqrt 3)$ and set every component to $s_Ha$.
This proves an algebraic nonempty set, not reachability by a graph/resource
state, an admitted selected root, or a binary64 witness arbitrarily close to
the strict boundary. The native predicate compares exact squares of represented
operands, so its necessary inequality holds without a square-root rounding
argument.

All ten frozen P9-10.4 expansion fixtures have
$\varepsilon_g=\varepsilon_H=1/2$. Their threshold slack is
$\varepsilon_g^2-3\varepsilon_H^2=-1/2$. **No trajectory under these unchanged
thresholds can acquire a geometric seed.** A longer run cannot repair that
exclusion. This applies to any completion proposal that requires these seeds;
it does not exclude a separately justified flow-only or other organization
criterion, and it does not invalidate mechanical expansion conformance.

The accepted separate C_OS seed example explicitly changes its specialization
identity and uses $\varepsilon_g=0.5$, $\varepsilon_H=0.01$. Its center has three
negative gradient coordinates of approximately $-0.02305603$ with $s_H=-1$ and
passes the seed test. This is a nonempty **local diagnostic** control. It has
zero topology events and supplies neither child gain nor persistence. Its
resources and threshold cannot silently replace the frozen expansion inputs.

## 3. Second feasibility constraint: available rows

The fixed-row differential is a weighted neighbor-minus-local resource mean:

$$
g_i[r]=\frac{\sum_{e\in\mathcal R_i(r)}w_e(C_{j(e)}-C_i)}
                  {\sum_{e\in\mathcal R_i(r)}w_e},
$$

with zero for a zero denominator. An unoccupied row therefore forces a zero
signed diagonal and rules out a seed for positive $\varepsilon_H$. Positive
total weight in each of the three rows is necessary, not sufficient.
Candidate A takes the applicable W history; Candidate C uses its declared
transport weights. The same algebraic exclusion applies to either, independently
of their distinct current solvers and carrier histories.

In each retained 17-node D52 example, thirteen target nodes lack a row. Only
the core and three satellites occupy all three rows. Those four are structurally
eligible for testing; this is not a claim that any becomes a seed. The retained
first target continuation contains zero seeds, consistent with both exclusions.
This observation is about these layouts, not all expansion degrees or graphs.

The two constraints make blind reuse of expansion fixtures inappropriate for
a seed-based completion campaign. A new policy/fixture must be justified and
identified explicitly before execution; changing thresholds after observing a
desired outcome would not establish the intended result.

## 4. What a stable child must mean

The [baseline basin interface](../../../../specs/grc-9-v4-basin-interface.md)
supplies a snapshot of represented directed flow. Its regions are forward-closed
in that frozen graph, not under subsequent GRCV4 evolution. Exactly divergence-free
closed represented currents have no assigned sink regions. Thus a sink-region
criterion alone cannot be a general detector of every equilibrium or attractor.
Conversely, a geometric seed need not be a flow sink. Neither diagnostic should
be silently substituted for the other.

Three distinctions must survive the definition:

| Question | Required substance | Insufficient substitute |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Child organization | A declared object, domain and nonzero distinction from the transported parent, using the actual event lineage | A newly allocated node or a new graph-dependent hash |
| Persistence | Correspondence of the defined object through positive committed evolution, with a declared window and elapsed time | Repeated equal basin IDs or repeated reads of one state |
| Dynamical stability | A separately justified invariant/return/attraction statement on a specified state domain, if claimed | A finite run or small current-only residual |

Finite-window evidence can be scientifically useful, but must be named as such.
For example, the elementary sequence $z_k=2^{-k}$ stays strictly above
$2^{-N-1}$ for every $k=0,\ldots,N$, then ceases to do so. Persistence over a
finite window cannot by itself exclude eventual loss. This logical example is
not a GRCV4 counterexample. The investigation must decide whether a bounded
observational completion contract satisfies the intended meaning or whether a
return/invariance certificate is required. Neither choice is made by this note.

## 5. Proposed definition work, with a finite finish line

**SC-1 — specify one candidate and its conditional argument.** Compare the two
existing observables as inputs: flow regions alone, and regions with separately
tracked geometric support. Do not require them to coincide without proof.
Choose and state the child object, the parent comparison and the precise level
of stability. Fix the residual, units, thresholds and sampling duration before
selecting numerical outcomes. A conditional theorem must explain why those
predicates imply exactly the claimed completion property.

The candidate must answer these concrete cases:

- Use the pre-event parent's full organization and the expansion lineage to
  distinguish additional children from transported/relabelled parent structure.
  Do not assume a spark candidate was already a geometric seed or assigned sink.
  If a required parent organization is absent, report inapplicability rather
  than treating its count as zero to manufacture gain.
- Match objects through actual node/edge lineage and their stated features.
  Sink-ID equality is insufficient; membership can change while that ID stays
  equal. Changed graph hashes and split/merged regions cannot count as births by
  themselves. Ambiguous matches must not use a label-based tie-break.
- Declare what survives across positive ordinary steps and what invalidates or
  restarts the window: rejected operations, zero-duration requests, topology or
  policy changes, disabled execution, reset and rebase. Replay reproduces evidence
  rather than earning another beat. Track actual elapsed time as well as count.
- Use fresh selected reads of the full C/W/Z state and context. CI evidence binds
  the joint selected root, PC includes the carrier and its read/write stage, and
  RG2b preserves its section/certificate limits. Current and reset are independent
  histories; reset evidence cannot extend a live timeline. If both-role admission
  is required, prove it separately from live completion.
- State represented-value versus exact-state claims and explicit unresolved
  numerical margins. Exact equality or an undecidable sign cannot be silently
  turned into success. Do not redefine abundance by counting these objects.

**SC-2 — establish a nonempty native-compatible domain.** Start with a small
C_OS graph because no W/Z history is needed to isolate the definition. First
derive the threshold, row-occupancy, parent/child and conservative-transfer
constraints; use accepted mechanical expansion, not a new partition law.
Only then choose a separately identified source/policy and execute the bounded
positive evolution needed by the selected criterion. Include unchanged-parent,
transient and relabelling controls. If the candidate has an empty or unresolved
domain, record that result and revise the scientific choice; do not widen
tolerances until a desired count appears.

One C_OS witness will not establish all-ten completion. Before propagation,
identify exactly which arguments are realization-independent and which require
W/Z, joint-root or section-specific evidence. Reuse accepted Tranches 8–10
mechanics and readmission evidence rather than repeating them wholesale.

**SC-3 — review and scoped adjudication.** Admit only the reviewed claim and
resolve only its matching debts, then propagate authority → proposal/paper →
specification. Native completion, receipts, hierarchy and window lifecycle land
in P9-9.1a afterward. P9-10.5 records the debt dispositions and P9-10.6/G4 the
resulting support boundary; those closeout tasks do not substitute for SC-1/2.
