# D1 Retained-Representation Ontology And Candidate Admission

**Record:** `GRC9V4-CD-D1-v1`  
**Status:** accepted bounded  
**Decision digest:** `f8ae101beb9fa6e9827555eba64452087c1d19b6933325966f10c4c4ec64c507`

## Purpose

D1 asks which retained-representation families are coherent enough to continue
through the constitutive design investigation. It does not select GRC9V4 and it
does not prove any transition, formation law, Read-Back operator, or runtime
implementation.

The comparison keeps three questions independent:

```text
What has causal state authority?
What, if anything, carries or projects a resource?
How does the representation participate in transport?
```

This separation matters. An independently serialized coordinate need not carry
coherence resource. A derived sector may project existing coherence without
being independently stored. A transport parameter does not automatically own
geometry, resource, or historical state.

```text
sole_surviving_candidate = none

current_candidate_set_exhausted = false
current_temporalization = deferred_to_D6
```

`V4-D` was a source-bounded admission slot, not a fourth object already supplied
by theory. The frozen sources did not yield a materially distinct causal
representation. Every concrete form either temporalizes `W`, introduces an
independent serialized carrier, remains a constitutive sector of `C`, or is
analysis-only. Leaving D admitted as an abstract label would hide the absence
of state, resource, transport, formation, and lifecycle semantics. Inventing a
hybrid after comparison would violate the D0 admission deadline.

The rejection of D is not an exhaustion result and does not reject GRC9V4.

D1 uses three candidate-local dispositions:

```text
admitted
  ontology is coherent enough to proceed to formation/write dynamics

rejected_on_ontology
  hidden state, absent causal authority, resource duplication, observer
  dependence, or impossible reduction cannot be repaired without changing the
  architecture

reclassified_or_revision_needed
  the repair changes the causal state or ownership enough to leave the frozen
  D0 family
```

Failure to already possess a D2 write law, D3 structural operator, D5 Read-Back
operator, D6 current solve, or D7-D9 transition/reduction proof is not a D1
rejection. Those absences are named later-gate obligations.

## Candidate A: Temporalized W

Candidate A changes `W` from a reconstructed stage surface into an
independently persisted cross-beat state when the V4 feature is enabled.

```text
state authority       = independent_state
resource accounting   = nonresource_structural_information
provisional transport roles = conditions_mobility, direct_transport_parameter
geometry authority    = unresolved until D4
```

This is closest to existing GRC9V3 transport mechanics because `W` already
participates in current formation. That does not make A the preferred or
smallest correct extension. Enabled V4 semantics would materially change who
owns `W`: legacy `step()` reconstructs differential and transport surfaces,
whereas A requires persisted `W` to remain causally authoritative.

D1 therefore separates two contributions before asking D4 to compose them:

```text
W_instantaneous = legacy GRC9V3 reconstruction from current C/gradient/J^2
W_retained      = enabled cross-beat causal state

possible later compositions:
  W_effective = W_instantaneous + W_retained
  W_effective = declared nonlinear composition
  W_effective = replacement with explicit loss of the legacy law
```

D1 does not choose among them. If instantaneous and retained authority cannot be
typed without adding another causal object, A requires a split/hybrid
reclassification. It also does not count persistence by numerical inertia as
retained structural meaning. D2-D3 must show what prior activity `W_retained`
can carry and why it is more than an old coefficient that was not overwritten.

A survives D1 only under these conditions:

- every history-bearing `W` value is serialized;
- one declared stage owns its enabled update;
- no cache or analysis projector carries additional unsaved history;
- `W` is not silently equated with metric geometry;
- the disabled profile reconstructs `W` exactly as GRC9V3 does and accumulates
  no hidden V4 history.

The elimination test is causal, not representational. If enabled `W` can always
be reconstructed from current declared `C`, `J_C`, geometry, and lifecycle
state, A collapses to a reconstructed view and fails. A later discriminator
must show that admissible distinct `W` histories can change the future under an
otherwise matched declared state.

`W` has fixed dimension only within one declared topology. Its value domain
must be bounded or coercively controlled, and topology events must own release,
transport, or reconfiguration. An append-only or unbounded historical reservoir
is not admitted.

## Candidate B: Independent Derived Carrier

Candidate B introduces a separate retained coordinate `T`. Its formation and
update must be derived from permitted RC variables, but after formation it has
independent serialized causal authority.

The distinction is:

```text
admissible independent update:
  T[k+1] = U(T[k], C[k], J[k], declared current state)

normally reconstructed rather than independent:
  T[k] = f(C[k], J[k])
```

Calling `U` RC-derived does not make the present value of `T` an instantaneous
functional of current RC variables.

```text
state authority       = independent_state
resource accounting   = nonresource_structural_information
provisional transport roles = conditions_geometry
W relation            = separate transport surface
```

B gives the cleanest separation of retention, geometry, mobility, and current
authority. It is not inherited as a new primitive by the coherence-only core.
It is an underdetermined revision-level constitutive completion, and D2-D4 must
justify its update law, nonresource accounting, and map into geometry or
mobility.

Its elimination test requires matched reduced states with distinct admissible
`T` values to produce distinct futures. If `T` is deterministic from current
declared state, it must be reclassified as Candidate C or as a reconstructed
view. If it is reconstructable only from finite history, D2 must decide whether
that history is already part of the declared complete state. If so, `T` is
redundant; if not, `T` may be a legitimate Markov completion, but its history
contract cannot remain a hidden cache.

The disabled reduction construction is:

```text
X_V4_B = X_V3 x T
i_B(x) = (x, T_neutral)
pi_B(x, T_neutral) = x
```

`T_neutral` must remain canonical, inert, budget-neutral, RNG-neutral, and free
of event or hidden-history effects. D1 constructs this state-space relation; it
does not prove transition commutation.

The parity embedding uses one canonical constant neutral state. A
state-dependent equilibrium `T*(x)` is not silently substituted because it
could encode V3 state and alter later re-enabling. A different migration or
embedding profile requires an explicit successor contract.

## Candidate C: Constitutive C Sector

Candidate C keeps coherence primary. A historically formed, currently
addressable sector of `C` is derived from declared current state and is consumed
by the runtime as a constitutive input.

```text
state authority       = derived_constitutive_representation
resource accounting   = projects_existing_resource
provisional transport roles = conditions_geometry
independent storage   = no
```

This is closest to the inherited coherence-only state vocabulary. That is a
property, not an architecture ranking.

The admissible D1 selector is a deterministic current-state construction such
as:

```text
C_M^(Delta) = P_M^(Delta)[C, geometry, cutoff] C
```

The cutoff or sector rule is preregistered and constitutive. The selector may
not use later persistence, future trajectory labels, an analysis-only slow
cluster, or unsaved history. If selector evolution depends on its own prior
state, that state must be serialized and the architecture reclassifies toward
Candidate B.

D1 admits variable-rank sectors in principle, but does not treat rank changes as
invisible. The later contract must cover:

```text
P_M(C[k]) != P_M(C[k+1])
rank changes
mode crossings
symmetry exchange
branch transitions
topology changes
```

A rank change is initially a changed decomposition of the same `C` resource,
not automatic creation or destruction of resource. D2/D4 must account for
content entering or leaving the sector and transport its basis/projector. D3
and D8 may not assume a fixed tangent dimension that D1 never guaranteed.

Candidate C is reconstructable as a stored view, but that does not remove its
possible constitutive authority. The correct elimination distinction is:

```text
stored derived coordinate may be eliminated
runtime read of the exact selected sector may not be eliminated
```

If bypassing or replacing the selected sector never changes the transition,
the sector is only a diagnostic and Candidate C fails. D2 and D7 must also
separate historical sector formation, ordinary `C` continuity, and later
retained-conditioned read effect. Generic persistence of `C` is not enough.

## Candidate D: Closed Admission Slot

The D0 slot prevented A/B/C from becoming a forced exhaustive ontology before
the frozen sources were inspected. Its search function is now complete.

```text
state authority       = observer_only
resource accounting   = no_resource_role
provisional transport roles = none
complete-step authority = none
```

These values describe the empty slot, not a fourth retained carrier. No
candidate-specific state space, embedding, projection, or lifecycle exists to
admit. A later source or transparent distinct derivation can reopen candidate
admission only through a D0 successor and a repeated D1 comparison.

## Cross-Candidate Pressure Audit

### Elimination and causal authority

D1 distinguishes five cases:

```text
algebraically_reconstructable_now
reconstructable_only_with_declared_history
reconstructable_only_with_hidden_state
independently_variable_under_constraints
observationally_redundant_but_not_algebraically_redundant
```

A and B are admitted only as independently variable candidates. Serialization
alone is insufficient. C is algebraically reconstructable now, but its exact
constitutive runtime read must be load-bearing; otherwise it is only a view or
observer. Non-eliminability proves only that a representation is load-bearing
inside its own candidate architecture. It does not establish universal
necessity.

### Candidate equivalence

A, B, and C currently occupy separate provisional equivalence classes. No
causal coordinate transform has been established. Two candidates are equivalent
only if a bijection preserves transition, outputs, resource accounting,
transport authority, and lifecycle. Equal dimensions or two edge scalars are
not enough.

Important reclassification cases remain explicit:

```text
C + persistent selector identity state -> B-like independent state
A + separately authoritative helper/selector -> split or hybrid candidate
materially new state/accounting/transport contract -> D0 successor
```

### Hidden authority and administration

The hidden-state audit includes producer and helper state, not only public
fields. Accumulators, optimizers, running covariance, previous-current caches,
adaptive bases, schedules, and mutable registries are causal state if they
change future behavior and cannot be reconstructed from declared state.

RNG, scheduler phase, event index, and registry dependence are not forbidden by
name, but any causal dependence must be declared and serialized under the
complete-state and reduction contracts. Ignoring an RNG stream is not causal
closure.

### Resource and information capacity

Candidate C projects the existing `C` resource:

```text
T_M = P_M C
```

It is counted once. A serialized copy would duplicate representation, not
physical resource. A and B carry nonresource structural information in their
current candidate forms. They may not become unbounded unreleased history
reservoirs. A new independent conserved or quasi-conserved resource would
require theory-level conservation work and a reopened candidate contract.

### Historical and causal sanity checks

Each survivor states two separate reasons:

| Candidate | What may be historical at `k`? | What native calculation may consume it? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | persisted `W_retained` update history, not current legacy reconstruction | effective transport/current calculation |
| B | recursive `T[k-1] -> T[k]` dependence after forming inputs end | constitutive geometry map before transport |
| C | prior activity still present in the selected part of current `C` | exact sector-conditioned geometry or Read-Back calculation |

These are ontology rationales, not retention or Read-Back proofs.

The structured record contains 28 pressure rows. Every row is either satisfied
in D1 or deferred under a named later-gate obligation extending through D8
where variable-rank perturbation handling requires it; none is unclassified.

The transport roles above are provisional candidate signatures, not early D4
decisions. D4 may confirm, narrow, or reclassify them while preserving the
candidate's state and accounting ontology. A material state, accounting, or
causal-architecture change requires a successor record.

## Disabled GRC9V3 Reduction

D1 constructs state-space compatibility for each admitted family:

| Candidate | Disabled embedding | Projection | Remaining proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | copy V3 state; disable temporal `W` authority | remove V4 policy metadata | legacy `W` reconstruction remains invariant and no hidden history accumulates |
| B | copy V3 state; set `T = T_neutral` | drop neutral `T` | neutral state remains inert across transition and lifecycle |
| C | identity on V3 state; disable constitutive sector read/write | erase only recomputable view | diagnostic recomputation has no causal side effect |

The required relation remains unproved:

```text
pi_a(F_V4_a_disabled(i_a(x))) = F_V3(x)
```

D7 must close the candidate transition. D9 must execute the reduction witness,
including repeated-step invariance and snapshot, observable, event, topology,
clock, RNG, and reset-baseline parity.

## Topology Boundary

The fixed-topology language in D1 names the local verification envelope only.
The intended V4 successor remains topology-capable.

```text
fixed-topology smooth stratum
  -> enough to state local causal ownership and reduction maps

topology event
  -> requires explicit interspace transport, reconfiguration, release, or
     termination plus resource and lifecycle accounting
```

Lineage or stable node identifiers are not retained-state transport. Every
admitted candidate carries this hard D4/D9 debt into the full runtime scope.

## Comparative Interpretation

The three survivors expose different design advantages:

| Candidate | Bounded property | Unresolved risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | closest to existing transport mechanics | conflates retained structure, mobility, and possibly geometry |
| B | cleanest separation of causal authorities | adds independent constitutive state and requires theory/budget justification |
| C | closest to coherence-only state vocabulary | can collapse into a tautological or observer-only relabel |

These are not scores. D2-D9 provide the load-bearing discriminators: formation,
retention, release, structural domain, geometry/mobility ownership, directional
Read-Back, current closure, transition closure, perturbative consistency, and
legacy reduction.

## Claim Ceiling

D1 supports only:

> Three bounded, causally coherent retained-representation candidate families,
> with explicit state, resource, transport, selector, hidden-state, and
> disabled-reduction obligations.

It does not support a final architecture, a runtime implementation, a normative
GRC9V4 specification, topology-changing retention, a completed V3 reduction
witness, Read-Back, or a decision to temporalize current.

## Open Debt

1. A must separate enabled persistent-`W` authority from disabled legacy
   reconstructed-`W` semantics.
2. B must justify its independent constitutive state, update law, and
   nonresource accounting.
3. C must freeze a constitutive selector and separate historical sector
   formation from ordinary `C` persistence.
4. Every candidate must close its disabled transition and lifecycle witness.
5. Every candidate must define topology-event transport, reconfiguration,
   release, or termination before D10.
6. The D slot remains closed unless a D0 successor admits a materially distinct
   source-backed ontology.
7. Every candidate must bound information capacity and define pruning, release,
   or rank-change accounting without duplicating resource.
8. Producer/helper, RNG, scheduler, event, and registry dependencies must enter
   the complete causal-state and reduction contracts or be reconstructable.
9. D7/D10 must revisit pairwise causal equivalence before treating A/B/C as
   substantively different selection alternatives.
