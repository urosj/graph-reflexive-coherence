## 3. G2: obstruction on a cone, not the old guard by itself

b_u+b_v=[(5/2)S^2+D^2/2-aS]x+D(3S-a)y.

$$
b_u+b_v\ge(12/125)x-(9-x)/100\ge(19/250)x.

Take r=1/783 and B0=11/2. Both reference structural components have
absolute value <=rB0. Since |d_u|,|d_v|<=9, the exact normalized-star formulas
give the per-contact geometry correction bound

$$
\delta=(225/2)(rB_0)^2=3025/544968.
$$

Hence each fresh current differs from its bare value by at most
epsilon=delta+r(B0+delta). With x>=3,

$$
x^+\ge\{1+(5/16)[19/250-2\epsilon/3]\}x
> (51/50)x.
$$

This is an actual full-feedback growth theorem. Conductance targets exceed

## 4. G3: an exact structural sector definition

Use the finite graph with its **complete physical decorations**: resource and
history fields, reference measures, geometry/context and constitutive data.
For a distinguished candidate parent, let Gamma be the automorphism group
fixing that parent and all those decorations. Define its incident sectors as
Gamma's edge orbits, with current components compared in the common inward
orientation at the parent. This uses physical structure, not stable-ID order.

For vertex permutation P and signed edge permutation Q, incidence transforms
as PB=BQ. Componentwise conductance evaluation commutes with permutation;
currents and structural forms transform by Q. Shared-vertex multiplicities
are preserved, so S(Qf)=Q S(f) Q^T. H inversion transforms by conjugacy, as do
the generated geometry and fresh read. Continuity and the componentwise writer
commute with these actions. Consequently the full staged map preserves the
fixed subspace of Gamma. This proves exact full-response equality on each
orbit and a well-defined invariant quotient, including orientation covariance.

The original two leaf swaps are the smallest example. The embedded case below
also swaps the corresponding external vertices; local equality without those
environmental decorations would be insufficient. Extra symmetries may appear
later; the theorem does not guarantee that distinct orbits never merge.
G2 intentionally does not depend on persistent strict u<v after the event.

This is a sufficient structural answer, **not** a theorem that every equitable
partition of the bare graph closes the nonlinear constitutive equations.
Pooling unequal currents generally does not commute with exp(-gamma J²/2):
two currents (+j,-j) have zero mean but mean target exp(-gamma j²/2)<1,
whereas applying the target to the mean gives one. Reference histories and
star outer products introduce further information requirements. Unequal
microscopic-state quotienting therefore remains a distinct open question;
no fitted equality tolerance is added to obtain it.
Nor does this structural theorem extend the equal-cardinality two-sector
allocator or sector-to-site recipe to arbitrary orbit sizes.

### Why this does not license arbitrary surroundings

As a contrasting graph, let a second unsplit parent share the four leaves.
After splitting only the first parent, the target vector
(-1,-1,-1,-1,0,0,4) is mean-zero and has Laplacian Rayleigh quotient
26/5>19/4. The unchanged high-degree support leaves an unstable resource
mode. At uniform C and W=1, reference current is zero and the feedback
correction has zero linearization; gamma-writing also has zero resource
derivative. The full feedback box therefore does not remove this linear
instability. This is a counterexample to **return near the uniform target**,
not a proof that every possible trajectory on that graph fails.

The positive class above includes graph relabellings/orientation changes and
the declared continuous state/history/parameter domains. It does not include
arbitrary exterior graphs, arbitrary external signals, extra couplings or an
unproved quasi-static Schur reduction. Operation-compatible support remains
the scientific requirement, not an administrative gate.

## 6. G5–G7 assessment and remaining claims/debts

There is a concrete reason not to claim the remaining stages by continuity.
The [Candidate-A law](../../../../specs/grc-v4-spec.md) gives, at uniform C=c1,
zero current and a constant-annihilating differential descriptor,

$$
W_{eq}=\max(1/2,\exp(-\alpha c)).
$$

For positive alpha this is not one. G5 must replace the history-limit claim
and recheck the resource spectral window around that equilibrium. A genuinely
nonzero beta lift additionally needs an explicit differential-reference /
descriptor binding on the source, environment and target; none was necessary
in the alpha=beta=0 reduction. A broader potential class needs slope/curvature
bounds in the source obstruction and target remainder estimates. None is
silently supplied by this certificate. **G5 remains open**, with a definite
next mathematical problem, not another experiment inside the old box.

**G6 remains open:** changing h at fixed tau_A replaces the square-root writer
by the zero-order-hold exponent exp(-h/tau_A), and changes both stability
polynomials. Merely substituting a new h while keeping W^+=sqrt(W G_W)
would change the law. No time/subdivision equivalence is inferred.

**G7 remains open:** native potential, descriptor, source and event admission
must follow reviewed claim/debt decisions → topology-only proposal → extension
paper → specification. Investigation-local code is permitted; this result
does not authorize changing src or advertising native autonomous splitting.

These results earn prospective bounded portions of DB-07 (predictive
obstruction), DB-08 (source/operation relation), DB-10 (universal share bounds
and exact structural covariance), DB-19 (independent-role whole-graph return),
and DB-26 (a new A_OS state/environment scope). They do not discharge those
debts. DB-20 formation, DB-23 native execution and DB-24 source readmission
remain open, as do all other families, repeated autonomous operation and
aggregate ATC-2. No accepted graph nodes or reviewed documents are rewritten.
