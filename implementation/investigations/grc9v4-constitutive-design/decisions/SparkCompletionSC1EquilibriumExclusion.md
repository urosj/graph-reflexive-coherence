## 2. Tree obstruction

Stationarity of resources gives

$$
C^+=C-hBJ^{\mathrm{use}}=C,\qquad BJ^{\mathrm{use}}=0.
$$

On a connected tree, incidence has full column rank, so $J^{\mathrm{use}}=0$.
The retained A/C read contributions are linear in their driving current at a
fixed read geometry and vanish there. Current closure then gives $J_0=0$.
This concerns the consumed read; a fresh poststate current is not substituted.

For C_OS and C_CI, at that consumed geometry,

$$
J_0=-M B^\top\Phi,\qquad
\Phi=\kappa_\Phi B H_M B^\top C,\qquad M\succ0,\ H_M\succ0,\ \kappa_\Phi>0.
$$

Thus $B^\top\Phi=0$. Connectivity makes $\Phi$ constant, while
$\mathbf1^\top\Phi=0$ makes that constant zero. Consequently

$$
0=C^\top\Phi=\kappa_\Phi(B^\top C)^\top H_M(B^\top C),
\qquad B^\top C=0.
$$

Hence $C$ is constant. C_OS requires no equality between corrector geometry and
fresh restart geometry, and no vanishing source assumption beyond this algebra.

For A_CI and A_PC, zero consumed current gives zero lowered read and zero
quadratic star source. CI's zero-base geometry is reference geometry.
Full PC stationarity additionally implies

$$
Z=aZ+(1-a)S,\qquad a=e^{-h/\tau_{\rm PC}}\in(0,1),
$$

hence $Z=0$. At reference geometry,

$$
J_0=-\eta\operatorname{Diag}(W)B^\top\Phi,\qquad
\Phi=\kappa_c L_W C,\qquad W_e>0,\quad \kappa_c>0.
$$

The same zero-mean/potential argument gives constant $C$. The retained W writer
must equal its zero-current drive at a full fixed point; retained history does
not evade this conclusion.

Constant $C$ has zero row gradient, signed Hessian and neighbor contrast. It
cannot qualify an SC-1 anchor at positive Hessian and contrast thresholds.
An invariant complete contracting domain with a fixed nonempty anchor roster
would contain its unique fixed point and contradict this result.

**Therefore these bindings admit no SC-1 domain with persistent qualified child
basins.** This is an analytic exclusion, not “a witness has not been found.”
It leaves accepted finite-horizon mechanical expansion/continuation intact.

## 3. Candidate A: cycles and local instability

For A, extend the argument to any connected graph only when

$$
J=-D_{\rm eff}B^\top\Phi,\qquad
(D_{\rm eff})_{ee}=\frac{\eta W_e}{1-\zeta_A\chi_A q_e}>0.
$$

Then $BJ=0$ implies
$0=\Phi^\top BJ=-\sum_e(D_{\rm eff})_{ee}(B^\top\Phi)_e^2$, so $J=0$.
The same zero-source/carrier/base argument applies. In the retained A
declarations $|\zeta_A\chi_A|<1$; positive $W,\widehat W$ give $|q_e|<1$,
which suffices for these positive denominators. A larger graph or added cycles
alone therefore does not remove this A_CI/A_PC exclusion.

On a smooth uniform equilibrium branch, the fixed writer gives
$W=w\mathbf1$, $w=\max(W_{\rm floor},e^{-\alpha c})$, and $q_A=0$.
Let $L=BB^\top$ be the unweighted incidence Laplacian. The resource block is

$$
D_C T_h=I+h\eta\kappa_c w^2L^2.
$$

At uniform $C$, derivatives of the potential with respect to W/geometry vanish
because they multiply differences of a constant field. The quadratic star
source has zero first derivative at zero current. Thus the reverse W/Z-to-C
blocks vanish, and these resource-block eigenvalues remain eigenvalues of the
full derivative; C-to-history coupling cannot cancel them. Every nonconstant
Laplacian mode is tangent to fixed charge and has multiplier

$$
1+h\eta\kappa_cw^2\lambda^2>1.
$$

No norm change or smaller positive step yields contraction there. This
derivative claim is restricted to the smooth branch; the uniform-resource
exclusion above does not require differentiability at the W-floor boundary.
No substrate sign change is proposed. The checker includes an exact
two-vertex mode control, not a substitute for this general derivation.
