## 3. Exact carrier boundary is a predicate, not an interval tolerance

The exact input factory checks rational norm squared against R_Z squared
**before** conversion to the outward grid. It checks exact symmetry,
decoration and support as well. The retained carrier keeps the exact
preimage and norm bound separately from numerical enclosures. In particular,
four equal diagonal entries of magnitude R_Z/2 have norm exactly R_Z,
even when their outward enclosure has a norm upper bound greater than R_Z.
Both signs are admitted on both graphs, without PSD assumptions.

Only executing operations can mint derived carrier facts. The ordinary
writer supplies its convex-invariant theorem and selected-root provenance;
the event supplies the lossless isometry and source lineage. Private
construction checks that numerical data do not contradict the bound or
exact preimage. Raw output and serialized receipts cannot mint successors.
This is a mathematical proof interface, not protection from hostile Python
code with access to private constructors or monkeypatches.

Pressure includes exact R_Z, sub-grid offsets just inside/outside, signed
source/target roots and steps, endpoint transfer, malformed decoration and
support, and inherited exact C/W boundaries. Represented-point admission
uses the exact dyadic norm: adjacent binary64 values straddling R_Z must
produce distinct admission outcomes. Nonpoint overlapping boxes cannot
establish represented symmetry or a norm predicate. No epsilon expands R_Z.

The small source S-budget margin is explicitly exercised at the input
carrier boundary. It is not represented as a sampled proof of the maximum:
the exact uniform CIP-0 inequality remains the proof of writer invariance.
