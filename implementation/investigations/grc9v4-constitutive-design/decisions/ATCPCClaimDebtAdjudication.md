## Limits that remain part of the result

- The whole-box transfer/entry/return/asymptotic theorem assumes an admitted
  event state with a resolved source-only share. It does not prove event
  formation for every state or every profile in the box.
- The potential theorem covers the stated common C1 slope envelope; the
  executed independent oracle/reference covers its quadratic subclass.
- The selected law preserves `c_target=c_source`. The five-dimensional event
  image is not the seven-dimensional decorated target carrier ball. Signed Z
  is permitted while H remains SPD; PSD is not a fallback policy.
- The graph domain is the decorated four-edge paired source and double-star
  target. Active external-edge carrier transport requires a future extension;
  it is not an unmet PC-4 acceptance criterion.
- Exact-real charge preservation is not exact stored binary64 conservation.
  The finite C/W/Z publication bridge retains measured charge drift and
  enclosed intermediate calculations. It is not a universal floating-point
  theorem or an all-binary64 internal implementation.
- Ordinary requests may vary in the proved interval at a fixed chosen profile
  and physical tau. Repeated topology events, non-Zeno, general formation,
  other realizations and native production admission are not established.
