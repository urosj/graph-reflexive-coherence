# CAN-LSF — bounded localized response-sector fission

**Status:** proposed scientific choice for review, not accepted authority.

## 1. Scientific recommendation

**Recommend selecting CAN-LSF as a bounded new constitutive fission law:**
when an isolated accumulating parent has two exact persistent response sectors
and its declared ordinary continuation is obstructed, those sectors determine
the two prospective sites. Attempt that one split only when the source locus
is uniquely resolved and its prescribed transfer is funded and constructible;
publish only after independent current/reset target admission.

This is a source-driven **symmetry-sector law**, not generic self-fission,
core spark, a universal restoration operator or completion of classical CAN-B.
It is scientifically defensible as an explicit model extension in this bounded
domain. It is **not compelled by ordinary V4** or an empirical law established
by one designed mathematical example. The recommendation is a decision offered
for scientific review; this document does not record the user's acceptance.

The remaining new physical assertion is:

> Exact persistent response sectors of a locally obstructed shared site may
> acquire separate-site realization, with their incident histories transported
> along the resulting edge lineage.

"May" here identifies the proposed physical response, not an event-time
discretionary callback. Once this law is selected, its resolution and transfer
rules below are deterministic. Failure produces a stated unresolved/blocked
result, not another physical choice.

## 2. Why this is a defensible extension, and what it does not prove

The proposal is constrained by five linked facts, rather than by a desired
target score:

1. **A distinction already exists in the source.** Equal resources and equal
   retained mobilities within each pair persist under the declared ordinary
   dynamics. The two unequal mobilities also determine the distinct contact
   amplitudes of the simple top source mode. This is not an arbitrary grouping
   or a tolerance fitted after target construction.
2. **The distinction is occupied by an obstruction.** Strict inward currents
   and the positive accumulation cone imply finite loss of positive unsplit
   continuation. An unstable spectrum alone does not fire: the homogeneous
   no-current control remains nonfiring.
3. **The surgery acts on the obstructing support.** Splitting the common parent
   changes its concentrated incidence. The stopped cross-edge counterexample
   fails precisely this test and remains a counterexample, not a tuned variant.
4. **The source fixes the operation before its consequences are tested.** The
   exact sectors, unique locus and existing dyadic activity allocator determine
   one target. No target return score, resource reset state or ID breaks ties.
5. **A causal contrast supports the interpretation.** The actual selected
   current and independent reset continue positively after lineage-preserving
   fission; unsplit continuation and reset-without-split fail in the retained
   controls. Artificial history erasure is unnecessary for that result.

These facts justify considering a change in site realization as a constitutive
response. They do not prove that it is the only response possible. Other new
physics could change transport, measure, dissipation or interaction support.
Those are different laws, not unexplored runner-ups inside CAN-LSF. Nor does
"one remaining postulate" mean the whole package has no other selected
conventions: binary surgery, unit site measure, unit bridge, dyadic allocation,
funding benchmark and K0 scheduling are explicit choices, not uniquely derived
physical necessities. The principle is minimal only relative to that declared
package; no global minimality or derivation from the core papers is asserted.

## 4. Activation and deterministic outcome contract

At an eligible parent, let the two distinct W classes be u<v, each on two
contacts, with equal neighbour resources r_u,r_v inside each class. Put

$$
S=u+v,\quad x=s-(r_u+r_v)/2,\quad y=(r_u-r_v)/2.
$$

The source obstruction/decomposition certificate requires

$$
(5/2)S>a,\qquad -B_{ve}J_e>0\ \text{on all four contacts},
\qquad x>0,\ y\ge0.
$$

The reviewed cone recurrence implies geometric growth of x while positivity
holds; component charge Q bounds x<Q if every leaf is positive. This justifies
the word **obstructed** for the declared unsplit dynamics, not a core viability
threshold or classical negative structural-Hessian criterion.

Resolve all certified pressure loci before admission. No certified locus and
no unresolved eligible partition gives `no_event` under this law. An unresolved physical sector decomposition stays
unresolved. Multiple resolved loci give `resolved_per_locus` decomposition and
`multiple_unresolved` locus resolution; neither atomic batching nor sequential
arbitration is authorized. A funded locus cannot outrank an unfunded one.

For one uniquely resolved locus, take the two positive inward block activities
A_u,A_v and select the existing allocation exactly:

$$
k=\operatorname{round}_{\rm even}
\left(2^{16}\frac{A_u}{A_u+A_v}\right),\qquad
(s_u,s_v)=(k/2^{16},1-k/2^{16}).
$$

Funding requires s_u s>r_u and s_v s>r_v. Failed funding is
`blocked_transfer` with pressure and the prescription retained, not `no_event`.
Construction-size admission is separate. Both failures can be present; neither
negates source obstruction. No alternative partition, allocation, chart, step,
history policy or locus is tried. Missing sector equalities are not repaired
by a near-equality tolerance. The [typed status adapter](../scripts/check_atc_sector_status.py)
is the current research description, not a native outcome-schema amendment.
