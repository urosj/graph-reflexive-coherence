## 5. What the current grouping means, and what it does not mean

On this isolated star, closed continuity has generator G=-BJ^(1). For each
degree-one leaf, G_i=-a_selected,i; the parent's rate is sum(a_selected).
Consequently, for any two-block partition P,

$$
\min_{\beta_A,\beta_B}\sum_{D\in P}\sum_{i\in D}(G_i-\beta_D)^2
=\sum_{D\in P}\sum_{i\in D}(a_i-\bar a_D)^2=V(P;a).
$$

The minimizing block rates are their means. The blockwise rate approximation
preserves each block's total leaf rate and, with the parent rate left unchanged,
the star's total resource balance.
This is an observation of the existing continuity generator, **not an extra
resource write or a proposed child resource map**.

So the two observables answer distinct questions:

| Observable being grouped | Exact two-block least-squares meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Leaf resources | Approximate the present inventory with one value per block |
| Selected inward activities | Approximate the present leaf continuity rates with one value per block |

This provides a bounded **instantaneous rate-coherence** interpretation for
current similarity, rather than similarity of inventory.
But the choice to optimize equal-weight two-block rate representation is
itself conditional scientific content. Continuity does not demand that
objective, the number of blocks, this weighting, or a topology change.
The derivation uses degree-one leaves and this declared pairing; it is not a
generic claim about total vertex rates in graphs with other incident edges.

### Instantaneous projection is not dynamical lumpability

For the four leaf rates define

$$
\mathcal S_P=\{g\in\mathbb R^4:\ g_i=\beta_D\text{ for }i\in D,
\ D\in P\}.
$$

The score is exactly the squared distance of the current leaf-rate vector
from this block-constant subspace. It approximates one instantaneous generator
vector; it does not prove that a corresponding block subspace is invariant
under subsequent ordinary evolution. Nor does the uniform parameter-box
theorem prove that an evolving source stays in that box or keeps its partition.

In particular, no autonomous coarse evolution satisfying

$$
\pi T(X)=\widetilde T(\pi X)
$$

has been constructed, where X includes C and retained W, T is complete source
evolution, and pi would be a separately defined coarse-state map. No such map
or closed reduced dynamics follows from the projection identity. It also
does not show that post-fission children evolve as the two block means.
The safe description is instantaneous rate approximation, not an unqualified
claim of dynamical coherence or lumpability.

The topology restriction is equally important: with other edges incident on
a neighbor, its total resource-change rate includes those other currents.
The parent-contact activity would still be a contact-flow observation, but
would not by itself equal that neighbor's full rate. That broader-neighborhood
interpretation requires new analysis.

The example also does **not** establish that current is always the preferable
observable. History-only variance selects P_W here as well. The history
contrast was chosen to dominate the resource contrast; no universal
superiority over history, flux balancing, response operators or other lawful
source invariants follows. The read-off control retains the partition, so

There is no proof that one parent cannot continue, that its grouped contacts
